A luminous cinematic oil painting, impressionistic brushwork, of a four-player card game in
progress, seen from a high three-quarter angle looking down across a dark walnut table. Rich
saturated blues and golds, deep shadowed edges, one warm hanging lamp above the table.

ABSOLUTELY NO FACES AND NO PEOPLE ABOVE THE CHEST. Only hands, wrists and forearms enter the
frame from the four table edges - four pairs of hands with cuffs and sleeves. No heads, no
chins, no faces anywhere.

THE CARDS ARE THE MOST IMPORTANT THING. Copy the reference card images closely. Every Word Card
is a TALL PORTRAIT card built in this exact stack, top to bottom:
  1. a deep NAVY ornate border with fine gold filigree running all the way around the card;
  2. inside it a warm CREAM PARCHMENT interior;
  3. near the top, a short WIDE LANDSCAPE illustration panel in a thin gold frame - a small
     painting inset into the card, clearly wider than it is tall;
  4. under that panel, THREE SHORT ROWS of small round dark dots - the stat pips;
  5. below those, two or three NARROW HORIZONTAL TEXT PANELS, each a lighter parchment block
     with a thin dark outline, stacked with small gaps between them;
  6. a dark navy bar across the very bottom edge.
Card text is suggested with soft illegible marks, never real letters. The proportions matter:
navy frame thin and even, parchment interior filling most of the card, the art panel small and
landscape, the text panels stacked and rectangular.

The FACE-DOWN cards show the navy card back from the reference: a gold filigree frame around a
central gold monogram. Lot cards are the same navy-and-parchment build but hold a row of small
empty bracket boxes instead of an illustration.

ON THE TABLE:
- CENTRE: a neat face-down draw pile of navy-backed cards, and beside it a loose face-up pile.
- One Lot card face-up near the centre, and one face-up in front of each seat.
- Each seat has a small fan of cards held in one hand, and a few gold coin tokens.
- The near-left player is PLAYING a card: their hand lowers a single Word Card face-up onto the
  table, mid-motion, catching the lamplight so its parchment face and small art panel read clearly.
- One seat has three Word Cards laid face-up in a neat row, a completed set.

WHISKEY: a cut-crystal tumbler of amber whiskey sits beside three of the four seats, one with a
single large ice cube, the crystal catching the lamplight and throwing warm amber highlights onto
the wood. A stoppered decanter of whiskey stands at the far edge of the table, half in shadow.
The amber of the glass should echo the gold of the lamp.

Warm, absorbed, unhurried - a serious game between friends, late in the evening. No text overlays,
no title, no logo, no watermark, no UI.
