## Word
**MAN** — A human being; mankind; the first human created by God; a husband or individual male.

## Old Testament
> Genesis 2:7 — "And the LORD God formed man of the dust of the ground, and breathed into his nostrils the breath of life."

## New Testament
> John 19:5 — "Then came Jesus forth, wearing the crown of thorns... And Pilate saith unto them, Behold the man!"

## Trivia
- The Hebrew word 'adam' is a play on 'adamah', meaning 'ground' or 'earth'.
- Jesus uses the title 'Son of Man' over 80 times in the Gospels to refer to Himself.
- Pilate's phrase 'Ecce Homo' (Behold the Man) presents Jesus as the archetype of humanity.
