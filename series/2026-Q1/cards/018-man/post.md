## Word
**MAN** — An adult human male; a human being; the human race; a person created in the image of God.

## Old Testament
> Genesis 2:7 — "The Lord God formed man of the dust of the ground, and breathed into his nostrils the breath of life."

## New Testament
> 1 Corinthians 15:47 — "The first man was of the earth, made of dust; the second Man is the Lord from heaven."

## Trivia
- The Hebrew 'adam' is related to 'adamah' (ground), emphasizing humanity's earthly origin.
- Occurs over 2,000 times in the Old Testament, often contrasted with God to highlight mortality.
- Jesus is frequently called the 'Son of Man,' linking His divinity with the human experience.
