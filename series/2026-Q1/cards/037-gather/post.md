## Word
**GATHER** — To bring together into one what was scattered abroad

## Old Testament
> Isaiah 11:12 — "gather together the dispersed of Judah"

## New Testament
> John 11:52 — "gather together in one the children of God"

## Trivia
- Hebrew qavats is the word the prophets use for God gathering exiles out of every land.
- Greek synago, to gather, is the root of synagogue - the gathering place.
- John 11:52 promises to gather into one the children scattered abroad - Babel reversed.
