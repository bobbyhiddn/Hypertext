## Word
**SEED** — Offspring; the kernel of life sown now for a later harvest

## Old Testament
> Genesis 3:15 — "enmity between thy seed and her seed"

## New Testament
> Galatians 3:16 — "and to thy seed, which is Christ"

## Trivia
- Hebrew zera names both literal grain and human offspring with one collective word.
- Paul's argument in Galatians 3:16 turns on the promise reading seed in the singular.
- Genesis 3:15 is called the protoevangelium, Scripture's first gospel promise.
