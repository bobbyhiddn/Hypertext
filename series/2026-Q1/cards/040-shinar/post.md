## Word
**SHINAR** — The plain of Babel, whose clay supplied brick for the tower

## Old Testament
> Genesis 11:2 — "a plain in the land of Shinar"

## New Testament
> Revelation 18:2 — "Babylon the great is fallen"

## Trivia
- Genesis 10:10 places Babel, Erech, and Accad in Shinar - the plain scholars link with Sumer.
- With no stone on the plain, Babel's builders fired brick for stone and used slime for mortar.
- Zechariah's vision carries wickedness in a basket back to Shinar - the land of exile again.
