## Word
**EVIL** — Morally wrong, wicked, or causing ruin and calamity.

## Old Testament
> Psalm 23:4 — "Even though I walk through the valley of the shadow of death, I will fear no evil, for you are with me..."

## New Testament
> Romans 12:21 — "Do not be overcome by evil, but overcome evil with good."

## Trivia
- In Hebrew, 'ra' can mean moral wickedness, but also natural calamity, injury, or distress.
- The Greek 'ponēros' emphasizes the active, harmful, and malignant nature of evil.
- Biblical authors frequently personify evil, culminating in the New Testament title 'the evil one'.
