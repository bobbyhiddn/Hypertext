## Word
**EVIL** — Morally wrong, wicked, or harmful; that which causes ruin or opposes God's good.

## Old Testament
> Genesis 6:5 — "Every intent of the thoughts of his heart was only evil continually."

## New Testament
> Romans 12:9 — "Abhor what is evil; hold fast to what is good."

## Trivia
- The Hebrew 'ra' encompasses not just moral wickedness, but also physical calamity, distress, and harm.
- In Greek, 'ponēros' describes an active, spreading malice that seeks to drag others down with it.
- The biblical concept of evil is not an equal opposite force to good, but a corruption of God's good design.
