## Word
**EVIL** — Morally wrong, wicked, harmful, or causing ruin.

## Old Testament
> Genesis 6:5 — "...every intention of the thoughts of his heart was only evil continually."

## New Testament
> Matthew 12:35 — "...and the evil person out of his evil treasure brings forth evil."

## Trivia
- The Hebrew word 'ra' is broad, meaning moral wickedness as well as disaster, calamity, or ruin.
- In the New Testament, the Greek word 'ponēros' conveys active harm or hurt, not just passive badness.
- Biblically, evil is not an original created substance, but rather the corruption or twisting of God's good creation.
