## Word
**EVIL** — Morally wrong, wicked, causing harm or destruction

## Old Testament
> Psalm 23:4 — "Even though I walk through the darkest valley, I will fear no evil, for you are with me."

## New Testament
> Romans 12:21 — "Do not be overcome by evil, but overcome evil with good."

## Trivia
- The Hebrew 'ra' encompasses not just moral wickedness, but also disaster, calamity, and physical harm.
- The Greek 'ponēros' often describes active, malignant evil that seeks to corrupt and burden others.
- In Genesis, evil first appears not as a created substance, but as the rejection of God's definition of good.
