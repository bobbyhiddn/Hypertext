## Word
**EVIL** — Morally wrong; wicked; causing harm, sorrow, or ruin.

## Old Testament
> Proverbs 8:13 — "To fear the Lord is to hate evil..."

## New Testament
> Romans 12:9 — "Hate what is evil; cling to what is good."

## Trivia
- The Hebrew word 'ra' can denote moral wickedness, but is also frequently used to describe physical disaster or calamity.
- In Greek, 'ponēros' emphasizes active, harmful malice and corruption, rather than just a lack of goodness.
- The biblical concept of evil is often depicted not as an original created substance, but as a parasitic corruption of what is good.
