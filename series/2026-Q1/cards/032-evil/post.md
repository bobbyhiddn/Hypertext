## Word
**EVIL** — morally wrong, wicked, causing ruin or harm

## Old Testament
> Psalm 23:4 — "I will fear no evil, for you are with me."

## New Testament
> Matthew 6:13 — "And lead us not into temptation, but deliver us from evil."

## Trivia
- In Hebrew, 'ra' can describe moral wickedness or refer to physical calamity and distress.
- The Greek 'ponēros' emphasizes an active, malignant corruption that seeks to drag others down.
- In the Lord's Prayer, 'deliver us from evil' can be translated as an abstract concept or 'the evil one'.
