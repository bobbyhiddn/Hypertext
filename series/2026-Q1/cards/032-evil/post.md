## Word
**EVIL** — Morally wrong, wicked, or causing harm and distress.

## Old Testament
> Genesis 6:5 — "Every inclination of the thoughts of the human heart was only evil all the time."

## New Testament
> Matthew 6:13 — "And lead us not into temptation, but deliver us from the evil one."

## Trivia
- The Hebrew word 'ra' can mean moral wickedness, but is also used to describe physical disaster or distress.
- In the Lord's Prayer, the Greek 'ponēros' can be translated as the abstract concept of 'evil' or personified as 'the evil one'.
- Biblical authors often contrast evil with good, portraying it not just as the absence of good, but as an active, corrupting force.
