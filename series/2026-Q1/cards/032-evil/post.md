## Word
**EVIL** — Morally wrong; wicked; causing harm or destruction

## Old Testament
> Genesis 6:5 — "The LORD saw that the wickedness of man was great in the earth, and that every intention of the thoughts of his heart was only evil continually."

## New Testament
> Romans 12:21 — "Do not be overcome by evil, but overcome evil with good."

## Trivia
- The Hebrew word 'ra' is used to describe both moral wickedness and physical calamity or distress.
- In the New Testament, 'poneros' often denotes active, malignant evil, sometimes personified as the 'evil one'.
- The first mention of evil in the Bible is the 'tree of the knowledge of good and evil' in the Garden of Eden.
