## Word
**EVIL** — Morally wrong, wicked, or causing ruin; that which actively opposes God's goodness.

## Old Testament
> Psalm 23:4 — "I will fear no evil, for you are with me..."

## New Testament
> Romans 12:9 — "Hate what is evil; cling to what is good."

## Trivia
- In Hebrew, 'ra' can refer to moral wickedness, but is also used to describe natural disasters, calamity, or physical harm.
- The Greek word 'ponēros' implies an active, malignant corruption that seeks to drag others down, rather than just passive badness.
- Biblically, evil is often depicted not as an equal opposite to God, but as a parasitic corruption of His originally good creation.
