## Word
**EVIL** — Actively wicked, morally corrupt, or causing harm and destruction.

## Old Testament
> Genesis 6:5 — "Every intent of the thoughts of their hearts was only evil continually."

## New Testament
> Romans 12:9 — "Abhor what is evil; hold fast to what is good."

## Trivia
- The Hebrew word 'ra' is first used in Genesis to describe the Tree of the Knowledge of Good and Evil.
- In Greek, 'ponēros' implies an active, malignant, and corrupting evil, distinct from 'kakos' which is a more general badness.
- The phrase 'the evil one' in the New Testament is derived from 'ho ponēros', personifying active rebellion against God.
