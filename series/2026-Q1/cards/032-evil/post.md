## Word
**EVIL** — morally bad, wicked, or harmful

## Old Testament
> Genesis 6:5 — "...every inclination of the thoughts of the human heart was only evil all the time."

## New Testament
> Romans 12:9 — "Hate what is evil; cling to what is good."

## Trivia
- In Hebrew, 'ra' can refer to moral wickedness, but is also frequently used to describe physical calamity, disaster, or distress.
- The Greek word 'ponēros' emphasizes an active, corrupting, and malignant kind of evil, rather than mere passive badness.
- Throughout Scripture, evil is consistently depicted not as a created substance, but as the perversion or absence of God's good order.
