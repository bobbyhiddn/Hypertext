## Word
**EVIL** — Morally corrupt, wicked, or causing ruin.

## Old Testament
> Psalm 23:4 — "I will fear no evil, for you are with me; your rod and your staff, they comfort me."

## New Testament
> Romans 12:21 — "Do not be overcome by evil, but overcome evil with good."

## Trivia
- In Hebrew, 'ra' can denote moral wickedness, but is also frequently used to describe natural disasters, distress, or calamity.
- The Greek word 'ponēros' emphasizes the active, malignant, and corrupting nature of evil, rather than just a passive flaw.
- In the Lord's Prayer, the Greek phrase 'deliver us from evil' can also be accurately translated as 'deliver us from the evil one.'
