## Word
**EVIL** — morally corrupt, actively harmful, or wicked

## Old Testament
> Genesis 50:20 — "As for you, you meant evil against me, but God meant it for good..."

## New Testament
> Matthew 6:13 — "And lead us not into temptation, but deliver us from evil."
