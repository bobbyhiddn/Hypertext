## Word
**EVIL** — Morally wrong, wicked, or causing ruin

## Old Testament
> Psalm 23:4 — "I will fear no evil, for you are with me;"

## New Testament
> Romans 12:9 — "Hate what is evil; cling to what is good."

## Trivia
- The Hebrew word 'ra' can refer to moral wickedness, but also to natural calamity or distress.
- In the Lord's Prayer, the Greek 'ponēros' can be translated as either the abstract 'evil' or the personified 'evil one'.
- The tree of the knowledge of good and evil represented humanity's grasp for moral autonomy apart from God's wisdom.
