## Word
**EVIL** — morally wrong, wicked, harmful, or calamitous

## Old Testament
> Psalm 23:4 — "I will fear no evil, for you are with me..."

## New Testament
> Romans 12:9 — "Hate what is evil; cling to what is good."

## Trivia
- The Hebrew word 'ra' describes moral wickedness, but is also frequently used to describe natural disasters or physical harm.
- In the Lord's Prayer, 'deliver us from evil' uses a form of 'ponēros' that can be translated personally as 'the evil one'.
- Biblically, evil is not an eternal opposing force equal to God, but rather a corruption or absence of God's good creation.
