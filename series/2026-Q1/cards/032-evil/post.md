## Word
**EVIL** — Morally bad, wicked, or harmful

## Old Testament
> Genesis 6:5 — "...every inclination of the thoughts of the human heart was only evil all the time."

## New Testament
> Romans 12:21 — "Do not be overcome by evil, but overcome evil with good."

## Trivia
- In Hebrew, the word 'ra' encompasses moral wickedness as well as disaster, distress, or calamity.
- The Greek word 'kakos' denotes something that is intrinsically base, depraved, or injurious.
- Scripture frames evil not as an equal opposite to good, but as a corruption that is ultimately conquered by God's goodness.
