## Word
**EVIL** — morally wrong or bad; wicked; bringing sorrow, distress, or calamity

## Old Testament
> Genesis 6:5 — "Every inclination of the thoughts of the human heart was only evil all the time."

## New Testament
> Romans 12:9 — "Hate what is evil; cling to what is good."

## Trivia
- In Hebrew, 'ra' encompasses not just moral wickedness, but also calamity, distress, and physical harm.
- The Greek word 'ponēros' emphasizes an active, malignant evil that seeks to corrupt and drag others down.
- In Eden, the tree of the knowledge of good and evil represented mankind claiming moral autonomy apart from God.
