## Word
**EVIL** — Morally wrong, wicked, or causing harm and destruction.

## Old Testament
> Psalm 140:1 — "Deliver me, O Lord, from evil men; preserve me from violent men."

## New Testament
> Ephesians 5:16 — "...making the best use of the time, because the days are evil."

## Trivia
- In Hebrew, 'ra' can describe moral wickedness, but also physical calamity, misery, or distress.
- The Greek word 'ponēros' emphasizes the active, corrupting, and malignant nature of evil.
- Biblical authors frequently contrast evil with light, life, and the inherent goodness of God's creation.
