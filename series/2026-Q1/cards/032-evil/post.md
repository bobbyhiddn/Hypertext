## Word
**EVIL** — morally wrong, wicked, or harmful

## Old Testament
> Psalm 23:4 — "I will fear no evil, for you are with me..."

## New Testament
> Romans 12:21 — "Do not be overcome by evil, but overcome evil with good."

## Trivia
- The Hebrew word 'ra' encompasses moral wickedness as well as calamity, distress, and injury.
- In the Lord's Prayer, the Greek phrase for 'deliver us from evil' can also be translated as 'the evil one'.
- The Greek 'ponēros' describes an active, malignant evil that seeks to corrupt and drag others down.
