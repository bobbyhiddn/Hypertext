## Word
**EVIL** — morally wrong, wicked, or causing harm and ruin

## Old Testament
> Isaiah 5:20 — "Woe to those who call evil good and good evil, who put darkness for light and light for darkness..."

## New Testament
> Romans 12:21 — "Do not be overcome by evil, but overcome evil with good."

## Trivia
- In Hebrew, 'ra' can refer to moral wickedness, but also natural disaster, calamity, or physical harm.
- The Greek word 'ponēros' emphasizes active, harmful malice rather than just being bad in quality.
- The phrase 'the evil one' in the Lord's Prayer uses the substantive form of ponēros.
