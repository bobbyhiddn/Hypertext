## Word
**EVIL** — Morally wrong, wicked, harmful, or calamitous.

## Old Testament
> Isaiah 5:20 — "Woe to those who call evil good and good evil, who put darkness for light and light for darkness..."

## New Testament
> Romans 12:9 — "Love must be sincere. Hate what is evil; cling to what is good."

## Trivia
- The Hebrew word 'ra' is used for both moral wickedness and the calamitous consequences or disasters brought as judgment [1.1.8].
- Greek distinguishes between 'kakos' (a passive lack of goodness) and 'ponēros' (an active, malicious, and corrupting evil).
- Isaiah 5:20 warns against moral inversion, pronouncing a strict 'woe' on those who celebrate evil as good.
