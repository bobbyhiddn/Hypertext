## Word
**EVIL** — Wicked, morally wrong, or harmful

## Old Testament
> Genesis 6:5 — "...every inclination of the thoughts of the human heart was only evil all the time."

## New Testament
> Romans 12:9 — "Love must be sincere. Hate what is evil; cling to what is good."

## Trivia
- In Hebrew, 'ra' can refer to moral wickedness, physical injury, or bringing calamity.
- The Greek word 'ponēros' emphasizes an active, harmful evil that seeks to corrupt others.
- Isaiah 5:20 pronounces a severe woe on those who invert morality by calling evil good.
