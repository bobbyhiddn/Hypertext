## Word
**EVIL** — Morally bad, wicked, or harmful

## Old Testament
> Genesis 6:5 — "...every inclination of the thoughts of the human heart was only evil all the time."

## New Testament
> Matthew 6:13 — "And lead us not into temptation, but deliver us from the evil one."

## Trivia
- In the Old Testament, the Hebrew word 'ra' can denote moral wickedness, but also physical harm, disaster, or calamity.
- The Greek word 'ponēros' often emphasizes an active, corrupting nature of evil, rather than just a passive badness.
- In the Lord's Prayer, the phrase 'the evil one' uses the substantive form of this adjective to personify wickedness.
