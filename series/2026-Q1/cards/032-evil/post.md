## Word
**EVIL** — wicked, harmful, bad

## Old Testament
> Genesis 6:5 — "...every inclination of the thoughts of the human heart was only evil all the time."

## New Testament
> Romans 12:9 — "Let love be genuine. Abhor what is evil; hold fast to what is good."

## Trivia
- The Hebrew word 'ra' [1.5] encompasses not only moral wickedness, but also natural calamity, distress, and injury.
- In the Greek New Testament, 'ponēros' emphasizes an active, malignant evil that causes pain and destruction.
- The Lord's Prayer petition to 'deliver us from the evil one' uses 'ponēros', highlighting an active spiritual adversary.
