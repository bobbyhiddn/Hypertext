## Word
**EVIL** — bad, harmful, wicked, adversity

## Old Testament
> Genesis 6:5 — "Every intent of the thoughts of his heart was only evil continually."

## New Testament
> Romans 12:21 — "Do not be overcome by evil, but overcome evil with good."

## Trivia
- In Hebrew, 'ra' can refer to moral wickedness, but also to physical calamity, dysfunction, or distress.
- The Greek 'kakos' describes something inherently bad or harmful, while 'ponēros' emphasizes active, spreading malice.
- Ancient Hebrew thought often contrasted 'ra' (dysfunctional) with 'tov' (functional) rather than strictly as spiritual opposites.
