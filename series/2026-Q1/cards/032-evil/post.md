## Word
**EVIL** — Morally bad, wicked, harmful, or malicious.

## Old Testament
> Genesis 6:5 — "...every inclination of the thoughts of the human heart was only evil all the time."

## New Testament
> Romans 12:9 — "Love must be sincere. Hate what is evil; cling to what is good."

## Trivia
- In Hebrew, 'ra' can refer to moral wickedness, but also to physical calamity, injury, or distress.
- The Greek 'ponēros' emphasizes an active, corrupting evil that seeks to drag others down with it.
- Isaiah 5:20 pronounces a severe woe on those who distort reality by calling evil good and good evil.
