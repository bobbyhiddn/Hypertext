## Word
**EVIL** — Morally corrupt, wicked, harmful, or bringing sorrow.

## Old Testament
> Genesis 6:5 — "...every inclination of the thoughts of the human heart was only evil all the time."

## New Testament
> Romans 12:9 — "Love must be sincere. Hate what is evil; cling to what is good."

## Trivia
- In Hebrew, 'ra' can refer to moral wickedness, but also to calamity, misery, or physical harm.
- The Greek word 'ponēros' describes an active, malignant evil that intentionally seeks to corrupt others.
- Scripture frequently personifies evil, culminating in the title 'the evil one' (ho ponēros) in the New Testament.
