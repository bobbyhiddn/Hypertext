## Word
**EVIL** — Morally wrong, wicked, harmful, or calamitous

## Old Testament
> Psalm 23:4 — "Even though I walk through the valley of the shadow of death, I will fear no evil, for you are with me..."

## New Testament
> Romans 12:21 — "Do not be overcome by evil, but overcome evil with good."

## Trivia
- The Hebrew word 'ra' can describe moral wickedness, but is also frequently used for natural disasters, calamity, or distress.
- In the Lord's Prayer (Matthew 6:13), the Greek 'ponēros' can be translated as the abstract concept of 'evil' or personified as 'the evil one'.
- Isaiah 5:20 pronounces a severe woe on those who distort reality by reversing moral categories, calling evil good and good evil.
