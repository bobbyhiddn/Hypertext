## Word
**EVIL** — Morally wrong, wicked, or causing harm.

## Old Testament
> Genesis 6:5 — "Every intention of the thoughts of his heart was only evil continually."

## New Testament
> Romans 12:9 — "Abhor what is evil; hold fast to what is good."

## Trivia
- The Hebrew word 'ra' encompasses not only moral wickedness but also physical calamity and distress.
- In the Lord's Prayer, 'deliver us from evil' uses 'ponēros', which can also be translated as 'the evil one'.
- Scripture often contrasts evil with good, showing it as a privation or corruption of God's original design.
