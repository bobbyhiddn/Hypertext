## Word
**EVIL** — Morally wrong, wicked, or harmful; causing ruin or pain.

## Old Testament
> Psalm 23:4 — "I will fear no evil, for you are with me..."

## New Testament
> Romans 12:21 — "Do not be overcome by evil, but overcome evil with good."

## Trivia
- In Hebrew, 'ra' can describe moral wickedness, but also physical calamity, misery, or disaster.
- The Greek term 'ponēros' emphasizes active, harmful corruption rather than just a passive lack of goodness.
- Biblical authors frequently contrast evil not merely with 'good', but with 'shalom'—complete peace and wholeness.
