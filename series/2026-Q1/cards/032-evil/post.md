## Word
**EVIL** — morally wrong, bad, or wicked; causing harm, destruction, or calamity

## Old Testament
> Proverbs 8:13 — "To fear the Lord is to hate evil..."

## New Testament
> Romans 12:9 — "Abhor what is evil; hold fast to what is good."

## Trivia
- The Hebrew word 'ra' is used extensively in the Old Testament to describe both moral wickedness and physical calamity or disaster.
- In the Greek New Testament, 'ponēros' implies an active, corrupting malice that seeks to drag others down into ruin.
- The famous petition 'deliver us from evil' in the Lord's Prayer (Matthew 6:13) can also be grammatically translated as 'deliver us from the evil one'.
