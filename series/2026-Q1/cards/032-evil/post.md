## Word
**EVIL** — Morally wrong, wicked, or inherently harmful and destructive.

## Old Testament
> Genesis 6:5 — "Every inclination of the thoughts of the human heart was only evil all the time."

## New Testament
> Matthew 6:13 — "And lead us not into temptation, but deliver us from the evil one."

## Trivia
- In biblical Hebrew, 'ra' can refer to moral wickedness, but it is also frequently used to describe physical calamity or disaster.
- The Greek word 'ponēros' emphasizes the active, corrupting nature of evil, rather than just the passive absence of good.
- When Isaiah 45:7 states that God creates 'evil' (ra), context shows it refers to bringing judgment or disaster, not moral sin.
