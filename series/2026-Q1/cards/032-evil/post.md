## Word
**EVIL** — Profoundly immoral, wicked, or harmful

## Old Testament
> Genesis 6:5 — "...every intent of the thoughts of his heart was only evil continually."

## New Testament
> Romans 12:9 — "Abhor what is evil; cling to what is good."

## Trivia
- The Hebrew word 'ra' is used for both moral wickedness and physical calamity or disaster.
- In Greek, 'ponēros' emphasizes the active, destructive, and pain-causing nature of evil.
- The first biblical mention of evil is the 'tree of the knowledge of good and evil' in Genesis 2.
