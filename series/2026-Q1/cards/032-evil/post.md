## Word
**EVIL** — Morally wrong, wicked, harmful; or, disaster, calamity, and misfortune.

## Old Testament
> Isaiah 45:7 (KJV) — "I form the light, and create darkness: I make peace, and create evil: I the LORD do all these things."

## New Testament
> Matthew 6:13 — "And lead us not into temptation, but deliver us from evil: For thine is the kingdom..."

## Trivia
- The Hebrew 'ra' encompasses both moral wickedness and physical calamity or disaster.
- Greek 'poneros' implies active, harmful evil, often personified as 'The Evil One' (Satan).
- In Isaiah 45:7, God 'creating evil' refers to His sovereignty over disaster and judgment, not sin.
