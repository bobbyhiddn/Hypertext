## Word
**EVIL** — Morally wrong, wicked, or causing harm.

## Old Testament
> Genesis 6:5 — "Every intent of the thoughts of his heart was only evil continually."

## New Testament
> Romans 12:9 — "Abhor what is evil; hold fast to what is good."

## Trivia
- In Hebrew, 'ra' can refer to moral wickedness as well as physical calamity or disaster.
- The Greek word 'ponēros' emphasizes the active, corrupting, and actively harmful nature of evil.
- Isaiah 5 pronounces a woe upon those who invert morality by calling evil good and good evil.
