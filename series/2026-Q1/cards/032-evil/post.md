## Word
**EVIL** — Profoundly immoral and malevolent; causing harm, sorrow, or destruction.

## Old Testament
> Genesis 6:5 — "Every inclination of the thoughts of the human heart was only evil all the time."

## New Testament
> Matthew 12:35 — "The evil person out of his evil treasure brings forth evil."

## Trivia
- The Greek ponēros denotes active, harmful wickedness, emphasizing the misery and agony that accompanies it.
- In Matthew 6:13, the phrase 'deliver us from evil' uses a substantive adjective, often translated as 'the evil one.'
- The Hebrew ra encompasses a wide spectrum, from moral depravity to the physical calamities and distress it causes.
