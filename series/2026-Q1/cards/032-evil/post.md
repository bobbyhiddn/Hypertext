## Word
**EVIL** — Wickedness, malice, or that which causes harm and destruction.

## Old Testament
> Psalm 23:4 — "Even though I walk through the darkest valley, I will fear no evil, for you are with me..."

## New Testament
> Romans 12:9 — "Love must be sincere. Hate what is evil; cling to what is good."

## Trivia
- The Hebrew word 'ra' encompasses both moral wickedness and physical calamity or harm.
- In the New Testament, 'ponēros' describes active, malicious evil, sometimes personified as 'the evil one'.
- The biblical narrative frames evil not as an original substance, but as a corruption of God's good creation.
