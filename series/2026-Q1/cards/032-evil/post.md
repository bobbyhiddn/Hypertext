## Word
**EVIL** — Profoundly immoral and wicked; causing ruin, harm, or injury.

## Old Testament
> Genesis 6:5 — "The LORD saw that the wickedness of man was great in the earth, and that every intention of the thoughts of his heart was only evil continually."

## New Testament
> Romans 12:9 — "Let love be genuine. Abhor what is evil; hold fast to what is good."

## Trivia
- The Greek word 'ponēros' refers to active, malignant evil that causes pain and sorrow, rather than just general badness.
- In Hebrew, 'ra' can refer to moral wickedness, but also to physical calamity, disaster, or distress.
- The phrase 'the evil one' in the New Testament specifically uses 'ponēros' to describe Satan's active rebellion.
