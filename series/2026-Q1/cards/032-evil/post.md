## Word
**EVIL** — Morally bad, wicked, or causing harm and destruction

## Old Testament
> Genesis 6:5 — "Every inclination of the thoughts of the human heart was only evil all the time."

## New Testament
> Romans 12:9 — "Love must be sincere. Hate what is evil; cling to what is good."

## Trivia
- The Hebrew word 'ra' is used for moral wickedness, but is also frequently translated as physical harm, disaster, or calamity.
- In the Lord's Prayer, the Greek 'ponēros' can be translated as the abstract concept of 'evil' or personified as 'the evil one.'
- Christian theology historically defines evil not as an independent substance, but as a privation or corruption of God's originally good creation.
