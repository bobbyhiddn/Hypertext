## Word
**EVIL** — Morally wrong, wicked, or harmful

## Old Testament
> Genesis 6:5 — "...every inclination of the thoughts of the human heart was only evil all the time."

## New Testament
> Matthew 6:13 — "And lead us not into temptation, but deliver us from the evil one."

## Trivia
- In the Old Testament, the Hebrew word 'ra' can mean moral wickedness or physical calamity.
- The Greek 'ponēros' often personifies evil, as seen in the title 'the evil one'.
- Scripture frequently contrasts evil with light, truth, and the holy nature of God.
