## Word
**EVIL** — Morally wrong, wicked, or causing harm, destruction, and calamity.

## Old Testament
> Psalm 23:4 — "I will fear no evil, for you are with me..."

## New Testament
> Romans 12:9 — "Hate what is evil; cling to what is good."

## Trivia
- In Hebrew, 'ra' can refer to moral wickedness, but is also frequently used to describe natural disasters, distress, or calamity.
- The Greek word 'ponēros' emphasizes an active, malignant kind of evil that intentionally seeks to corrupt others.
- In the Lord's Prayer, the petition 'deliver us from evil' is grammatically ambiguous and is often translated as 'deliver us from the evil one.'
