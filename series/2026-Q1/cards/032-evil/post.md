## Word
**EVIL** — Morally corrupt, wicked, or malevolent; causing harm, destruction, or sorrow.

## Old Testament
> Genesis 6:5 — "...every inclination of the thoughts of the human heart was only evil all the time."

## New Testament
> Romans 12:9 — "Hate what is evil; cling to what is good."

## Trivia
- The Hebrew word 'ra' encompasses not only moral wickedness, but also natural disaster, calamity, and distress.
- In the Lord's Prayer, 'deliver us from evil' can also be accurately translated from Greek as 'the evil one'.
- Isaiah 45:7 uses 'ra' to describe God bringing 'calamity' or 'disaster' as a judgment, rather than moral evil.
