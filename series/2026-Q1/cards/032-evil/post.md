## Word
**EVIL** — Morally wrong, wicked, or harmful; destructive calamity or active rebellion against God.

## Old Testament
> Isaiah 5:20 — "Woe to those who call evil good and good evil, who put darkness for light and light for darkness..."

## New Testament
> Romans 12:9 — "Let love be genuine. Abhor what is evil; hold fast to what is good."

## Trivia
- The Hebrew word 'ra' can describe moral wickedness, but also physical calamity, disaster, or dysfunction.
- In Greek, 'ponēros' emphasizes an active, malignant form of evil that causes pain and destruction.
- Biblically, evil is not an equal opposite force to God, but a corruption and distortion of His good creation.
