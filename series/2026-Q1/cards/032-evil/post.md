## Word
**EVIL** — Morally wrong, wicked, harmful, or corrupted.

## Old Testament
> Genesis 6:5 — "...every inclination of the thoughts of the human heart was only evil continually."

## New Testament
> Romans 12:9 — "Abhor what is evil; cling to what is good."

## Trivia
- The Hebrew word 'ra' encompasses moral wickedness, physical harm, and calamity.
- In Greek, 'ponēros' describes an active, malignant evil that actively causes destruction.
- The phrase 'the evil one' (ho ponēros) is frequently used in the New Testament to describe Satan.
