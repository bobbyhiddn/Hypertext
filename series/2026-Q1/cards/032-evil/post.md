## Word
**EVIL** — Morally wrong, wicked; causing ruin, harm, or distress.

## Old Testament
> Psalm 23:4 — "I will fear no evil, for you are with me; your rod and your staff, they comfort me."

## New Testament
> Romans 12:21 — "Do not be overcome by evil, but overcome evil with good."

## Trivia
- The Hebrew word 'ra' can refer to moral wickedness, but is also frequently used to describe physical calamity, distress, or injury.
- In the New Testament, 'ponēros' emphasizes the active, harmful, and aggressively corrupting nature of evil.
- The phrase 'the evil one' (ho ponēros) is frequently used as a specific title for Satan throughout the Gospels and Epistles.
