## Word
**EVIL** — Morally bad, wicked, harmful, malicious

## Old Testament
> Psalm 34:14 — "Depart from evil, and do good; seek peace, and pursue it."

## New Testament
> Romans 12:9 — "Abhor what is evil; hold fast to what is good."

## Trivia
- The Hebrew word 'ra' can mean moral wickedness, but also natural disaster or calamity.
- In the Lord's Prayer, 'deliver us from evil' is often translated as 'deliver us from the evil one.'
- The Greek word 'poneros' describes an active, malignant evil that causes pain and labor.
