## Word
**EVIL** — Wicked, harmful, or morally corrupt.

## Old Testament
> Isaiah 5:20 — "Woe to those who call evil good and good evil, who put darkness for light and light for darkness..."

## New Testament
> Romans 12:9 — "Love must be sincere. Hate what is evil; cling to what is good."

## Trivia
- The Hebrew 'ra' encompasses not just moral wickedness, but also calamity, distress, and physical harm.
- In the Lord's Prayer, 'deliver us from evil' can also be translated as 'the evil one' (ponēros).
- The biblical concept of evil is often defined as the corruption or absence of God's original good creation.
