## Word
**EVIL** — Morally wrong, wicked, or causing harm and ruin.

## Old Testament
> Psalm 34:14 — "Turn from evil and do good; seek peace and pursue it."

## New Testament
> Romans 12:21 — "Do not be overcome by evil, but overcome evil with good."

## Trivia
- The Hebrew word 'ra' encompasses not only moral wickedness but also physical harm, calamity, and distress.
- In Greek, 'ponēros' emphasizes the active, destructive, and pain-causing nature of evil.
- Scripture presents evil not as an equal opposite to good, but as a corruption or absence of God's good design.
