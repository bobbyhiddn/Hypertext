## Word
**EVIL** — Morally wrong, wicked, or causing ruin

## Old Testament
> Genesis 6:5 — "...every inclination of the thoughts of the human heart was only evil all the time."

## New Testament
> Romans 12:9 — "Hate what is evil; cling to what is good."

## Trivia
- In Hebrew, 'ra' can refer to moral wickedness, but also to calamity, misery, or physical injury.
- The Greek word 'ponēros' emphasizes an active, corrupting evil that seeks to spread and cause harm.
- Biblical writers frequently contrast evil with light, truth, and the unchanging goodness of God.
