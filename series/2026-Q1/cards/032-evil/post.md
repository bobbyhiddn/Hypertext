## Word
**EVIL** — wicked, bad, harmful

## Old Testament
> Genesis 6:5 — "...every inclination of the thoughts of the human heart was only evil all the time."

## New Testament
> Matthew 12:35 — "...an evil man brings evil things out of the evil stored up in him."

## Trivia
- In Hebrew, 'ra' spans the full spectrum of evil, including moral depravity, social wrongdoing, and material calamity.
- The Greek word 'ponēros' emphasizes an active, corrupting evil that causes labor, pain, and hardship.
- In the Lord's Prayer, the phrase 'deliver us from evil' can also be translated as 'deliver us from the evil one'.
