## Word
**EVIL** — Morally wrong, wicked, or causing ruin and calamity.

## Old Testament
> Genesis 6:5 — "...every intention of the thoughts of his heart was only evil continually."

## New Testament
> Romans 12:9 — "Abhor what is evil; hold fast to what is good."

## Trivia
- In Hebrew, 'ra' encompasses moral wickedness as well as calamity, disaster, and distress.
- The Greek 'ponēros' emphasizes the active, corrupting nature of evil, spreading like a disease.
- The Tree of the Knowledge of Good and Evil uses 'ra' to represent the full spectrum of moral autonomy.
