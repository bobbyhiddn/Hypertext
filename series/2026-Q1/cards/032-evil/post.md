## Word
**EVIL** — Profoundly immoral, harmful, or wicked; the absence or opposite of good.

## Old Testament
> Genesis 6:5 — "Every imagination of the thoughts of his heart was only evil continually."

## New Testament
> Matthew 6:13 — "And lead us not into temptation, but deliver us from evil."

## Trivia
- From Old English 'yfel', originally meaning 'exceeding proper limits' or 'vicious'.
- Greek 'poneros' implies active, harmful wickedness, while 'kakos' refers to bad nature.
- Theodicy is the theological study attempting to reconcile God's goodness with the existence of evil.
