## Word
**EVIL** — Morally bad, wicked, or malicious; causing harm, sorrow, or destruction.

## Old Testament
> Genesis 6:5 — "Every inclination of the thoughts of the human heart was only evil all the time."

## New Testament
> Romans 12:9 — "Love must be sincere. Hate what is evil; cling to what is good."

## Trivia
- InGreek, ponērosemphasizesanactive, malignantevilthatcauseslabor, pain, andsorrow[1.20].
- The Hebrew word ra' can refer to moral wickedness, but also to natural disaster, calamity, or distress.
- Biblical writers often contrast evil not just with 'good', but with life, peace, and the natural order of creation.
