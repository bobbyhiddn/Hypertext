## Word
**EVIL** — Morally bad, wicked, harmful, or destructive.

## Old Testament
> Genesis 6:5 — "Every inclination of the thoughts of the human heart was only evil all the time."

## New Testament
> Matthew 6:13 — "And lead us not into temptation, but deliver us from the evil one."

## Trivia
- The Hebrew word 'ra' can mean moral wickedness, but also physical harm, calamity, or distress.
- In the Lord's Prayer, 'the evil' (ponēros) is often translated as a personification: 'the evil one.'
- Biblical authors frequently contrast evil with good, light, and life, portraying it as a corruption of God's original design.
