## Word
**EVIL** — Profound immorality, wickedness, or destructive corruption

## Old Testament
> Genesis 6:5 — "The Lord saw that the wickedness of man was great in the earth, and that every intention of the thoughts of his heart was only evil continually."

## New Testament
> Romans 12:9 — "Let love be genuine. Abhor what is evil; hold fast to what is good."

## Trivia
- In Hebrew, 'ra' (evil) encompasses not only moral wickedness but also physical calamity, distress, and dysfunction.
- The Greek 'ponēros' emphasizes an active, malignant evil that causes pain and destruction, distinct from passive badness.
- Scripture often contrasts evil with good, commanding believers to 'abhor' or shudder away from wickedness.
