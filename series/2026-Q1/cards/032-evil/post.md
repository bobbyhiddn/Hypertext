## Word
**EVIL** — morally wrong, wicked, causing ruin or harm

## Old Testament
> Psalm 23:4 — "I will fear no evil, for you are with me..."

## New Testament
> Romans 12:9 — "Abhor what is evil; hold fast to what is good."

## Trivia
- In Hebrew, 'ra' can mean moral wickedness, but also natural disaster, calamity, or physical distress.
- The Greek 'ponēros' denotes an active, malignant form of evil that spreads and causes pain.
- In the Lord's Prayer, 'deliver us from evil' uses 'ponēros' and can also be translated 'the evil one'.
