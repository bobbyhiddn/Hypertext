## Word
**EVIL** — wickedness, harm, malice

## Old Testament
> Isaiah 5:20 — "Woe to those who call evil good and good evil, who put darkness for light and light for darkness..."

## New Testament
> Romans 12:9 — "Abhor what is evil; hold fast to what is good."

## Trivia
- In Hebrew, 'ra' (רַע) encompasses not just moral wickedness, but also physical calamity, distress, and harm.
- The Greek word 'ponēros' (πονηρός) emphasizes active, malignant evil that spreads like a disease, distinguishing it from general badness.
- In the Lord's Prayer, the phrase 'deliver us from evil' uses 'ponēros', which can also be translated personally as 'the evil one'.
