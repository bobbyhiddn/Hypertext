## Word
**EVIL** — Morally wrong, wicked, or causing harm and ruin.

## Old Testament
> Genesis 6:5 — "Every inclination of the thoughts of the human heart was only evil all the time."

## New Testament
> Romans 12:21 — "Do not be overcome by evil, but overcome evil with good."

## Trivia
- The Hebrew word 'ra' encompasses both moral wickedness and physical calamity or disaster.
- In the Lord's Prayer, 'deliver us from evil' uses 'ponēros', often translated as 'the evil one'.
- The Tree of the Knowledge of Good and Evil represented humanity's choice to define morality apart from God.
