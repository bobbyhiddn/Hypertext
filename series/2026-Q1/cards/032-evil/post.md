## Word
**EVIL** — wicked, bad, harmful, or malicious

## Old Testament
> Isaiah 5:20 — "Woe to those who call evil good and good evil, who put darkness for light and light for darkness..."

## New Testament
> Romans 12:9 — "Let love be genuine. Abhor what is evil; hold fast to what is good."

## Trivia
- In Hebrew, 'ra' can refer to moral wickedness, but it is also frequently used to describe natural disasters, calamity, or physical harm.
- The Greek 'ponēros' emphasizes an active, malignant evil that seeks to corrupt others, often contrasting with 'kakos' (which can mean base or worthless).
- Biblical authors frequently describe evil not as an independent force, but as a corruption or privation of the inherent goodness of God's creation.
