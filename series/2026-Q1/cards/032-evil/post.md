## Word
**EVIL** — Morally wrong, wicked, or causing harm, distress, and destruction.

## Old Testament
> Genesis 6:5 — "...every inclination of the thoughts of the human heart was only evil all the time."

## New Testament
> Matthew 6:13 — "...And lead us not into temptation, but deliver us from the evil one."

## Trivia
- The Hebrew word 'ra' can refer to moral wickedness, but is also frequently used to describe physical harm, disaster, or distress.
- In the Lord's Prayer (Matthew 6:13), the Greek adjective 'ponēros' is articulated, leading many to translate it as a personification: 'the evil one'.
- When Isaiah 45:7 says God creates 'evil' (KJV), the word 'ra' is used in the sense of bringing 'calamity' or 'disaster' in judgment, not moral sin.
