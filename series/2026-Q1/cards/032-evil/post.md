## Word
**EVIL** — Morally wrong, wicked, or harmful

## Old Testament
> Genesis 6:5 — "Every inclination of the thoughts of the human heart was only evil all the time."

## New Testament
> Romans 12:9 — "Hate what is evil; cling to what is good."

## Trivia
- The Hebrew 'ra' encompasses moral wickedness as well as calamity, distress, or injury.
- In Greek, 'ponēros' emphasizes active, harmful malice, whereas 'kakos' often implies a base lack of good.
- The petition 'deliver us from evil' in the Lord's Prayer is frequently translated by scholars as 'the evil one'.
