## Word
**EVIL** — bad, harmful, malicious, or morally corrupt

## Old Testament
> Isaiah 5:20 — "Woe to those who call evil good and good evil, who put darkness for light and light for darkness..."

## New Testament
> Romans 12:9 — "Let love be genuine. Abhor what is evil; hold fast to what is good."

## Trivia
- The Greek word 'ponēros' implies an active, malignant evil, while 'kakos' refers to a base or deeply flawed character.
- In Hebrew, 'ra' can describe moral wickedness, but also physical calamity, distress, or a harmful outcome.
- The Bible does not treat evil as an eternal counterpart to good, but as a corruption of God's original, good creation.
