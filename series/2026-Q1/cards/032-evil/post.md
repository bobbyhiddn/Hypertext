## Word
**EVIL** — Wickedness, malice, or harmful corruption.

## Old Testament
> Genesis 6:5 — "...every inclination of the thoughts of the human heart was only evil all the time."

## New Testament
> Romans 12:9 — "Hate what is evil; cling to what is good."

## Trivia
- The Hebrew word 'ra' encompasses moral wickedness as well as physical calamity, distress, and injury.
- The Greek 'ponēros' emphasizes active, malignant evil that spreads pain and corruption, rather than just bad character.
- In the Lord's Prayer, the phrase 'deliver us from evil' uses 'ho ponēros', which can also be translated as 'the evil one.'
