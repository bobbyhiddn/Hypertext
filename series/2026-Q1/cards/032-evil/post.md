## Word
**EVIL** — wickedness, harm, or moral corruption

## Old Testament
> Genesis 6:5 — "every intention of the thoughts of his heart was only evil continually."

## New Testament
> Matthew 6:13 — "deliver us from evil."

## Trivia
- In Hebrew, 'ra' can refer to moral wickedness, destructive actions, or physical calamity.
- The Greek word 'ponēros' emphasizes the active, malignant, and corrupting nature of evil.
- The Lord's Prayer petition 'deliver us from evil' can also be translated as 'deliver us from the evil one.'
