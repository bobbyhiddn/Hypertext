## Word
**EVIL** — Morally wrong, wicked, or causing sorrow, distress, and calamity.

## Old Testament
> Genesis 6:5 — "Every inclination of the thoughts of the human heart was only evil all the time."

## New Testament
> Romans 12:9 — "Abhor what is evil; hold fast to what is good."

## Trivia
- In Hebrew, 'ra' can refer to moral wickedness, but also natural disaster, calamity, or distress.
- The Greek 'ponēros' emphasizes active, harmful evil, distinguishing it from 'kakos' (general badness).
- In the original Greek of the Lord's Prayer, 'deliver us from evil' can also be translated as 'the evil one'.
