## Word
**EVIL** — Morally wrong or wicked; producing sorrow, distress, or ruin

## Old Testament
> Proverbs 8:13 — "To fear the LORD is to hate evil; I hate pride and arrogance, evil behavior and perverse speech."

## New Testament
> Romans 12:9 — "Love must be sincere. Hate what is evil; cling to what is good."

## Trivia
- The Hebrew word 'ra' can refer to moral wickedness, but is also frequently used to describe natural disaster, calamity, or distress.
- In the Lord's Prayer, the Greek phrase for 'deliver us from evil' can also be accurately translated as 'deliver us from the evil one'.
- Biblical authors frequently contrast evil directly with elemental concepts like light, life, and truth to emphasize its corrupting nature.
