## Word
**EVIL** — Morally bad, wicked, or harmful.

## Old Testament
> Psalm 23:4 — "Even though I walk through the valley of the shadow of death, I will fear no evil, for you are with me..."

## New Testament
> Matthew 6:13 — "And lead us not into temptation, but deliver us from evil."

## Trivia
- The Hebrew word 'ra' can mean moral wickedness, as well as physical harm, disaster, or calamity [1.1.2].
- In the Lord's Prayer, the Greek 'ponēros' can be translated as either 'evil' or 'the evil one'.
- The biblical narrative consistently contrasts evil with good, culminating in God overcoming evil with good.
