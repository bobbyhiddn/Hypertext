## Word
**EVIL** — Morally corrupt, wicked, or bringing harm and ruin.

## Old Testament
> Genesis 6:5 — "...every inclination of the thoughts of the human heart was only evil all the time."

## New Testament
> Matthew 6:13 — "...but deliver us from the evil one."

## Trivia
- In the Old Testament, the Hebrew word 'ra' can denote moral wickedness, but it is also frequently translated as physical calamity or disaster.
- Jesus frequently personified evil as 'the evil one' (ho ponēros), pointing to a conscious, spiritual adversary rather than just an abstract concept.
- Biblical authors continually contrast evil with light, truth, and the inherent goodness of God's original creation.
