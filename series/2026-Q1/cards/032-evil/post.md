## Word
**EVIL** — Profoundly immoral and wicked; harmful or destructive.

## Old Testament
> Genesis 6:5 — "Every imagination of the thoughts of his heart was only evil continually."

## New Testament
> Romans 12:21 — "Do not be overcome by evil, but overcome evil with good."

## Trivia
- The Hebrew word 'ra' can mean moral wickedness, but is also frequently translated as 'calamity' or 'disaster'.
- In the Lord's Prayer, 'deliver us from evil' uses 'ponēros' with a definite article, often translated as 'the evil one'.
- Isaiah 5:20 pronounces a woe on those who call evil good and good evil, subverting God's moral order.
