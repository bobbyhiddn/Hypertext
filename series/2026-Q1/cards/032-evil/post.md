## Word
**EVIL** — Morally corrupt; wicked; causing ruin, sorrow, or calamity.

## Old Testament
> Genesis 6:5 — "...every inclination of the thoughts of the human heart was only evil all the time."

## New Testament
> Matthew 6:13 — "And lead us not into temptation, but deliver us from the evil one."

## Trivia
- In Hebrew, 'ra' encompasses not just moral wickedness, but also physical calamity, disaster, or distress.
- The Greek word 'ponēros' emphasizes an active, malignant corruption that actively seeks to drag others down.
- Isaiah 5:20 pronounces a severe woe on those who reverse moral order by calling evil good and good evil.
