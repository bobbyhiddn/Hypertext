## Word
**EVIL** — profoundly immoral or wicked; harmful and destructive

## Old Testament
> Proverbs 8:13 — "To fear the Lord is to hate evil..."

## New Testament
> Romans 12:21 — "Do not be overcome by evil, but overcome evil with good."

## Trivia
- In Hebrew, 'ra' can denote moral wickedness, but is also frequently used to describe natural disaster, injury, or calamity.
- The Greek 'ponēros' specifically implies an active, corrupting kind of wickedness that seeks to drag others down.
- Biblical authors frequently contrast evil not just with good, but with life, light, and wisdom.
