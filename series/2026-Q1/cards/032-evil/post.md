## Word
**EVIL** — Morally bad, wicked, or harmful; producing sorrow, distress, or calamity.

## Old Testament
> Isaiah 5:20 — "Woe to those who call evil good and good evil, who put darkness for light and light for darkness..."

## New Testament
> Romans 12:21 — "Do not be overcome by evil, but overcome evil with good."

## Trivia
- In Hebrew, 'ra' can mean moral wickedness, but also natural disaster, calamity, or physical distress.
- The Greek 'ponēros' implies active, harmful malice, often personified in the New Testament as the 'evil one'.
- The biblical concept of evil is fundamentally defined as a corruption of or opposition to God's good creation.
