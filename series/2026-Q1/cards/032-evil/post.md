## Word
**EVIL** — morally bad, wicked, harmful

## Old Testament
> Psalm 23:4 — "Even though I walk through the valley of the shadow of death, I will fear no evil, for you are with me..."

## New Testament
> Romans 12:9 — "Let love be genuine. Abhor what is evil; hold fast to what is good."

## Trivia
- The Greek word 'ponēros' emphasizes evil that actively causes labor, pain, or sorrow.
- In Hebrew, 'ra' can describe moral wickedness as well as natural calamities and distress.
- New Testament writers frequently use 'ponēros' to personify malice as 'the evil one'.
