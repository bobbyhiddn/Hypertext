## Word
**EVIL** — morally wrong or bad; wicked; harmful

## Old Testament
> Genesis 6:5 — "...every intent of the thoughts of their hearts was only evil continually."

## New Testament
> Romans 12:9 — "Abhor what is evil; hold fast to what is good."

## Trivia
- The Hebrew word 'ra' can denote moral wickedness, but is also used for dysfunction, disaster, or physical harm.
- In Greek, 'ponēros' describes active, malignant evil that causes harm, contrasting with 'kakos' (inherent badness).
- In the Lord's Prayer, 'deliver us from evil' uses 'ponēros,' which many scholars translate personally as 'the evil one.'
