## Word
**EVIL** — Morally wrong, wicked, or harmful

## Old Testament
> Psalm 23:4 — "I will fear no evil, for you are with me..."

## New Testament
> Romans 12:9 — "Hate what is evil; cling to what is good."

## Trivia
- In Hebrew, 'ra' can mean moral wickedness, but also physical harm, disaster, or distress.
- The Greek word 'ponēros' implies an active, corrupting kind of evil that seeks to drag others down.
- The biblical concept of evil is often depicted not as an independent force, but as a corruption or privation of God's good creation.
