## Word
**EVIL** — Morally wrong, wicked, or causing harm and ruin.

## Old Testament
> Psalm 23:4 — "I will fear no evil, for you are with me..."

## New Testament
> Romans 12:21 — "Do not be overcome by evil, but overcome evil with good."

## Trivia
- The Hebrew word 'ra' encompasses both moral wickedness and physical calamity or distress.
- In the Lord's Prayer, the Greek 'ponēros' can be translated as either an abstract 'evil' or a personal 'evil one'.
- Isaiah warns against moral inversion, declaring a woe upon those who 'call evil good and good evil'.
