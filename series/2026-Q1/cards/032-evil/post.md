## Word
**EVIL** — Morally wrong, wicked, or bringing sorrow and distress

## Old Testament
> Genesis 6:5 — "...every inclination of the thoughts of the human heart was only evil all the time."

## New Testament
> Romans 12:21 — "Do not be overcome by evil, but overcome evil with good."

## Trivia
- The Hebrew word 'ra' (רַע) encompasses both moral wickedness and physical calamity or disaster.
- In the Lord's Prayer, the Greek 'ponēros' includes a definite article, leading many to translate it as 'the evil one'.
- The Tree of the Knowledge of Good and Evil represents the temptation to define morality apart from God's wisdom.
