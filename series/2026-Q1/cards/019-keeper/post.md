## Word
**KEEPER** — One who guards, watches over, maintains, or protects from loss

## Old Testament
> Genesis 4:9 — "And the Lord said unto Cain, Where is Abel thy brother? And he said, I know not: Am I my brother's keeper?"

## New Testament
> John 17:12 — "While I was with them in the world, I kept them in thy name... and none of them is lost."

## Trivia
- Cain famously used this word to deflect responsibility for Abel.
- The Sons of Korah preferred being doorkeepers in God's house to dwelling in wicked tents.
- Psalm 121 describes God as the Keeper of Israel who neither slumbers nor sleeps.
