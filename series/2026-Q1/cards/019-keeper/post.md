## Word
**KEEPER** — One who guards, watches, protects, or preserves; a warden or caretaker.

## Old Testament
> Psalm 121:5 — "The LORD is thy keeper: the LORD is thy shade upon thy right hand."

## New Testament
> Acts 5:23 — "The prison truly found we shut with all safety, and the keepers standing without before the doors."

## Trivia
- Cain famously deflected God's question by asking, "Am I my brother's keeper?"
- The Hebrew 'shamar' literally means to hedge about, guard, or protect with thorns.
