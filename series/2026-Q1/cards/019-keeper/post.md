## Word
**KEEPER** — One who guards, watches, or preserves

## Old Testament
> Psalm 121:5 — "The LORD is thy keeper: the LORD is thy shade upon thy right hand."

## New Testament
> Matthew 28:4 — "And for fear of him the keepers did shake, and became as dead men."

## Trivia
- Cain's question 'Am I my brother's keeper?' is a famous deflection of responsibility.
- The Hebrew 'shamar' means to hedge about, guard, or protect from harm.
- Psalm 121 declares that the Keeper of Israel neither slumbers nor sleeps.
