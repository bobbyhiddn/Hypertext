## Word
**KEEPER** — One who guards, watches over, maintains, or protects from harm.

## Old Testament
> Genesis 4:9 — "And the Lord said unto Cain, Where is Abel thy brother? And he said, I know not: Am I my brother's keeper?"

## New Testament
> Acts 5:23 — "The prison truly found we shut with all safety, and the keepers standing without before the doors."

## Trivia
- The Hebrew 'shomer' literally means 'to hedge about,' implying protection with thorns.
- First used by Cain in a deflection of responsibility regarding his brother Abel.
- Psalm 121 portrays God as the ultimate Keeper who 'shall not slumber nor sleep.'
