## Word
**DESTROYER** — The agent of God's wrath sent through the land

## Old Testament
> Exodus 12:23 — "will not suffer the destroyer to come in unto your houses"

## New Testament
> 1 Corinthians 10:10 — "and were destroyed of the destroyer"

## Trivia
- Mashchit is a participle - the one ruining - and the same word names the pit of corruption in Psalm 55.
- Paul's olothreutes in 1 Corinthians 10 is a New Testament hapax, echoing the Septuagint's word for the Passover destroyer.
- Revelation gives the office a name in two tongues, Abaddon and Apollyon - the Destroyer.
