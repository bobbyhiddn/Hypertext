## Word
**LAMECH** — The father of Noah, who named his son for comfort

## Old Testament
> Genesis 5:28 — "And Lamech lived an hundred eighty and two years, and begat a son"

## New Testament
> Luke 3:36 — "which was the son of Lamech"

## Trivia
- Both lines of Genesis carry a Lamech: one sings a song of vengeance, the other names the man who builds the ark.
- Lamech's is the last birth recorded before the flood generation begins.
- The name Noah sounds like rest, but Lamech's reason is comfort - the Hebrew leans on a second word.
