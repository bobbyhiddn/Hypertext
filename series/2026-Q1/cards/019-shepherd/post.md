## Word
**SHEPHERD** — One who tends, feeds, and guards a flock; a spiritual guide or protector.

## Old Testament
> Psalm 23:1 — "The LORD is my shepherd; I shall not want."

## New Testament
> John 10:11 — "I am the good shepherd: the good shepherd giveth his life for the sheep."

## Trivia
- "Pastor" is simply the Latin word for "shepherd."
- Egyptians considered all shepherds to be an abomination.
- David was called from the sheepfolds to shepherd Israel.
