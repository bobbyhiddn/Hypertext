## Word
**SHEPHERD** — One who tends, feeds, and guards a flock; a spiritual overseer.

## Old Testament
> Psalm 23:1 — "The LORD is my shepherd; I shall not want."

## New Testament
> John 10:11 — "I am the good shepherd. The good shepherd lays down his life for the sheep."

## Trivia
- Abel is the first person in the Bible described as a keeper of sheep.
- Shepherds were considered an abomination to the ancient Egyptians.
- David's skill with a sling was honed while defending his flock from predators.
