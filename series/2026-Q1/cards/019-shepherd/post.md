## Word
**SHEPHERD** — One who tends, feeds, and guards a flock; a spiritual guide or leader of people.

## Old Testament
> Psalm 23:1 — "The LORD is my shepherd; I shall not want."

## New Testament
> John 10:11 — "I am the good shepherd. The good shepherd gives His life for the sheep."

## Trivia
- Abel was the first shepherd mentioned in the Bible.
- King David tended sheep before he shepherded Israel.
- The English word 'pastor' comes from the Latin for shepherd.
