## Word
**WALK** — To go on step by step, as Enoch walked with God

## Old Testament
> Genesis 5:24 — "And Enoch walked with God"

## New Testament
> 2 Corinthians 5:7 — "For we walk by faith, not by sight"

## Trivia
- Genesis uses the same reflexive form for God walking in the garden in the cool of the day and for Enoch walking with God.
- Noah walked with God; Abram is told to walk before God - the prepositions mark two different callings.
- Paul's peripateo, to walk around, became the standard New Testament word for how a believer lives.
