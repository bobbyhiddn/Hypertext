## Word
**TONGUE** — The organ of speech, and the language it speaks; at Babel one became many

## Old Testament
> Genesis 10:5 — "every one after his tongue"

## New Testament
> Acts 2:4 — "began to speak with other tongues"

## Trivia
- Hebrew lashon names the tongue in the mouth, a language, and even a tongue of the sea.
- Genesis 10 lists the nations 'after his tongue' before Genesis 11 tells how the tongues were divided.
- Greek glossa gives English glossary - a list of hard words explained.
