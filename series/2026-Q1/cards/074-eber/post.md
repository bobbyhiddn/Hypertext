## Word
**EBER** — The ancestor whose name means the region beyond

## Old Testament
> Genesis 10:21 — "Shem also, the father of all the children of Eber"

## New Testament
> Luke 3:35 — "which was the son of Heber"

## Trivia
- Shem is introduced in Genesis 10:21 not as Noah's son but as the father of all the children of Eber - the line is named four generations early.
- Abram is first called the Hebrew in Genesis 14:13; the word is Eber's name with a gentilic ending.
- Eber lived four hundred sixty-four years and, by the numbers of Genesis 11, outlived Abraham.
