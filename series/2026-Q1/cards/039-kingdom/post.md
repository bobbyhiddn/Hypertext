## Word
**KINGDOM** — The realm and reign under one perfect rule, gathering every redeemed one

## Old Testament
> Daniel 2:44 — "a kingdom, which shall never be destroyed"

## New Testament
> Revelation 11:15 — "the kingdoms of this world are become the kingdoms of our Lord"

## Trivia
- Hebrew malkut and Greek basileia name both the realm and the active reign of the king.
- Daniel told Babylon's own king of a kingdom that would break every empire and stand forever.
- Revelation 11:15 crowns the story: the kingdoms of this world become the kingdom of our Lord.
