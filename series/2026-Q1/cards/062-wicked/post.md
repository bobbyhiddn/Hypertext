## Word
**WICKED** — Guilty before God and loose from his law

## Old Testament
> Psalm 1:5 — "the ungodly shall not stand in the judgment"

## New Testament
> Matthew 13:49 — "sever the wicked from among the just"

## Trivia
- Rasha is a courtroom word - the one found guilty - before it is a moral one.
- Genesis 6 says the wickedness of man was great in the earth; the flood answers a legal verdict.
- Poneros meant toilsome before it meant evil; the Evil One in the Lord's Prayer is the same word.
