## Word
**HEAR** — To perceive sound; to listen with attention, interest, and obedience.

## Old Testament
> Deuteronomy 6:4 — "Hear, O Israel: The LORD our God, the LORD is one."

## New Testament
> Romans 10:17 — "So faith comes from hearing, and hearing through the word of Christ."

## Trivia
- The Hebrew word 'shama' implies both listening and obeying.
- The 'Shema' is the central confession of faith in Judaism.
- Priests were consecrated by applying blood to the right ear lobe.
