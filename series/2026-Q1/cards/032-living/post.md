## Word
**LIVING** — Alive; active; enduring; flowing

## Old Testament
> Jeremiah 2:13 — "They have forsaken me, the fountain of living waters..."

## New Testament
> Mark 12:27 — "He is not God of the dead, but of the living."

## Trivia
- In Hebrew, 'chay' is often plural ('chayim'), reflecting the fullness and multifaceted nature of life.
- Jesus refers to Himself as the source of 'living water', a phrase that meant fresh, flowing water.
- The 'God of the living' argument by Jesus affirmed the resurrection against the Sadducees' denial.
