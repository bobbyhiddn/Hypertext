## Word
**DESCEND** — To move from a higher place to a lower one; to come down.

## Old Testament
> Genesis 28:12 — "And behold, the angels of God were ascending and descending on it."

## New Testament
> Matthew 3:16 — "He saw the Spirit of God descending like a dove and coming to rest on him."

## Trivia
- The Hebrew root *yarad* is the origin of the name 'Jordan' (the Descender).
- Used to describe God's presence coming down to Mount Sinai in fire.
- Theologically linked to the Incarnation and Christ's descent to the dead.
