## Word
**CHOSEN** — Set apart by God for his covenant

## Old Testament
> Psalm 105:6 — "ye children of Jacob his chosen"

## New Testament
> 1 Peter 2:9 — "ye are a chosen generation"

## Trivia
- Bachir is the passive of bachar, to choose - the chosen one is the one picked out.
- Eklektos means picked out from among; the same word gives eclectic, a choice made from many.
- After Babel scattered the nations, God chose one man from Ur - election begins as a departure.
