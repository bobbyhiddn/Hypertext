## Word
**NIMROD** — A mighty hunter and king; the first potentate on earth

## Old Testament
> Genesis 10:9 — "He was a mighty hunter before the Lord: wherefore it is said, Even as Nimrod the mighty hunter before the Lord."

## New Testament
> Luke 1:52 — "He hath put down the mighty from their seats, and exalted them of low degree."

## Trivia
- Name derives from the Hebrew verb 'marad', meaning 'to rebel'.
- Founded the cities of Babel, Erech, Accad, and Calneh.
- Described as the first 'mighty one' (gibbor) on the earth.
