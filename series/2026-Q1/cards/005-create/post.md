## Word
**CREATE** — To bring into existence; to form something new, often from nothing.

## Old Testament
> Genesis 1:1 — "In the beginning God created the heavens and the earth."

## New Testament
> Colossians 1:16 — "For by him all things were created, in heaven and on earth, visible and invisible."

## Trivia
- The Hebrew verb 'bara' is used exclusively for God's activity, never for humans.
- Genesis describes creation as bringing functional order out of chaos.
- The concept of 'creatio ex nihilo' (creation out of nothing) is central to theology.
