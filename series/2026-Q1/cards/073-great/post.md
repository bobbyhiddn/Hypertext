## Word
**GREAT** — Large in size or rank

## Old Testament
> Genesis 12:2 — "And I will make of thee a great nation"

## New Testament
> Matthew 20:26 — "whosoever will be great among you, let him be your minister"

## Trivia
- Genesis 1 never names the sun and moon; they are the greater light and the lesser light, gadol and qaton.
- Babel's builders wanted to make a name; God tells Abram he will make his name great - the same wish granted by gift instead of by brick.
- Nineveh is called that great city three times in Jonah; its founder Nimrod was the mighty hunter of Genesis 10.
