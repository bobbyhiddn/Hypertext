## Word
**BLESS** — To confer divine favor, invoke protection, or praise as holy; to speak well of.

## Old Testament
> Numbers 6:24 — "The LORD bless thee, and keep thee."

## New Testament
> Romans 12:14 — "Bless them which persecute you: bless, and curse not."

## Trivia
- The Hebrew root 'barak' is related to the word for 'knee,' implying kneeling to offer or receive favor.
- The 'Priestly Blessing' in Numbers 6 is one of the oldest known biblical texts, found on silver scrolls from 600 BC.
- The Greek word 'eulogeo' (to speak well of) is the origin of the English word 'eulogy.'
