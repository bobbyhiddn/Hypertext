## Word
**MESSENGER** — One sent to bear a word to those who wait for it

## Old Testament
> Proverbs 25:13 — "a faithful messenger to them that send him"

## New Testament
> Luke 7:27 — "I send my messenger before thy face"

## Trivia
- Hebrew malakh and Greek angelos both mean simply messenger - angel names the office, not the species.
- Isaiah 52:7 blesses the very feet of the messenger who brings good tidings over the mountains.
- Proverbs likens a faithful messenger to snow-cold water in harvest - refreshing those who sent him.
