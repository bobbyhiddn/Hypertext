## Word
**LORD** — One having power and authority; a master, ruler, or sovereign.

## Old Testament
> Job 1:21 — "The LORD gave, and the LORD has taken away; blessed be the name of the LORD."

## New Testament
> Philippians 2:11 — "And that every tongue should confess that Jesus Christ is Lord, to the glory of God the Father."

## Trivia
- "LORD" (all caps) represents the sacred name YHWH, while "Lord" translates the title Adonai.
- The Tetragrammaton (YHWH) appears over 6,800 times in the Hebrew Scriptures.
- The Greek Kyrios was used for YHWH in the Septuagint and later applied to Jesus.
