## Word
**LORD** — One having power and authority; a master or ruler. In Scripture, the personal name of God (YHWH).

## Old Testament
> Deuteronomy 6:4 — "Hear, O Israel: The LORD our God, the LORD is one."

## New Testament
> Philippians 2:11 — "And every tongue confess that Jesus Christ is Lord, to the glory of God the Father."

## Trivia
- "LORD" (all caps) represents the Tetragrammaton (YHWH), God's personal name.
- "Lord" (mixed case) translates Adonai (Master) or Kyrios (Ruler).
- The confession "Jesus is Lord" was a subversive challenge to the claim "Caesar is Lord."
