## Word
**LORD** — A master, ruler, or sovereign; the self-existent One (YHWH) who establishes the covenant.

## Old Testament
> Deuteronomy 6:4 — "Hear, O Israel: The LORD our God, the LORD is one."

## New Testament
> Revelation 19:16 — "On his robe and on his thigh he has this name written: KING OF KINGS AND LORD OF LORDS."

## Trivia
- "LORD" (all caps) represents the divine name YHWH, while "Lord" translates Adonai (Master).
- The Greek Kyrios was used for YHWH in the Septuagint and applied to Jesus in the New Testament.
- Appears over 6,000 times in the Old Testament, revealing God as the personal, covenant-keeping Sovereign.
