## Word
**LORD** — A master, ruler, or person of authority; in scripture, the proper name of God (YHWH) or title for Jesus.

## Old Testament
> Job 1:21 — "The LORD gave, and the LORD has taken away; blessed be the name of the LORD."

## New Testament
> Revelation 19:16 — "And he hath on his vesture and on his thigh a name written, KING OF KINGS, AND LORD OF LORDS."

## Trivia
- In English Bibles, 'LORD' (all caps) usually substitutes for the Hebrew name YHWH.
- The Greek 'Kurios' is used in the NT for both God the Father and Jesus.
- The phrase 'Lord of hosts' appears over 200 times, referring to armies of angels.
