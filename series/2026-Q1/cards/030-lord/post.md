## Word
**LORD** — One possessing supreme authority and ownership; the personal covenant name of God (YHWH).

## Old Testament
> 1 Samuel 2:6 — "The LORD kills and brings to life; he brings down to Sheol and raises up."

## New Testament
> Philippians 2:11 — "And that every tongue should confess that Jesus Christ is Lord, to the glory of God the Father."

## Trivia
- In English Bibles, 'LORD' (all caps) represents the Tetragrammaton YHWH.
- Jewish tradition substitutes 'Adonai' when reading YHWH to honor its sanctity.
- The Greek 'Kyrios' was used for YHWH in the Septuagint and later applied to Jesus.
