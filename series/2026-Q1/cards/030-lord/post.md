## Word
**LORD** — A master, ruler, or person in authority; specifically, the proper name of the God of Israel (YHWH).

## Old Testament
> Deuteronomy 6:4 — "Hear, O Israel: The LORD our God, the LORD is one."

## New Testament
> Philippians 2:11 — "...and every tongue acknowledge that Jesus Christ is Lord..."

## Trivia
- "LORD" in small caps represents the Hebrew Tetragrammaton (YHWH), God's personal name.
- The Greek *Kyrios* translates both *Adonai* (Master) and *YHWH* in the Septuagint.
- Applying *Kyrios* to Jesus in the NT was a radical claim of His divinity.
