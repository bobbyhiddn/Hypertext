## Word
**LORD** — A master, ruler, or owner; specifically the proper name of the God of Israel (YHWH) and title of Jesus.

## Old Testament
> 1 Samuel 2:6 — "The LORD kills and brings to life; he brings down to Sheol and raises up."

## New Testament
> Philippians 2:11 — "And that every tongue should confess that Jesus Christ is Lord, to the glory of God the Father."

## Trivia
- In English Bibles, 'LORD' (small caps) represents the Tetragrammaton, YHWH.
- The Greek 'Kyrios' was a title for Caesar, making its application to Jesus politically subversive.
- Jewish readers traditionally substitute 'Adonai' (Master) to avoid pronouncing the Divine Name.
