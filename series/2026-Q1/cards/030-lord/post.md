## Word
**LORD** — One possessing absolute authority, ownership, and power; a master or ruler. The personal covenant name of God.

## Old Testament
> 1 Samuel 2:7 — "The LORD makes poor and makes rich; he brings low and he exalts."

## New Testament
> Philippians 2:11 — "And that every tongue should confess that Jesus Christ is Lord, to the glory of God the Father."

## Trivia
- The Tetragrammaton (YHWH) appears over 6,800 times in the OT, usually translated as LORD.
- Jewish tradition substitutes 'Adonai' (Master) when reading YHWH to avoid pronouncing the divine name.
- The Greek 'Kyrios' is used in the NT for both YHWH and Jesus, affirming Christ's deity.
