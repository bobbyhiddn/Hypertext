## Word
**LORD** — The personal name of God (YHWH); a master, ruler, or sovereign with supreme authority.

## Old Testament
> Deuteronomy 6:4 — "Hear, O Israel: The LORD our God, the LORD is one."

## New Testament
> Philippians 2:11 — "And that every tongue should confess that Jesus Christ is Lord, to the glory of God the Father."

## Trivia
- In English Bibles, 'LORD' (all caps) represents the divine name YHWH, which appears over 6,800 times.
- The Septuagint translated YHWH as 'Kyrios', a title the New Testament writers applied directly to Jesus.
- Jewish tradition substitutes 'Adonai' (my Lord) when reading to avoid pronouncing the sacred name.
