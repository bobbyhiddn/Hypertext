## Word
**LORD** — A master, ruler, or owner; the one possessing supreme authority and power; the personal name of God (Yahweh).

## Old Testament
> Exodus 20:2 — "I am the LORD your God, who brought you out of the land of Egypt, out of the house of slavery."

## New Testament
> Philippians 2:11 — "And that every tongue should confess that Jesus Christ is Lord, to the glory of God the Father."

## Trivia
- In English Bibles, 'LORD' (small caps) represents the Tetragrammaton (YHWH), God's personal name.
- The Greek 'Kyrios' is used for both Yahweh and Jesus in the NT, affirming Christ's divinity.
- The word implies absolute ownership, sovereignty, and the right to command obedience.
