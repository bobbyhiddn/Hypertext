## Word
**LORD** — One having power and authority over others; a master or ruler.

## Old Testament
> Deuteronomy 6:4 — "Hear, O Israel: The LORD our God, the LORD is one."

## New Testament
> Philippians 2:11 — "And every tongue confess that Jesus Christ is Lord, to the glory of God the Father."

## Trivia
- In English Bibles, 'LORD' (small caps) represents the Hebrew name Yahweh.
- The Greek 'Kyrios' was used by the Septuagint to translate the divine name.
- This title appears over 6,800 times in the Old Testament, more than any other.
