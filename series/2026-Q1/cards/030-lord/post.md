## Word
**LORD** — One having power and authority over others; the supreme Sovereign; the Tetragrammaton (YHWH).

## Old Testament
> 1 Samuel 2:6 — "The LORD kills and brings to life; he brings down to Sheol and raises up."

## New Testament
> Philippians 2:11 — "...and every tongue confess that Jesus Christ is Lord, to the glory of God the Father."

## Trivia
- In English Bibles, 'LORD' (small caps) denotes the Hebrew name YHWH, while 'Lord' translates Adonai.
- The Greek Kyrios was used in the Septuagint for YHWH and later applied to Jesus in the New Testament.
- The Tetragrammaton appears over 6,800 times in the Hebrew Bible, often substituted vocally with Adonai.
