## Word
**LORD** — The personal name of God (YHWH); supreme master, owner, or sovereign ruler.

## Old Testament
> Deuteronomy 6:4 — "Hear, O Israel: The LORD our God, the LORD is one."

## New Testament
> Revelation 19:16 — "On his robe and on his thigh he has this name written: KING OF KINGS AND LORD OF LORDS."

## Trivia
- The Tetragrammaton (YHWH) appears over 6,800 times in the OT.
- English Bibles use 'LORD' (small caps) for YHWH and 'Lord' for Adonai.
- The name implies eternal self-existence: 'I AM WHO I AM'.
