## Word
**LORD** — A master, ruler, or sovereign having authority; often denotes the personal name of God (YHWH).

## Old Testament
> Job 1:21 — "The LORD gave, and the LORD has taken away; blessed be the name of the LORD."

## New Testament
> Philippians 2:11 — "And that every tongue should confess that Jesus Christ is Lord, to the glory of God the Father."

## Trivia
- In the KJV, 'LORD' (all caps) represents the divine name YHWH.
- The Greek 'Kyrios' is used for both God the Father and Jesus.
- Appears over 6,000 times in the Old Testament alone.
