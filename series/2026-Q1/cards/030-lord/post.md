## Word
**LORD** — A master, ruler, or sovereign; one having power and authority over others.

## Old Testament
> Deuteronomy 6:4 — "Hear, O Israel: The LORD our God, the LORD is one."

## New Testament
> Revelation 19:16 — "On his robe and on his thigh he has this name written: KING OF KINGS AND LORD OF LORDS."

## Trivia
- Often printed in small caps (LORD) to distinguish the name YHWH from the title Adonai.
- Derived from Old English 'hlaf-weard' meaning 'loaf-guardian' or provider of bread.
- The Septuagint translated YHWH as Kyrios, a title later applied to Jesus in the New Testament.
