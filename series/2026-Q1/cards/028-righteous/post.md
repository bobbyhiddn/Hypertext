## Word
**RIGHTEOUS** — Acting in accord with divine or moral law; free from guilt or sin; justifiable.

## Old Testament
> Proverbs 10:2 — "Treasures of wickedness profit nothing: but righteousness delivereth from death."

## New Testament
> 2 Corinthians 5:21 — "For he hath made him to be sin for us... that we might be made the righteousness of God in him."

## Trivia
- Hebrew 'tsaddiq' implies adhering to a standard or walking a straight path.
- Paul reveals righteousness as a gift received by faith, not a wage earned by works.
- Jehovah Tsidkenu is a messianic title meaning 'The LORD Our Righteousness'.
