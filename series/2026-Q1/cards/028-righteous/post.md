## Word
**RIGHTEOUS** — Morally right or justifiable; acting in accord with divine or moral law.

## Old Testament
> Genesis 15:6 — "Abram believed the LORD, and he credited it to him as righteousness."

## New Testament
> Romans 1:17 — "For in the gospel the righteousness of God is revealed... The righteous will live by faith."

## Trivia
- First used in the Bible to describe Noah (Genesis 6:9).
- The Hebrew 'tsaddiq' implies adherence to a standard or covenant.
- In the NT, righteousness is often 'imputed' (credited) by faith rather than earned.
