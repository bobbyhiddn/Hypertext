## Word
**MULTIPLY** — To increase greatly in number, quantity, or extent; to reproduce.

## Old Testament
> Genesis 1:22 — "Be fruitful and multiply, and fill the waters in the seas."

## New Testament
> Acts 6:7 — "The number of the disciples continued to multiply greatly in Jerusalem."

## Trivia
- The first command God gave to living creatures (Gen 1:22).
- Jesus multiplied loaves and fish to feed thousands on two separate occasions.
- In Acts, the 'multiplication' of disciples describes the church's explosive growth.
