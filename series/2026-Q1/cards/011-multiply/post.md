## Word
**MULTIPLY** — To increase in number, quantity, or degree; to reproduce.

## Old Testament
> Genesis 1:28 — "Be fruitful and multiply, and fill the earth."

## New Testament
> Acts 6:7 — "The number of the disciples multiplied greatly in Jerusalem."

## Trivia
- First command given to humanity (Gen 1:28)
- Describes the rapid growth of the early church
- God promised to multiply Abraham's offspring like stars
