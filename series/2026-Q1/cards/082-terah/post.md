![TERAH card](./outputs/card_1024x1536.png)

## Word
**TERAH** — The father of Abram, who set out for Canaan and stopped at Haran

## Old Testament
> Genesis 11:31 — "And Terah took Abram his son ... to go into the land of Canaan"

## New Testament
> Luke 3:34 — "which was the son of Thara"

## Trivia
- Terah starts for Canaan a generation before Abram is called there, and never arrives.
- Haran is both the name of Terah's dead son and the name of the city where he settles.
- Genesis 11 closes with Terah's death; Genesis 12 opens with the call, so the journey is finished by the son.
