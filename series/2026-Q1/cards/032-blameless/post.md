## Word
**BLAMELESS** — Without fault, innocent, pure

## Old Testament
> Genesis 17:1 — "I am God Almighty; walk before me, and be blameless."

## New Testament
> Philippians 2:15 — "That you may be blameless and innocent, children of God without blemish..."

## Trivia
- In the Old Testament, 'tamim' is frequently used to describe the strict requirement that sacrificial animals be entirely without physical blemish.
- Job is introduced as a 'blameless and upright' man, demonstrating that biblical blamelessness refers to relational integrity, not sinless perfection.
- The Greek word 'amemptos' was used in civic contexts to describe a citizen whose life left no room for legitimate public accusation.
