## Word
**BLAMELESS** — Without fault, innocent, free from accusation.

## Old Testament
> Job 1:1 — "That man was blameless and upright, one who feared God and turned away from evil."

## New Testament
> Philippians 2:15 — "...that you may be blameless and innocent, children of God without blemish..."

## Trivia
- In Hebrew, 'tam' implies completeness, moral integrity, or being upright, famously used to describe Job.
- The Greek 'amemptos' describes a life free from legitimate accusation before God or humanity.
- The concept does not imply sinless perfection, but rather a character that is above reproach.
