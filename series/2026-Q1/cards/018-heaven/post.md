## Word
**HEAVEN** — The dwelling place of God and the home of the blessed; the sky or firmament.

## Old Testament
> Psalm 19:1 — "The heavens declare the glory of God; and the firmament sheweth his handywork."

## New Testament
> Matthew 6:20 — "Lay up for yourselves treasures in heaven, where neither moth nor rust doth corrupt."

## Trivia
- The Hebrew word *shamayim* is a dual form, implying 'two skies' or 'waters there'.
- Matthew uses 'Kingdom of Heaven' 32 times to respect Jewish avoidance of God's name.
- Paul mentions a 'third heaven' in 2 Corinthians, referring to God's immediate presence.
