## Word
**DRY** — Without water, as the dry land that appeared

## Old Testament
> Genesis 1:9 — "let the dry land appear: and it was so"

## New Testament
> Hebrews 11:29 — "they passed through the Red sea as by dry land"

## Trivia
- The dry appears on the third day before anything is planted in it; the ground is made ready before it is filled.
- Genesis 8 measures the flood's end twice - the waters were abated, and then the face of the ground was dry.
- Jesus asks what shall be done in the dry, using the same word Greek readers knew for dry land.
