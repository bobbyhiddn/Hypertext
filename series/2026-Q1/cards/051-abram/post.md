## Word
**ABRAM** — Called out of Ur to a land unseen, to become a blessing

## Old Testament
> Genesis 12:1 — "the LORD had said unto Abram, Get thee out"

## New Testament
> Hebrews 11:8 — "By faith Abraham, when he was called"

## Trivia
- Abram means exalted father; God lengthened it to Abraham, father of a multitude.
- Ur of the Chaldees lay on the same plain of Shinar where Babel's tower rose.
- The New Testament never uses the short form - to Paul and Hebrews he is always Abraham, the name of the promise.
