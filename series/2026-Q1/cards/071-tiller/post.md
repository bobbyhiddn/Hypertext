## Word
**TILLER** — One who works the ground

## Old Testament
> Genesis 4:2 — "but Cain was a tiller of the ground"

## New Testament
> John 15:1 — "I am the true vine, and my Father is the husbandman"

## Trivia
- Genesis 2:15 gives the man two verbs, to dress and to keep; the tiller and the keeper are the two oldest trades.
- Cain's offering came from the ground he tilled; after the murder the ground would no longer yield him its strength.
- Georgos, earth-worker, gives English the name George; Jesus calls his Father the georgos of the true vine.
