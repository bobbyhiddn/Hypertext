## Word
**CITY** — A large human settlement; a center of population, commerce, and defense.

## Old Testament
> Psalm 127:1 — "Unless the Lord watches over the city, the watchmen stand guard in vain."

## New Testament
> Hebrews 11:10 — "For he was looking forward to the city with foundations, whose architect and builder is God."

## Trivia
- Cain built the first city in Scripture, naming it Enoch after his son.
- Jerusalem is mentioned over 800 times in the Bible.
- The Bible begins in a garden (Eden) and ends in a city (New Jerusalem).
