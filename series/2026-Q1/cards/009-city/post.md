## Word
**CITY** — A large human settlement; a center of population, commerce, and defense.

## Old Testament
> Psalm 127:1 — "Unless the Lord watches over the city, the watchman stays awake in vain."

## New Testament
> Matthew 5:14 — "You are the light of the world. A city set on a hill cannot be hidden."

## Trivia
- The first city recorded in Scripture was built by Cain and named Enoch.
- Jerusalem is mentioned over 800 times in the Bible.
- Scripture begins in a garden (Eden) and ends in a city (New Jerusalem).
