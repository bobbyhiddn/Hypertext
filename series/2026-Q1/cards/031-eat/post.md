## Word
**EAT** — To consume, devour, or take in; often used metaphorically for learning, destruction, or spiritual participation.

## Old Testament
> Ezekiel 3:1 — "Son of man, eat what is before you, eat this scroll; then go and speak to the people of Israel."

## New Testament
> Matthew 26:26 — "Jesus took bread... and gave it to the disciples, saying, 'Take, eat; this is my body.'"

## Trivia
- The first command given to humanity was a permission to eat freely from the garden's trees.
- In Hebrew idiom, fire and swords are often described as 'eating' (consuming) their targets.
- John the Apostle was commanded to eat a scroll in Revelation, mirroring Ezekiel's prophetic act.
