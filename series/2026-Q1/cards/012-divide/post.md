![DIVIDE card](./outputs/card_1024x1536.png)

## Word
**DIVIDE** — To separate into parts; to distinguish, apportion, or cleave.

## Old Testament
> Genesis 1:4 — "And God saw the light, that it was good: and God divided the light from the darkness."

## New Testament
> Hebrews 4:12 — "Piercing even to the dividing asunder of soul and spirit, and of the joints and marrow..."

## Trivia
- The first specific act of ordering creation involved dividing light from darkness.
- The Greek 'merizo' often implies distributing portions or shares, not just cutting.
- In the Kingdom, division is often a precursor to judgment or multiplication.
