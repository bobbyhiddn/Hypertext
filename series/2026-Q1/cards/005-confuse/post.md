![CONFUSE card](./outputs/card_1024x1536.png)

## Word
**CONFUSE** — To mix up, bewilder, or make unclear; to disrupt order or understanding.

## Old Testament
> Genesis 11:7 — "Come, let Us go down and there confuse their language, that they may not understand one another’s speech."

## New Testament
> Acts 19:32 — "The assembly was confused, and most of them did not know why they had come together."

## Trivia
- The Hebrew word 'balal' sounds like 'Babel', creating a thematic wordplay.
- At Pentecost (Acts 2), the confusion of languages from Babel was supernaturally reversed.
- Paul contrasts confusion with peace in 1 Corinthians, stating God is not its author.
