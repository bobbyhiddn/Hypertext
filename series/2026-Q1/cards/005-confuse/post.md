## Word
**CONFUSE** — To mix up, disorder, or baffle; to make indistinct or obscure.

## Old Testament
> Genesis 11:7 — "Come, let us go down and there confuse their language, so that they will not understand one another's speech."

## New Testament
> Acts 2:6 — "The multitude came together, and were confounded, because that every man heard them speak in his own language."

## Trivia
- From Latin 'confundere' meaning 'to pour together'.
- Hebrew 'balal' is a wordplay on 'Babel', linking the city to confusion.
- Pentecost (Acts 2) reverses the linguistic confusion of Babel.
