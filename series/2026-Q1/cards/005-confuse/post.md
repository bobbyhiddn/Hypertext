![CONFUSE card](./outputs/card_1024x1536.png)

## Word
**CONFUSE** — To mix up, disorder, or bewilder; to make indistinguishable.

## Old Testament
> Genesis 11:7 — "Come, let us go down and there confuse their language, so that they will not understand one another's speech."

## New Testament
> Acts 2:6 — "Now when this was noised abroad, the multitude came together, and were confounded..."

## Trivia
- The Hebrew word 'balal' sounds like 'babble', mimicking incoherent speech.
- The confusion of Babel is thematically reversed at Pentecost in Acts 2.
- Paul contrasts confusion with peace in 1 Corinthians 14:33.
