![CONFUSE card](./outputs/card_1024x1536.png)

## Word
**CONFUSE** — To mix up, bewilder, or throw into disorder; to make unclear or indistinguishable.

## Old Testament
> Genesis 11:7 — "Come, let Us go down and there confuse their language, that they may not understand one another's speech."

## New Testament
> Acts 2:6 — "And when this sound occurred, the crowd came together, and were bewildered, because each one was hearing them speak in his own language."

## Trivia
- The Hebrew 'balal' sounds like 'Babel' and means to mix or mingle.
- God confused language at Babel to halt the prideful construction of the tower.
- The confusion of Babel was spiritually reversed by the Holy Spirit at Pentecost.
