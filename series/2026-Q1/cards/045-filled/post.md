## Word
**FILLED** — Made full; brimming with what has been poured in

## Old Testament
> Exodus 31:3 — "I have filled him with the spirit of God"

## New Testament
> Ephesians 5:18 — "be filled with the Spirit"

## Trivia
- Hebrew male describes Bezalel filled with the Spirit for craftsmanship long before Pentecost.
- Greek pleroo means both to fill full and to fulfil - one word for a filled net and a fulfilled prophecy.
- Ephesians 5:18 sets being filled with wine against being filled with the Spirit - two fullnesses.
