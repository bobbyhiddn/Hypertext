## Word
**PRIEST** — The one who stands between and receives the offering

## Old Testament
> Genesis 14:18 — "he brought forth bread and wine: and he was the priest of the most high God"

## New Testament
> Hebrews 7:1 — "this Melchisedec, king of Salem, priest of the most high God"

## Trivia
- Melchizedek is the first priest named in Scripture, and he appears centuries before there is a priesthood.
- Abram gives him tithes of all - the tithe is older than the law that commands it.
- Hebrews argues from the silence of Genesis: no father, no mother, no beginning of days recorded, therefore a priest for ever.
