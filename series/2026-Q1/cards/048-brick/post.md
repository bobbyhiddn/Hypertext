# Hypertext — BRICK

![BRICK card](./outputs/card_1024x1536.png)

## Word
**BRICK** — Clay shaped and fired to build with, where stone is lacking

## Old Testament
> Genesis 11:3 — "let us make brick"

## New Testament
> 1 Peter 2:5 — "as lively stones, are built up"

## Trivia
- Babel's builders had brick for stone and slime for mortar - the plain of Shinar has no quarries.
- Egypt's bricks needed straw, and Pharaoh's cruelty was to withhold it while demanding the count.
- The New Testament never says brick: its builders use stone, lithos, and Peter calls believers living stones.
