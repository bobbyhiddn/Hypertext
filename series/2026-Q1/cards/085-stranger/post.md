## Word
**STRANGER** — One who dwells in a land that is not his own

## Old Testament
> Genesis 15:13 — "thy seed shall be a stranger in a land that is not theirs"

## New Testament
> Ephesians 2:19 — "Now therefore ye are no more strangers and foreigners"

## Trivia
- Abraham calls himself a stranger and a sojourner even while buying the field where he will be buried.
- Stephen quotes Genesis 15:13 word for word in his defence, and the Greek he uses is paroikos.
- The law's command to love the stranger is grounded in memory - ye were strangers yourselves.
