## Word
**TAKE** — To lay hold of and carry off

## Old Testament
> Genesis 2:15 — "the LORD God took the man, and put him into the garden"

## New Testament
> Matthew 26:26 — "Take, eat; this is my body"

## Trivia
- Genesis 5:24 ends with three words: God took him. Enoch is the one man of the era who does not die.
- The woman took of the fruit and did eat; the same verb carries both the taking in Eden and the taking of bread at the last supper.
- Lambano means both to take and to receive; John 1:12 says as many as received him.
