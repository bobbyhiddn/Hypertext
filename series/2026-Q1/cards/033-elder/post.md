## Word
**ELDER** — One honored with age and authority, seated in judgment at the gate

## Old Testament
> Ruth 4:2 — "ten men of the elders of the city"

## New Testament
> 1 Peter 5:1 — "the elders which are among you I exhort"

## Trivia
- Hebrew zaqen is kin to zaqan, the beard - seniority worn visibly.
- City gates were Israel's courtrooms; Boaz settled Ruth's case before ten elders there.
- Greek presbyteros is the root behind the English words priest and presbytery.
