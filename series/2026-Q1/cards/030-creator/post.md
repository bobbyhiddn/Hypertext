## Word
**CREATOR** — One who brings something into existence; specifically, God as the maker of the universe.

## Old Testament
> Isaiah 40:28 — "The Lord is the everlasting God, the Creator of the ends of the earth."

## New Testament
> 1 Peter 4:19 — "Entrust their souls to a faithful Creator in doing what is right."

## Trivia
- The Hebrew verb 'bara' is reserved solely for God's activity, implying only He can create from nothing.
- The Greek term 'ktistes' appears only once in the New Testament, portraying God as a faithful architect.
- Christ is identified as the agent of creation in the New Testament, through whom all things exist.
