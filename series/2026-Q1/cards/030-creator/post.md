## Word
**CREATOR** — One who brings something into existence; God as the originator of the universe.

## Old Testament
> Isaiah 40:28 — "The LORD is the everlasting God, the Creator of the ends of the earth."

## New Testament
> 1 Peter 4:19 — "Entrust their souls to a faithful Creator while doing good."

## Trivia
- The Hebrew verb 'bara' is used exclusively for God's creative acts.
- Creation 'ex nihilo' means creating out of nothing, not just shaping matter.
- New Testament theology identifies Jesus as the active agent of creation.
