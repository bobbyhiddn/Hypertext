## Word
**CREATOR** — One who brings into existence; God as the originator of all things.

## Old Testament
> Isaiah 40:28 — "The LORD is the everlasting God, the Creator of the ends of the earth."

## New Testament
> 1 Peter 4:19 — "Entrust their souls to a faithful Creator in doing what is right."

## Trivia
- The Hebrew verb 'bara' is used exclusively of God's creative activity, never of humans.
- The Greek noun 'Ktistes' appears only once in the New Testament (1 Peter 4:19).
- Biblical creation is 'ex nihilo' (out of nothing), distinguishing it from shaping pre-existing matter.
