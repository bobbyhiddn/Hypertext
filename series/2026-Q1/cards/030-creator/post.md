## Word
**CREATOR** — One who brings into existence; God as the originator and architect of the universe.

## Old Testament
> Isaiah 40:28 — "The Lord is the everlasting God, the Creator of the ends of the earth."

## New Testament
> 1 Peter 4:19 — "Commit the keeping of their souls to him in well doing, as unto a faithful Creator."

## Trivia
- The Hebrew verb 'bara' is used exclusively of God's creative activity, never of humans.
- The concept of 'creatio ex nihilo' (creation out of nothing) is unique to biblical cosmology.
- New Testament theology identifies Jesus as the agent through whom all things were made.
