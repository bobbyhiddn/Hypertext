## Word
**CREATOR** — One who brings into existence; the originator of all things, specifically God.

## Old Testament
> Isaiah 40:28 — "The LORD, the Creator of the ends of the earth, fainteth not, neither is weary."

## New Testament
> 1 Peter 4:19 — "Commit the keeping of their souls to him in well doing, as unto a faithful Creator."

## Trivia
- The Hebrew verb 'bara' is used exclusively with God as the subject.
- Creation 'ex nihilo' means God formed the universe out of nothing.
- Scripture identifies Jesus as the agent through whom all things were made.
