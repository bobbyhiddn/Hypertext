## Word
**CREATOR** — One who brings into existence; God as the originator of the universe.

## Old Testament
> Isaiah 40:28 — "The everlasting God, the LORD, the Creator of the ends of the earth, fainteth not."

## New Testament
> 1 Peter 4:19 — "Commit the keeping of their souls to him in well doing, as unto a faithful Creator."

## Trivia
- The Hebrew verb 'bara' is used exclusively for God's creative acts.
- Creation 'ex nihilo' (out of nothing) is a central biblical doctrine.
- The New Testament identifies Jesus as the active agent of creation.
