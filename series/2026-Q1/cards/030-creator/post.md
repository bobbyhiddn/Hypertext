## Word
**CREATOR** — One who brings something into existence; God as the originator of the universe.

## Old Testament
> Isaiah 40:28 — "The Lord is the everlasting God, the Creator of the ends of the earth."

## New Testament
> Colossians 1:16 — "For by him all things were created, in heaven and on earth, visible and invisible."

## Trivia
- The Hebrew verb 'bara' is used exclusively for God's creative acts, never for humans.
- New Testament scripture identifies Jesus as the agent through whom all things were created.
- 'Creatio ex nihilo' affirms God shaped the universe from no pre-existing matter.
