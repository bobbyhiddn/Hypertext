## Word
**CREATOR** — God as the originator of all things, who brings existence out of nothing.

## Old Testament
> Genesis 1:1 — "In the beginning God created the heavens and the earth."

## New Testament
> Colossians 1:16 — "For by him all things were created, in heaven and on earth, visible and invisible."

## Trivia
- The Hebrew verb 'bara' is used exclusively of God's creative activity, never of humans.
- The doctrine of creation 'ex nihilo' (out of nothing) affirms God's absolute sovereignty.
- The seven-day creation account establishes the pattern for the Sabbath rest.
