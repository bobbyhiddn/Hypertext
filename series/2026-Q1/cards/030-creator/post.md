## Word
**CREATOR** — One who brings into existence; the originator of the universe and life.

## Old Testament
> Isaiah 40:28 — "The LORD is the everlasting God, the Creator of the ends of the earth."

## New Testament
> 1 Peter 4:19 — "Let them that suffer according to the will of God commit the keeping of their souls to him... as unto a faithful Creator."

## Trivia
- The Hebrew verb 'bara' is used in the Bible exclusively with God as the subject.
- Creation 'ex nihilo' (out of nothing) distinguishes biblical cosmology from shaping existing matter.
- The New Testament identifies Jesus Christ as the agent of creation (Colossians 1:16).
