## Word
**CREATOR** — One who brings into existence; God as the originator of all things.

## Old Testament
> Isaiah 40:28 — "The everlasting God, the LORD, the Creator of the ends of the earth, fainteth not..."

## New Testament
> 1 Peter 4:19 — "...commit the keeping of their souls to him in well doing, as unto a faithful Creator."

## Trivia
- The Hebrew verb 'bara' is used exclusively of God's creative activity, never of humans.
- The concept of 'creatio ex nihilo' affirms that God created the universe out of nothing.
- The Greek noun 'Ktistēs' appears only once in the New Testament (1 Peter 4:19).
