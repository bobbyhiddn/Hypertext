## Word
**CREATOR** — One who brings existence from nothing; the originator of all things.

## Old Testament
> Isaiah 40:28 — "The everlasting God, the LORD, the Creator of the ends of the earth, fainteth not, neither is weary."

## New Testament
> 1 Peter 4:19 — "Let them that suffer according to the will of God commit the keeping of their souls to him in well doing, as unto a faithful Creator."

## Trivia
- The Hebrew verb 'bara' is used exclusively of God's activity, never of humans.
- Implies 'creatio ex nihilo'—creation out of nothing, without pre-existing material.
- New Testament theology identifies Jesus as the agent through whom all things were made.
