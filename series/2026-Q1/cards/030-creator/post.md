## Word
**CREATOR** — One who brings something into existence; specifically God as the originator of the universe.

## Old Testament
> Isaiah 40:28 — "The Lord is the everlasting God, the Creator of the ends of the earth."

## New Testament
> 1 Peter 4:19 — "Let those who suffer according to God's will entrust their souls to a faithful Creator."

## Trivia
- The Hebrew verb 'bara' is used exclusively for God's creative acts, never for humans.
- Colossians 1 identifies Jesus as the agent through whom all things were created.
- The doctrine of 'creatio ex nihilo' affirms God brought the universe into being from nothing.
