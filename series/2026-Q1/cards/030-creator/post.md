## Word
**CREATOR** — One who brings something into existence; specifically, God as the originator of the universe.

## Old Testament
> Genesis 1:1 — "In the beginning God created the heavens and the earth."

## New Testament
> Revelation 4:11 — "For you created all things, and by your will they existed and were created."

## Trivia
- The Hebrew verb 'bara' is used exclusively for God's activity, never for human making.
- Creation 'ex nihilo' (out of nothing) distinguishes biblical cosmology from ancient myths.
- New Testament theology identifies Jesus as the agent through whom all things were made.
