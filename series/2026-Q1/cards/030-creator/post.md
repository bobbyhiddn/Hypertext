## Word
**CREATOR** — One who brings into existence; the originator and sustainer of all things, distinct from creation.

## Old Testament
> Isaiah 40:28 — "The Lord is the everlasting God, the Creator of the ends of the earth. He does not faint or grow weary."

## New Testament
> Revelation 4:11 — "Worthy are you, our Lord and God... for you created all things, and by your will they existed and were created."

## Trivia
- The Hebrew verb 'bara' (create) is used in the Bible exclusively for God's activity, never for humans.
- The doctrine of 'creatio ex nihilo' affirms that God created the universe out of nothing, not pre-existing matter.
- In the New Testament, Jesus is identified as the agent of creation through whom all things were made.
