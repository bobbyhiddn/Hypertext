## Word
**CREATOR** — The originator who brings existence from non-existence; the architect of the cosmos.

## Old Testament
> Isaiah 40:28 — "The Lord is the everlasting God, the Creator of the ends of the earth."

## New Testament
> 1 Peter 4:19 — "So then, those who suffer according to God's will should commit themselves to their faithful Creator."

## Trivia
- The Hebrew verb 'bara' is used exclusively for God's creative activity, never for humans.
- New Testament theology identifies Jesus (the Word) as the active agent of creation.
- The concept of 'creatio ex nihilo' (creation out of nothing) distinguishes biblical cosmology.
