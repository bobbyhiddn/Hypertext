## Word
**CREATOR** — One who brings something into existence; specifically God as the originator of the universe.

## Old Testament
> Genesis 1:1 — "In the beginning, God created the heavens and the earth."

## New Testament
> Revelation 4:11 — "You created all things, and by your will they existed and were created."

## Trivia
- The Hebrew verb 'bara' is used exclusively for God's creative acts, never for human crafting.
- Creation 'ex nihilo' (out of nothing) distinguishes biblical cosmology from ancient myths.
- The New Testament identifies Jesus as the agent of creation, through whom all things were made.
