## Word
**CREATOR** — One who brings something into existence; specifically God as the maker of the universe.

## Old Testament
> Isaiah 40:28 — "The Lord is the everlasting God, the Creator of the ends of the earth."

## New Testament
> Colossians 1:16 — "For by him were all things created, that are in heaven, and that are in earth."

## Trivia
- The Hebrew verb 'bara' is used exclusively for God's creative acts, never for humans.
- New Testament writers identify Jesus as the agent through whom all things were created.
- 'Creatio ex nihilo' affirms that God brought the universe into existence out of nothing.
