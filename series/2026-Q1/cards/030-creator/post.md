## Word
**CREATOR** — One who brings into existence

## Old Testament
> Isaiah 40:28 — "The LORD is the everlasting God, the Creator of the ends of the earth."

## New Testament
> 1 Peter 4:19 — "Entrust their souls to a faithful Creator in doing what is right."

## Trivia
- Hebrew *bara* is used exclusively for God's acts, never for human making
- The theological term *creatio ex nihilo* means 'creation out of nothing'
- Romans 1 contrasts the Creator (*ktistes*) with the creation (*ktisis*)
