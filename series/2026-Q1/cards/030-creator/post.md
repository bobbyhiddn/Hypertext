## Word
**CREATOR** — One who brings into existence; God as the originator of all things

## Old Testament
> Isaiah 40:28 — "The LORD is the everlasting God, the Creator of the ends of the earth."

## New Testament
> 1 Peter 4:19 — "Entrust their souls to a faithful Creator while doing good."

## Trivia
- The Hebrew verb 'bara' is used exclusively for God's act of creation, never for human making.
- Creatio ex nihilo is the theological doctrine that God created the universe out of nothing.
- The New Testament identifies Jesus as the agent of creation, through whom all things were made.
