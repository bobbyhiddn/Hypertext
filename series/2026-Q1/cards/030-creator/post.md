## Word
**CREATOR** — One who brings into existence; the originator and sustainer of the universe.

## Old Testament
> Isaiah 40:28 — "The everlasting God, the Lord, the Creator of the ends of the earth, fainteth not, neither is weary."

## New Testament
> Romans 1:25 — "Who changed the truth of God into a lie, and worshipped and served the creature more than the Creator."

## Trivia
- The Hebrew verb 'bara' is used exclusively for God's activity; humans never 'bara'.
- The specific Greek noun 'Ktistes' appears only once in the New Testament (1 Peter 4:19).
- The doctrine of 'creatio ex nihilo' (creation out of nothing) is central to biblical theology.
