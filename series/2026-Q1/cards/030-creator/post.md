## Word
**CREATOR** — One who brings something into existence; the originator and sustainer of the universe.

## Old Testament
> Isaiah 40:28 — "The Creator of the ends of the earth, fainteth not, neither is weary."

## New Testament
> Revelation 4:11 — "For thou hast created all things, and for thy pleasure they are and were created."

## Trivia
- The Hebrew verb 'bara' is used exclusively for God's acts, never for human creation.
- Creation 'ex nihilo' affirms God formed the universe from nothing, not pre-existing matter.
- Colossians 1:16 identifies Jesus as the agent through whom 'all things were created.'
