## Word
**CREATOR** — One who brings into existence; the originator and sustainer of the universe.

## Old Testament
> Isaiah 40:28 — "The Lord is the everlasting God, the Creator of the ends of the earth."

## New Testament
> 1 Peter 4:19 — "Entrust their souls to a faithful Creator while doing good."

## Trivia
- The Hebrew verb 'bara' is used in the Bible exclusively with God as the subject.
- The Greek noun 'ktistēs' appears only once in the New Testament.
- Creation 'ex nihilo' means 'out of nothing,' a concept distinct to biblical theology.
