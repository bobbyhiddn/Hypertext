## Word
**CREATOR** — One who brings into existence; God as the originator and sustainer of all things.

## Old Testament
> Isaiah 40:28 — "The Creator of the ends of the earth, fainteth not, neither is weary."

## New Testament
> Romans 1:25 — "Who changed the truth of God into a lie, and worshipped and served the creature more than the Creator."

## Trivia
- The Hebrew verb 'bara' is used in scripture exclusively for God's creative activity.
- Colossians 1 identifies Jesus as the agent through whom all things were created.
- Creation 'ex nihilo' is the doctrine that God created the universe out of nothing.
