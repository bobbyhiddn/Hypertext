![ONE card](./outputs/card_1024x1536.png)

## Word
**ONE** — Single and undivided, as the whole earth was of one language

## Old Testament
> Genesis 11:6 — "Behold, the people is one, and they have all one language"

## New Testament
> John 17:21 — "That they all may be one"

## Trivia
- Genesis 11:1 says the whole earth was of one lip and one set of words - the Hebrew counts unity twice.
- Echad is the last word of the Shema, Hear O Israel, the LORD our God, the LORD is one.
- Jesus prays that they all may be one - Babel's unity restored by gift, not by tower.
