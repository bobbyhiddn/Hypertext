## Word
**HEIR** — The one to whom the estate passes

## Old Testament
> Genesis 15:4 — "he that shall come forth out of thine own bowels shall be thine heir"

## New Testament
> Hebrews 11:7 — "Noah ... became heir of the righteousness which is by faith"

## Trivia
- Abram's heir, before Isaac, was Eliezer of Damascus, a servant born in his house.
- Kleronomos joins kleros, a lot, to nemo, to hold - an heir is one who holds an allotted portion.
- Hebrews 11 names Noah heir of the righteousness which is by faith: the ark-builder inherits before Abraham does.
