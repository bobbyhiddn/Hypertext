## Word
**ABEL** — The second son of Adam and a shepherd, whose acceptable sacrifice incited his brother's envy and led to the first martyrdom.

## Old Testament
> Genesis 4:10 — "The voice of your brother’s blood cries out to Me from the ground."

## New Testament
> Hebrews 11:4 — "By faith Abel offered to God a more excellent sacrifice than Cain."

## Trivia
- His name means 'breath' or 'vapor,' symbolizing the brevity of his life.
- Jesus identified him as the first martyr in the history of the faithful.
- The description of his blood 'crying out' is the first mention of blood in the Bible.
