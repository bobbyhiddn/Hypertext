## Word
**ABEL** — Breath, vapor; the second son of Adam, a shepherd and first martyr.

## Old Testament
> Genesis 4:10 — "The voice of thy brother's blood crieth unto me from the ground."

## New Testament
> Hebrews 11:4 — "By faith Abel offered unto God a more excellent sacrifice than Cain."

## Trivia
- Name means 'breath' or 'vapor', symbolizing the brevity of life.
- The first martyr in the Bible; Jesus calls him 'righteous Abel'.
- His blood is described as 'crying out' from the ground for justice.
