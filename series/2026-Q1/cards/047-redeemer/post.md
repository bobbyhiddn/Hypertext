![REDEEMER card](./outputs/card_1024x1536.png)

## Word
**REDEEMER** — The kinsman who buys back what was lost and gathers his own

## Old Testament
> Job 19:25 — "I know that my redeemer liveth"

## New Testament
> Titus 2:14 — "that he might redeem us"

## Trivia
- Hebrew goel is the kinsman-redeemer who buys back a relative's land or liberty - Boaz for Ruth.
- Job 19:25 - I know that my redeemer liveth - is carved on countless gravestones.
- Greek lytroo comes from lytron, the ransom price paid to set a slave free.
