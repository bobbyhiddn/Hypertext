## Word
**FAITHFUL** — Steadfast in keeping covenant

## Old Testament
> Deuteronomy 7:9 — "the faithful God, which keepeth covenant"

## New Testament
> 1 Corinthians 1:9 — "God is faithful, by whom ye were called"

## Trivia
- Neeman is the passive of aman - to be made firm - the same root as amen and emunah, faith.
- Pistos means faithful and also believing; the faithful God and the faithful servant share one Greek word.
- After Babel's broken promise of a name, God made a covenant and kept it - faithfulness is the reply to the tower.
