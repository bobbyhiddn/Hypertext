## Word
**GREAT** — Large in magnitude, importance, or extent

## Old Testament
> Psalm 145:3 — "Great is the Lord, and greatly to be praised, and his greatness is unsearchable."

## New Testament
> Matthew 20:26 — "Whoever would be great among you must be your servant."

## Trivia
- In Greek, 'megas' is the root for English prefixes like 'mega-', denoting something of vast proportion.
- The Hebrew 'gadol' is used over 500 times in the Old Testament, often describing God's power or acts.
- Biblical greatness is frequently inverted by Jesus, who taught that the greatest must become a servant.
