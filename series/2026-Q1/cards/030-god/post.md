## Word
**GOD** — The Supreme Being, creator and ruler of the universe; the eternal and infinite One.

## Old Testament
> Genesis 1:1 — "In the beginning God created the heaven and the earth."

## New Testament
> John 1:1 — "In the beginning was the Word, and the Word was with God, and the Word was God."

## Trivia
- The Hebrew 'Elohim' is a plural noun used with singular verbs to denote supreme majesty.
- Derived from a Proto-Germanic root meaning 'that which is invoked' or 'poured out'.
- In the Greek New Testament, 'Theos' appears over 1,300 times, mostly referring to the Father.
