## Word
**GOD** — The supreme being, creator and ruler of the universe; the eternal and infinite One.

## Old Testament
> Genesis 1:1 — "In the beginning God created the heaven and the earth."

## New Testament
> John 1:1 — "In the beginning was the Word, and the Word was with God, and the Word was God."

## Trivia
- The Hebrew 'Elohim' is a plural noun used with singular verbs, denoting supreme majesty and sovereignty.
- The number 7 in the ability reflects the biblical number of divine perfection and completion.
- The English word derives from Proto-Germanic 'guthan', meaning 'that which is invoked' or 'poured out'.
