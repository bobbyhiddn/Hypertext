## Word
**GOD** — The supreme being, Creator, and ruler of the universe; the eternal and infinite Spirit.

## Old Testament
> Genesis 1:1 — "In the beginning God created the heavens and the earth."

## New Testament
> John 1:1 — "In the beginning was the Word, and the Word was with God, and the Word was God."

## Trivia
- The Hebrew 'Elohim' is plural but takes singular verbs to indicate majesty.
- Appears in the first verse of the Bible as the subject of creation.
- Used over 4,000 times in the Bible to refer to the one true Deity.
