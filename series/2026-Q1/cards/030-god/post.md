## Word
**GOD** — The Supreme Being, Creator, and Ruler of the universe; the eternal and infinite Spirit.

## Old Testament
> Genesis 1:1 — "In the beginning God created the heaven and the earth."

## New Testament
> John 1:1 — "In the beginning was the Word, and the Word was with God, and the Word was God."

## Trivia
- The Hebrew 'Elohim' is plural but takes singular verbs, hinting at the Trinity.
- Scripture defines God as Spirit, Light, and Love, possessing no physical form.
- This title appears in the first verse of Genesis and throughout Revelation.
