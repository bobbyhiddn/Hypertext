## Word
**GOD** — The Supreme Being, Creator, and Ruler of the universe; the one true deity.

## Old Testament
> Genesis 1:1 — "In the beginning God created the heaven and the earth."

## New Testament
> John 1:1 — "In the beginning was the Word, and the Word was with God, and the Word was God."

## Trivia
- The Hebrew 'Elohim' is grammatically plural but takes singular verbs when referring to the one true God.
- Greek 'Theos' was a common term for pagan deities before being redefined by New Testament writers.
- This title appears over 4,000 times in the Bible, distinct from the personal name 'Yahweh'.
