## Word
**GOD** — The Supreme Being, Creator, and Ruler of the universe; eternal, infinite, and all-powerful.

## Old Testament
> Genesis 1:1 — "In the beginning God created the heaven and the earth."

## New Testament
> John 1:1 — "In the beginning was the Word, and the Word was with God, and the Word was God."

## Trivia
- The Hebrew 'Elohim' is plural in form but takes singular verbs, emphasizing majesty.
- Used over 2,500 times in the OT, distinct from the personal name Yahweh.
- Encompasses the roles of Creator, Judge, and Savior in one supreme title.
