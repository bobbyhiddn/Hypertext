## Word
**GOD** — The Supreme Being, Creator, and Ruler of all things; eternal, infinite, and perfect in power, wisdom, and goodness.

## Old Testament
> Genesis 1:1 — "In the beginning God created the heaven and the earth."

## New Testament
> John 1:1 — "In the beginning was the Word, and the Word was with God, and the Word was God."

## Trivia
- The Hebrew 'Elohim' is a plural noun used with singular verbs, implying unity in plurality.
- The number 7, used in the ability, represents divine perfection and completion in scripture.
- This title appears over 2,500 times in the Old Testament and 1,300 in the New Testament.
