## Word
**GOD** — The supreme being, creator and ruler of all; the one true object of worship.

## Old Testament
> Genesis 1:1 — "In the beginning God created the heaven and the earth."

## New Testament
> John 1:1 — "In the beginning was the Word, and the Word was with God, and the Word was God."

## Trivia
- The Hebrew 'Elohim' is grammatically plural but takes a singular verb when referring to the Creator.
- The Greek 'Theos' was used by the Septuagint to translate both 'Elohim' and the divine name 'Yahweh'.
- This word appears in the first verse of the Bible as the subject of the act of creation.
