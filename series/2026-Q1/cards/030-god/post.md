## Word
**GOD** — The supreme being, creator and ruler of the universe; the eternal One.

## Old Testament
> Genesis 1:1 — "In the beginning God created the heaven and the earth."

## New Testament
> John 1:1 — "In the beginning was the Word, and the Word was with God, and the Word was God."

## Trivia
- Hebrew *Elohim* is grammatically plural but takes singular verbs.
- John 1:1 explicitly identifies Jesus (the Word) as *Theos*.
- Distinct from *Yahweh*, the personal covenant name of the Lord.
