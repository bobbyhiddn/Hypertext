## Word
**GOD** — The supreme being, creator, and ruler of the universe; the eternal and infinite Spirit.

## Old Testament
> Genesis 1:1 — "In the beginning God created the heaven and the earth."

## New Testament
> John 1:1 — "In the beginning was the Word, and the Word was with God, and the Word was God."

## Trivia
- The Hebrew 'Elohim' is grammatically plural, often interpreted as a 'plural of majesty' or hinting at the Trinity.
- Scripture uses anthropomorphic terms like 'hand' or 'eye' for God, yet defines Him as an invisible, immortal Spirit.
- The divine name YHWH appears over 6,000 times but was considered too holy to speak, often replaced by 'Adonai'.
