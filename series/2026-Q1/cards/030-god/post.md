## Word
**GOD** — The supreme being, creator, and ruler of all; the object of faith and worship.

## Old Testament
> Genesis 1:1 — "In the beginning God created the heavens and the earth."

## New Testament
> John 1:1 — "In the beginning was the Word, and the Word was with God, and the Word was God."

## Trivia
- Hebrew 'Elohim' is grammatically plural but takes singular verbs.
- Greek 'Theos' was standard for deities but redefined by the apostles.
- God is the first subject of the Bible, appearing in the very first verse.
