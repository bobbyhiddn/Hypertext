## Word
**GOD** — The Supreme Being, Creator, and Ruler of the universe; the eternal and infinite source of all.

## Old Testament
> Genesis 1:1 — "In the beginning God created the heaven and the earth."

## New Testament
> John 1:1 — "In the beginning was the Word, and the Word was with God, and the Word was God."

## Trivia
- The Hebrew 'Elohim' is plural in form but takes singular verbs, hinting at the Trinity.
- Capitalized 'God' distinguishes the one true Creator from false deities.
- Appears over 4,000 times in the Bible, revealing His nature as holy, just, and loving.
