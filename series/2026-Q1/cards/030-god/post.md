## Word
**GOD** — The Supreme Being; the Creator, Ruler, and Sustainer of the universe.

## Old Testament
> Genesis 1:1 — "In the beginning God created the heaven and the earth."

## New Testament
> John 1:1 — "In the beginning was the Word, and the Word was with God, and the Word was God."

## Trivia
- The Hebrew 'Elohim' is grammatically plural, often interpreted as denoting absolute majesty or the Trinity.
- The personal name YHWH was considered too holy to pronounce, so readers substituted 'Adonai' (Lord).
- Jesus introduced the intimate Aramaic term 'Abba' (Father) for God, marking a shift in relational theology.
