## Word
**GOD** — The Supreme Being, Creator, and Ruler of all things; the one eternal and infinite Spirit.

## Old Testament
> Genesis 1:1 — "In the beginning God created the heaven and the earth."

## New Testament
> John 1:1 — "In the beginning was the Word... and the Word was God."

## Trivia
- The Hebrew 'Elohim' is a plural noun used with singular verbs to denote majesty.
- The Bible never argues for God's existence; it assumes it from the very first verse.
- Capitalized 'God' usually translates Elohim/Theos, while 'LORD' translates YHWH.
