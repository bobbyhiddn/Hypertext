## Word
**GOD** — The supreme being, creator, and ruler of all; the object of faith and worship.

## Old Testament
> Genesis 1:1 — "In the beginning God created the heavens and the earth."

## New Testament
> John 1:1 — "In the beginning was the Word... and the Word was God."

## Trivia
- The Hebrew 'Elohim' is grammatically plural but takes singular verbs, emphasizing majesty.
- NT writers used the Greek 'Theos' to bridge Jewish monotheism with Gentile thought.
- In English Bibles, 'God' usually translates Elohim, while 'LORD' translates YHWH.
