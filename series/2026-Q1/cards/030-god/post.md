## Word
**GOD** — The Supreme Being, Creator and Ruler of the universe; the source of all life and authority.

## Old Testament
> Genesis 1:1 — "In the beginning God created the heaven and the earth."

## New Testament
> 1 John 1:5 — "God is light, and in him is no darkness at all."

## Trivia
- The Hebrew 'Elohim' is plural but takes singular verbs, hinting at the Trinity.
- The Greek 'Theos' appears over 1,300 times in the New Testament.
- This is a title of supreme power, distinct from the personal name YHWH.
