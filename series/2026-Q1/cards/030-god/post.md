## Word
**GOD** — The supreme being, creator and ruler of the universe; the eternal and infinite Spirit.

## Old Testament
> Genesis 1:1 — "In the beginning God created the heaven and the earth."

## New Testament
> John 4:24 — "God is a Spirit: and they that worship him must worship him in spirit and in truth."

## Trivia
- "Elohim" is grammatically plural but takes singular verbs, emphasizing majesty over multiplicity.
- The Bible gives no physical description of God, only describing His glory, light, and throne.
- NT writers repurposed the Greek "Theos"—used for pagan deities—to describe the One True God.
