## Word
**GOD** — The supreme being, creator, and ruler of the universe; the eternal, infinite, and unchangeable Spirit.

## Old Testament
> Genesis 1:1 — "In the beginning God created the heaven and the earth."

## New Testament
> John 1:1 — "In the beginning was the Word, and the Word was with God, and the Word was God."

## Trivia
- Hebrew 'Elohim' is grammatically plural but takes singular verbs, implying majesty or tri-unity.
- Greek 'Theos' was a common term for pagan deities before being redefined by NT writers.
- In the KJV, 'GOD' (all caps) translates YHWH only when preceded by 'Lord' (Adonai).
