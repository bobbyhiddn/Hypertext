## Word
**GOD** — The Supreme Being, Creator, and Ruler of all things; the eternal and infinite Spirit.

## Old Testament
> Genesis 1:1 — "In the beginning God created the heaven and the earth."

## New Testament
> 1 John 4:8 — "He that loveth not knoweth not God; for God is love."

## Trivia
- The Hebrew 'Elohim' is grammatically plural but takes a singular verb, emphasizing majesty.
- The Greek 'Theos' bridges Jewish monotheism with the language of the New Testament.
- Scripture defines God by His essence: Spirit (John 4:24), Light (1 John 1:5), and Love (1 John 4:8).
