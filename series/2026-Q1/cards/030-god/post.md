## Word
**GOD** — The Supreme Being, Creator, and Ruler of all; the eternal, infinite, and unchangeable Spirit.

## Old Testament
> Genesis 1:1 — "In the beginning God created the heaven and the earth."

## New Testament
> John 1:1 — "In the beginning was the Word, and the Word was with God, and the Word was God."

## Trivia
- The Hebrew 'Elohim' is plural in form but takes singular verbs, emphasizing majesty and unity.
- The Greek 'Theos' was repurposed by New Testament writers to distinguish the One True God from pagan deities.
- This title implies the attributes of omniscience (all-knowing), omnipotence (all-powerful), and omnipresence (everywhere).
