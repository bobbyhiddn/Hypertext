## Word
**GOD** — The Supreme Being, Creator and Ruler of all; the self-existent One who is infinite in power and perfection.

## Old Testament
> Genesis 1:1 — "In the beginning God created the heaven and the earth."

## New Testament
> John 4:24 — "God is a Spirit: and they that worship him must worship him in spirit and in truth."

## Trivia
- The Hebrew 'Elohim' is plural in form but takes a singular verb, hinting at the Trinity.
- The Bible does not argue for God's existence but asserts it as the foundational truth.
- As a TITLE card, this acts as a Wild and can substitute for any NOUN or NAME in a record.
