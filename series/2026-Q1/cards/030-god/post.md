## Word
**GOD** — The Supreme Being, Creator, and Ruler of the universe; the eternal and infinite One who is the source of all existence.

## Old Testament
> Genesis 1:1 — "In the beginning God created the heaven and the earth."

## New Testament
> Revelation 22:13 — "I am Alpha and Omega, the beginning and the end, the first and the last."

## Trivia
- Hebrew 'Elohim' uses a plural ending with singular verbs, often called the 'plural of majesty'.
- The Tetragrammaton YHWH appears over 6,800 times in the OT but was considered too holy to pronounce.
- Greek 'Theos' was repurposed from polytheistic vocabulary to exclusively denote the one true Creator.
