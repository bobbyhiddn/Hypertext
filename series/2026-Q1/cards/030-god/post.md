## Word
**GOD** — The eternal, supreme Creator and Ruler of the universe; the source of all existence.

## Old Testament
> Genesis 1:1 — "In the beginning God created the heaven and the earth."

## New Testament
> John 1:1 — "In the beginning was the Word, and the Word was with God, and the Word was God."

## Trivia
- Elohim is a plural noun governing singular verbs, suggesting supreme majesty rather than numerical plurality.
- The Greek Theos bridges Hebrew monotheism with the philosophical concept of the Ultimate Reality.
- While Elohim refers to the Creator's power, the name Yahweh (LORD) signifies His covenant relationship.
