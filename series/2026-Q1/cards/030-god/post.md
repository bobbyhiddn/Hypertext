## Word
**GOD** — The eternal, infinite Creator and Sustainer of the universe; the Supreme Being.

## Old Testament
> Genesis 1:1 — "In the beginning God created the heaven and the earth."

## New Testament
> John 1:1 — "In the beginning was the Word, and the Word was with God, and the Word was God."

## Trivia
- The Hebrew 'Elohim' is grammatically plural but takes singular verbs, emphasizing majesty and unity.
- Scripture does not argue for God's existence but assumes it as the foundational truth of reality.
- The number 7 in the ability reflects the biblical number of completion and the days of creation.
