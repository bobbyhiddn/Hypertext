## Word
**NAKED** — Exposed, vulnerable, or stripped of covering; lacking defense or concealment.

## Old Testament
> Job 1:21 — "Naked I came from my mother’s womb, and naked I will depart."

## New Testament
> Hebrews 4:13 — "Nothing in all creation is hidden... everything is naked and exposed before his eyes."

## Trivia
- Isaiah walked stripped of his sackcloth for three years as a sign to Egypt.
- Peter was fishing 'naked' (stripped for work) before swimming to Jesus.
- The Greek 'gymnos' often implies being poorly clad rather than fully nude.
