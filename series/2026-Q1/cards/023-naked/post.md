## Word
**NAKED** — Exposed; having no covering; vulnerable to sight or judgment.

## Old Testament
> Job 1:21 — "Naked I came from my mother’s womb, and naked shall I return."

## New Testament
> Hebrews 4:13 — "All things are naked and open to the eyes of Him to whom we must give account."

## Trivia
- Hebrew 'arowm derives from a root meaning 'to be exposed' or 'bare'.
- Biblical nakedness often symbolizes vulnerability, guilt, or a lack of spiritual covering.
- In John 21, Peter was 'naked' (gymnos) meaning he had stripped for work, not fully nude.
