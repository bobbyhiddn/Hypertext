## Word
**NAKED** — Exposed; without covering, defense, or disguise; striped of outer garments.

## Old Testament
> Genesis 2:25 — "And they were both naked, the man and his wife, and were not ashamed."

## New Testament
> Hebrews 4:13 — "And there is no creature hidden from His sight, but all things are naked and open to the eyes of Him to whom we must give account."

## Trivia
- The Greek word 'gymnos' is the root of 'gymnasium,' as ancient athletes trained and competed without clothing.
- In biblical context, being 'naked' often meant wearing only a tunic without an outer cloak, rather than total nudity.
- The prophet Isaiah walked 'naked and barefoot' for three years as a dramatic sign of Egypt's impending captivity.
