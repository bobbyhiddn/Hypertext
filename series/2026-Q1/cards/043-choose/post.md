## Word
**CHOOSE** — To pick out from among many and set apart for a purpose

## Old Testament
> Deuteronomy 7:6 — "the LORD thy God hath chosen thee"

## New Testament
> John 15:16 — "I have chosen you"

## Trivia
- Hebrew bachar is the covenant verb of election - Israel was chosen not for its size but for love.
- Greek eklegomai gives English eclectic - picked out from among many.
- Isaiah names the chosen a remnant that returns - the few kept through the scattering.
