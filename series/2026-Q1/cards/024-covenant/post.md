## Word
**COVENANT** — A binding agreement, compact, or treaty; in Scripture, a relationship established by God with His people, often sealed by oath or blood.

## Old Testament
> Jeremiah 31:31 — "Behold, the days are coming... when I will make a new covenant with the house of Israel."

## New Testament
> Luke 22:20 — "This cup that is poured out for you is the new covenant in my blood."

## Trivia
- Hebrew 'berit' implies 'cutting,' referring to the ancient ritual of walking between severed animals.
- Greek 'diatheke' means 'testament,' emphasizing God's unilateral promise rather than a negotiated contract.
- The Bible is structured around five major covenants: Noahic, Abrahamic, Mosaic, Davidic, and the New Covenant.
