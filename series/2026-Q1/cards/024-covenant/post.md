## Word
**COVENANT** — A bond cut between two parties

## Old Testament
> Genesis 9:13 — "I do set my bow in the cloud, and it shall be for a token of a covenant between me and the earth"

## New Testament
> Luke 22:20 — "This cup is the new testament in my blood"

## Trivia
- The first covenant named in Scripture is with Noah, and the second is made with every living creature, not with people alone.
- Hebrew cuts a covenant; Genesis 15 shows why, with the halves laid out and a flame passing between them.
- Diatheke is the ordinary Greek word for a will, which is why Hebrews 9 can argue from the death of the one who made it.
