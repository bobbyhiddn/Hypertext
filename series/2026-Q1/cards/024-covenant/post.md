## Word
**COVENANT** — A solemn, binding agreement; a divine pledge of relationship and faithfulness.

## Old Testament
> Jeremiah 31:31 — "Behold, the days are coming... when I will make a new covenant with the house of Israel."

## New Testament
> Luke 22:20 — "This cup is the new covenant in my blood, which is poured out for you."

## Trivia
- The Hebrew 'berit' implies 'cutting,' referencing the ancient ritual of walking between halved animals.
- Greek 'diathēkē' was chosen over 'synthēkē' to imply a divine ordinance rather than a mutual contract.
- The Bible outlines five major covenants: Noahic, Abrahamic, Mosaic, Davidic, and the New Covenant.
