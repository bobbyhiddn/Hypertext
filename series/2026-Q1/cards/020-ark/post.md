## Word
**ARK** — A vessel built for preservation; a sacred chest housing the presence of God.

## Old Testament
> Genesis 6:14 — "Make for yourself an ark of gopher wood; you shall make the ark with rooms..."

## New Testament
> Hebrews 11:7 — "By faith Noah... prepared an ark for the salvation of his household."

## Trivia
- The Hebrew word 'tebah' is used only for Noah's ship and baby Moses' basket.
- The Ark of the Covenant uses a different word, 'aron', meaning chest or coffin.
- Noah's Ark used a 6:1 length-to-width ratio, a standard for stability in modern ships.
