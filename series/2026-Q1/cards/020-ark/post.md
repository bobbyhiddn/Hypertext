## Word
**ARK** — A chest, vessel, or sanctuary used for preservation and safety during judgment.

## Old Testament
> Genesis 6:14 — "Make yourself an ark of gopher wood; make rooms in the ark, and cover it inside and out with pitch."

## New Testament
> Hebrews 11:7 — "By faith Noah, being divinely warned of things not yet seen, moved with godly fear, prepared an ark for the saving of his household..."

## Trivia
- The Hebrew word 'tebah' is used only for Noah's ark and the basket that carried baby Moses.
- Noah's ark was approximately 450 feet long, with a capacity equal to 500 railroad stock cars.
- The Ark of the Covenant contained the stone tablets, a pot of manna, and Aaron's rod.
