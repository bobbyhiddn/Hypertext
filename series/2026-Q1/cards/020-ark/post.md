## Word
**ARK** — The vessel built to carry life through the flood

## Old Testament
> Genesis 6:14 — "Make thee an ark of gopher wood ... and shalt pitch it within and without with pitch"

## New Testament
> Hebrews 11:7 — "prepared an ark to the saving of his house"

## Trivia
- The same word carries Moses down the Nile in a basket of bulrushes daubed with pitch - two rescues by water in one vocabulary.
- The ark has no rudder, no sail and no oars; it is described as a floating chest, and the LORD shuts the door.
- Greek uses kibotos for Noah's ark and for the ark of the covenant alike; Hebrew keeps them apart as tebah and aron.
