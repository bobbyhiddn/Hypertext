## Word
**DEPART** — To pull up stakes and leave the plain behind

## Old Testament
> Genesis 11:2 — "as they journeyed from the east"

## New Testament
> Matthew 2:12 — "they departed into their own country another way"

## Trivia
- Nasa is literally to pull up the pegs - a camp departs the way a tent comes down.
- The same root gives massa, a stage of the journey in Numbers 33 and, by another sense, a burden.
- Anachoreo is the verb behind anchorite, the hermit who departs from the world.
