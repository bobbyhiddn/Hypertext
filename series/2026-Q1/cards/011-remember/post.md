## Word
**REMEMBER** — To recall to mind; to retain in memory and act upon it.

## Old Testament
> Exodus 20:8 — "Remember the Sabbath day, to keep it holy."

## New Testament
> Luke 23:42 — "Jesus, remember me when you come into your kingdom."

## Trivia
- The Hebrew 'zakar' implies acting on a memory, not just mental recall.
- When God 'remembers' in Scripture, it often signals the start of a divine intervention.
- Malachi 3:16 mentions a 'book of remembrance' written for those who fear the Lord.
