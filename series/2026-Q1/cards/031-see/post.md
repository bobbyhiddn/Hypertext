## Word
**SEE** — To perceive with the eyes; to understand, witness, or have insight.

## Old Testament
> Psalm 34:8 — "Oh, taste and see that the LORD is good! Blessed is the man who takes refuge in him!"

## New Testament
> John 9:25 — "One thing I do know, that though I was blind, now I see."

## Trivia
- The Hebrew verb ra'ah (to see) is the root of Jehovah Jireh, literally meaning 'The LORD will see to it.'
- Jesus healing the blind is the most frequently recorded category of miracle in the Gospels.
- Hagar named God El Roi ('The God Who Sees') after He sustained her in the wilderness.
