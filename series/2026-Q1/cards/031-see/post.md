## Word
**SEE** — To perceive with the eyes; to inspect, experience, or understand spiritually.

## Old Testament
> Genesis 16:13 — "She gave this name to the Lord who spoke to her: 'You are the God who sees me.'"

## New Testament
> John 9:25 — "One thing I do know. I was blind but now I see!"

## Trivia
- The Hebrew word 'ra'ah' implies not just physical sight but perceiving, understanding, and providing.
- Hagar is the only person in Scripture to name God, calling Him 'El Roi' (The God Who Sees).
- In the Gospels, physical blindness is frequently used to illustrate a lack of spiritual insight.
