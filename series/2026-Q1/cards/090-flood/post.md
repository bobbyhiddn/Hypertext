## Word
**FLOOD** — The waters that came upon the earth and took them all away

## Old Testament
> Genesis 7:17 — "And the flood was forty days upon the earth"

## New Testament
> Matthew 24:39 — "knew not until the flood came, and took them all away"

## Trivia
- Hebrew keeps a separate word for this water: mabbul is never used of an ordinary river in flood.
- Psalm 29:10 is the one place outside Genesis that uses it - the LORD sitteth upon the flood, still enthroned over it.
- Peter reads the flood as a figure of baptism: the same water that destroys the world carries the ark.
