## Word
**ENOCH** — The seventh from Adam, who walked with God and was taken without death

## Old Testament
> Genesis 5:24 — "Enoch walked with God: and he was not; for God took him"

## New Testament
> Hebrews 11:5 — "By faith Enoch was translated"

## Trivia
- Enoch lived 365 years - a year of years - and then was not, for God took him.
- Jude quotes a prophecy of Enoch, and the Ethiopian church still keeps the Book of Enoch as Scripture.
- Hebrews says Enoch was translated - carried across - the same Greek verb used for moving a body or a law.
