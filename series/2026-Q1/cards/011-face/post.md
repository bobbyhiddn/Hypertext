## Word
**FACE** — The front part of the head; presence, surface, or appearance.

## Old Testament
> Psalm 27:8 — "Seek ye my face; my heart said unto thee, Thy face, Lord, will I seek."

## New Testament
> 1 Corinthians 13:12 — "For now we see through a glass, darkly; but then face to face."

## Trivia
- The Hebrew word 'panim' is grammatically plural, suggesting the many expressions of a person.
- The 'Bread of the Presence' in the tabernacle is literally the 'Bread of the Face'.
- Seeing God's face was often feared as fatal, yet implies the ultimate intimacy of the beatific vision.
