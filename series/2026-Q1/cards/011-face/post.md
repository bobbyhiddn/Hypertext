## Word
**FACE** — The front part of the head; presence, countenance, or surface.

## Old Testament
> Numbers 6:25 — "The LORD make his face shine upon thee, and be gracious unto thee..."

## New Testament
> 1 Corinthians 13:12 — "For now we see through a glass, darkly; but then face to face..."

## Trivia
- The Hebrew word 'panim' is grammatically plural, suggesting the complexity of presence.
- To 'hide one's face' was a cultural idiom for withdrawing favor, support, or attention.
- The 'Bread of the Presence' in the temple is literally 'Bread of the Face' (Lechem haPanim).
