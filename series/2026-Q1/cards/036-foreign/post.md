## Word
**FOREIGN** — Of another people, land, or tongue; strange to the hearer

## Old Testament
> Psalm 137:4 — "the LORD's song in a strange land"

## New Testament
> Ephesians 2:19 — "no more strangers and foreigners"

## Trivia
- Hebrew nokri covers foreign lands, foreign gods, and the stranger at the gate alike.
- Psalm 137 asks how to sing the LORD's song in a strange land - the exiles' question in Babylon.
- Greek xenos gives English both xenophobia and xenia, the old duty of welcoming strangers.
