## Word
**ENOCH** — Dedicated; initiated. A patriarch who walked closely with God and was taken into heaven without dying.

## Old Testament
> Genesis 5:24 — "And Enoch walked with God: and he was not; for God took him."

## New Testament
> Hebrews 11:5 — "By faith Enoch was translated that he should not see death..."

## Trivia
- Enoch is the 'seventh from Adam' and father of Methuselah, the longest-lived man.
- He lived 365 years—matching the days of the solar year—before God took him.
- Along with Elijah, he is one of only two biblical figures who did not die.
