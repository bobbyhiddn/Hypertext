## Word
**ENOCH** — Dedicated; the patriarch who walked with God and was taken up without dying

## Old Testament
> Genesis 5:24 — "Enoch walked faithfully with God; then he was no more, because God took him."

## New Testament
> Hebrews 11:5 — "By faith Enoch was taken from this life, so that he did not experience death."

## Trivia
- One of only two biblical figures taken to heaven without dying
- The Epistle of Jude quotes a prophecy directly from the Book of Enoch
- Father of Methuselah, the longest-lived man in the Bible
