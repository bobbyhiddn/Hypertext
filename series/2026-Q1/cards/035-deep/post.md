## Word
**DEEP** — Reaching far beneath the surface

## Old Testament
> Proverbs 20:5 — "Counsel in the heart of man is like deep water; but a man of understanding will draw it out"

## New Testament
> John 4:11 — "the well is deep: from whence then hast thou that living water?"

## Trivia
- Hebrew keeps two depths apart: amoq for a well or a thought, tehom for the deep that covered the earth.
- Jacob's well at Sychar, the one the woman calls deep, is still over thirty metres to the water.
- Luke calls the dawn of the resurrection deep morning, using the same adjective as the well.
