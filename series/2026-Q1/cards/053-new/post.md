## Word
**NEW** — A new tongue, a new heart, a new people

## Old Testament
> Ezekiel 36:26 — "A new heart also will I give you, and a new spirit"

## New Testament
> Mark 16:17 — "they shall speak with new tongues"

## Trivia
- Greek has two words for new: neos means recent, kainos means unprecedented in kind; the new tongues and the new covenant are kainos.
- Babel confused one tongue into many; at Pentecost many tongues carried one message - a new thing, not a repaired old one.
- Chadash also names the new moon, chodesh - the month begins when the light is made new.
