## Word
**EARTH** — The dry land, ground, or soil; the material world distinct from the heavens.

## Old Testament
> Genesis 1:1 — "In the beginning God created the heaven and the earth."

## New Testament
> Matthew 5:5 — "Blessed are the meek: for they shall inherit the earth."

## Trivia
- The Hebrew word 'eretz' appears over 2,500 times in the Old Testament.
- Adam's name is derived from 'adamah', meaning ground or earth.
- Ancient cosmology often depicted the earth as a foundation set upon pillars.
