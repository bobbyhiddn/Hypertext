## Word
**SETH** — Appointed / Compensation

## Old Testament
> Genesis 4:25 — "God hath appointed me another seed instead of Abel, whom Cain slew."

## New Testament
> Luke 3:38 — "Which was the son of Enos, which was the son of Seth, which was the son of Adam."

## Trivia
- Name means 'appointed' or 'compensation' for the loss of Abel.
- Lived 912 years and became the ancestor of Noah and Jesus.
- Born when Adam was 130, expressly in his father's likeness.
