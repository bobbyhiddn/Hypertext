![SETH card](./outputs/card_1024x1536.png)

## Word
**SETH** — Appointed; the seed set in Abel's place

## Old Testament
> Genesis 4:25 — "she called his name Seth: For God, said she, hath appointed me another seed"

## New Testament
> Luke 3:38 — "which was the son of Seth, which was the son of Adam"

## Trivia
- Genesis 5:3 says Adam begat a son in his own likeness, after his image - the words of Genesis 1 handed down one generation.
- In the days of Seth's son Enos men began to call upon the name of the LORD.
- Luke's genealogy runs backward through Seth to Adam, and only there stops at the son of God.
