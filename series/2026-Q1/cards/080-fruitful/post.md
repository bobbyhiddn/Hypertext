## Word
**FRUITFUL** — Bearing much, as the first blessing on living things

## Old Testament
> Genesis 1:28 — "Be fruitful, and multiply, and replenish the earth"

## New Testament
> Colossians 1:10 — "being fruitful in every good work, and increasing in the knowledge of God"

## Trivia
- The blessing be fruitful is spoken over the fish and birds before it is spoken over the man.
- After the flood God repeats the blessing to Noah word for word; the world restarts on the same terms.
- Ephraim's name is built on this verb - God hath caused me to be fruitful in the land of my affliction.
