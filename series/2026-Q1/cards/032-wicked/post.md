## Word
**WICKED** — evil, morally corrupt, malicious

## Old Testament
> Psalm 1:4 — "The wicked are not so, but are like chaff that the wind drives away."

## New Testament
> Matthew 13:49 — "The angels will come and separate the wicked from the righteous."

## Trivia
- The Hebrew word 'rasha' implies active rebellion and hostility toward God, not just passive wrongdoing.
- In Psalm 1, the wicked are compared to chaff, weightless and easily blown away by the wind.
- The Greek 'ponēros' emphasizes evil that causes labor, pain, and sorrow, often linked to Satan.
