## Word
**WICKED** — Morally bad or evil; guilty and unrighteous

## Old Testament
> Psalm 1:6 — "But the way of the wicked will perish."

## New Testament
> Matthew 13:49 — "Sever the wicked from among the just,"

## Trivia
- The Hebrew word 'rasha' emphasizes someone who is guilty in a legal or moral sense, often contrasted with the righteous.
- In Greek, 'ponēros' describes an active, aggressive evil that opposes what is good, not merely a passive badness.
- Wisdom literature, particularly Psalms and Proverbs, frequently contrasts the path of the wicked with the way of the righteous.
