## Word
**WICKED** — Morally corrupt, evil, guilty, opposed to God's law.

## Old Testament
> Psalm 1:4 — "The wicked are not so, but are like chaff that the wind drives away."

## New Testament
> 2 Thessalonians 3:2 — "and that we may be delivered from wicked and evil men. For not all have faith."

## Trivia
- The Hebrew word 'rasha' spans legal and theological dimensions, describing those who violate covenant obligations.
- In Psalm 1, the wicked are contrasted with the righteous and compared to weightless chaff driven away by the wind.
- The Greek word 'poneros' is frequently used in the New Testament to describe the 'evil one' and active moral corruption.
