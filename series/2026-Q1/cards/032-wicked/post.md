## Word
**WICKED** — Morally bad in principle or practice; guilty of sin or crime.

## Old Testament
> Psalm 1:4 — "The wicked are not so, but are like chaff that the wind drives away."

## New Testament
> Matthew 13:49 — "The angels will come out and separate the wicked from the righteous."

## Trivia
- In Hebrew, 'rasha' often carries a legal sense of being guilty or in the wrong before a judge.
- The Greek 'ponēros' emphasizes active, harmful evil that intentionally corrupts others, rather than just a lack of goodness.
- Biblical poetry frequently contrasts the wicked with the righteous, comparing their paths to darkness and light.
