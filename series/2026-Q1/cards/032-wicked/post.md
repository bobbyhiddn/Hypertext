## Word
**WICKED** — Morally bad, evil, guilty, hostile to God

## Old Testament
> Psalm 1:4 — "The wicked are not so, but are like chaff that the wind drives away."

## New Testament
> Matthew 18:32 — "Then his master summoned him and said to him, 'You wicked servant! I forgave you all that debt because you pleaded with me.'"

## Trivia
- The Hebrew 'rasha' describes one who is guilty of a crime or lives in defiance of God's order.
- The Greek 'ponēros' emphasizes the painful, laborious, and destructive nature of evil.
- Wisdom literature frequently contrasts the fleeting success of the wicked with the enduring root of the righteous.
