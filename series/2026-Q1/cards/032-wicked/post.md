## Word
**WICKED** — Morally wrong, evil, or actively hostile toward God and His righteous design.

## Old Testament
> Proverbs 28:1 — "The wicked flee when no one pursues, but the righteous are bold as a lion."

## New Testament
> Ephesians 6:16 — "...taking the shield of faith with which you will be able to quench all the fiery darts of the wicked one."

## Trivia
- The Hebrew 'rasha' describes one who is guilty of a crime or actively hostile toward God's order.
- In Greek, 'ponēros' denotes a malignant kind of evil that is actively injurious and destructive.
- Proverbs frequently contrasts the wicked with the righteous, highlighting their instability and ultimate downfall.
