## Word
**WICKED** — Morally wrong, actively harmful, or guilty before the law.

## Old Testament
> Psalm 1:4 — "Not so the wicked! They are like chaff that the wind blows away."

## New Testament
> Matthew 13:49 — "The angels will come and separate the wicked from the righteous."

## Trivia
- In Hebrew, 'rasha' often carries a legal sense of being guilty or in the wrong, not just morally bad.
- The Book of Proverbs frequently uses poetic parallelism to directly contrast the 'wicked' with the 'righteous'.
- The Greek word 'ponēros' implies an active, corrupting, and harmful evil, rather than a mere lack of goodness.
