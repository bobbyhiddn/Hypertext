## Word
**WICKED** — Morally corrupt, evil in principle or practice, guilty of sin.

## Old Testament
> Psalm 1:4 — "The wicked are not so, but are like chaff that the wind drives away."

## New Testament
> Matthew 13:49 — "The angels will come out and separate the evil from the righteous."

## Trivia
- The Hebrew 'rasha' often carries a legal sense of being guilty or in the wrong.
- In Psalms, 'the wicked' frequently appear as the primary antagonists of the righteous.
- The Greek 'ponēros' implies an active, harmful, and corrupting kind of evil.
