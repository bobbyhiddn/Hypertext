## Word
**WICKED** — Morally bad, guilty, or actively hostile to God.

## Old Testament
> Proverbs 15:29 — "The LORD is far from the wicked, but he hears the prayer of the righteous."

## New Testament
> Matthew 13:49 — "The angels will come forth, and sever the wicked from among the just."

## Trivia
- The Hebrew word 'rasha' often carries a legal sense of being guilty or condemned before a judge.
- In Greek, 'ponēros' emphasizes the active, destructive nature of evil, whereas 'kakos' often refers to intrinsic worthlessness.
- Wisdom literature frequently contrasts the wicked with the righteous to highlight their fundamentally different paths and ends.
