## Word
**WICKED** — morally wrong, actively bad, guilty

## Old Testament
> Psalm 1:4 — "The wicked are not so, but are like chaff that the wind drives away."

## New Testament
> Matthew 18:32 — "Then his master summoned him and said to him, 'You wicked servant!'"

## Trivia
- The Hebrew word 'rasha' is often used as a legal term meaning 'guilty' or 'in the wrong'.
- In Greek, 'ponēros' emphasizes an active, malignant evil that seeks to corrupt others.
- The Book of Proverbs frequently contrasts the destructive path of the wicked with the way of the righteous.
