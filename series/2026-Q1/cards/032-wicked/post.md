## Word
**WICKED** — Actively evil or morally wrong; guilty of hostility toward God; injurious and destructive.

## Old Testament
> Psalm 1:4 — "The wicked are not so, but are like chaff that the wind drives away."

## New Testament
> Matthew 18:32 — "Then his master summoned him and said to him, 'You wicked servant! I forgave you all that debt because you pleaded with me.'"

## Trivia
- The Hebrew 'rasha' describes someone who is actively guilty of a crime or hostility toward God.
- The Greek 'ponēros' emphasizes a malignant, spreading evil that causes pain and destruction.
- Throughout biblical wisdom literature, the wicked are frequently contrasted with the righteous.
