## Word
**WICKED** — Evil, malicious, guilty, or morally wrong.

## Old Testament
> Psalm 1:4 — "The wicked are not so, but are like chaff that the wind drives away."

## New Testament
> Matthew 18:32 — "Then his master summoned him and said to him, 'You wicked servant! I forgave you all that debt because you pleaded with me.'"

## Trivia
- In Hebrew, 'rasha' describes someone who is guilty, criminal, or morally wrong, acting in defiance of God.
- The Greek 'ponēros' implies active, malicious evil that seeks to cause harm or corruption to others.
- Psalm 1 vividly contrasts the wicked, who are blown away like chaff, with the righteous, who are like deeply rooted trees.
