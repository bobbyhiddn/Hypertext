## Word
**WICKED** — Morally corrupt; guilty of hostility toward God and others.

## Old Testament
> Psalm 1:4 — "The wicked are not so, but are like chaff that the wind drives away."

## New Testament
> Matthew 13:49 — "The angels will come out and separate the wicked from the righteous."

## Trivia
- In Hebrew, 'rasha' describes someone guilty of a crime or actively causing harm to others in the community.
- Psalm 1 contrasts the wicked, who are blown away like chaff, with the righteous, who are like deeply rooted trees.
- The Greek word 'ponēros' is used in the Lord's Prayer when asking for deliverance from 'the evil one'.
