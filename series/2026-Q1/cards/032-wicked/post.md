## Word
**WICKED** — Morally wrong, evil, or guilty; living in defiance of God's order.

## Old Testament
> Psalm 1:4 — "The wicked are not so, but are like chaff that the wind drives away."

## New Testament
> Matthew 13:49 — "The angels will come out and separate the wicked from the righteous."

## Trivia
- The Hebrew word 'rasha' is often used as a legal term denoting someone who is guilty of injustice or a crime.
- In Greek, 'ponēros' describes an active, malignant evil that seeks to corrupt and cause harm to others.
- The Book of Proverbs contrasts the 'wicked' with the 'righteous' over 80 times to illustrate the paths of foolishness and wisdom.
