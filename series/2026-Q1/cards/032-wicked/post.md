## Word
**WICKED** — Morally bad, guilty, actively evil

## Old Testament
> Psalm 1:4 — "The wicked are not so, but are like chaff that the wind drives away."

## New Testament
> Matthew 18:32 — "Then his master summoned him and said to him, 'You wicked servant! I forgave you all that debt because you pleaded with me.'"

## Trivia
- The Hebrew 'rasha' implies being guilty of a crime, hostile to God, or living in defiance of His character.
- The Greek 'poneros' emphasizes an active, malignant form of evil that causes pain, sorrow, and destruction.
- In the New Testament, 'poneros' is often translated as 'evil,' including the specific title 'the evil one' for Satan.
