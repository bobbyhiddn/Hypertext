## Word
**WICKED** — Morally bad, evil, or guilty

## Old Testament
> Psalm 1:4 — "The wicked are not so, but are like chaff that the wind drives away."

## New Testament
> Colossians 1:21 — "...alienated and hostile in mind, doing wicked deeds."

## Trivia
- In Hebrew, 'rasha' implies being guilty of an offense or living in defiance of God's character.
- The Greek word 'ponēros' emphasizes the inevitable agonies and misery that always accompany evil.
- The biblical concept of wickedness is often contrasted directly with righteousness, describing a heart condition rather than just actions.
