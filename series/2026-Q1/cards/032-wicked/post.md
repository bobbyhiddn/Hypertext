## Word
**WICKED** — Morally bad, guilty, hostile to God, evil in principle or practice.

## Old Testament
> Psalm 1:4 — "The wicked are not so, but are like chaff that the wind drives away."

## New Testament
> Matthew 13:49 — "So it will be at the end of the age. The angels will come out and separate the evil from the righteous."

## Trivia
- The Hebrew 'rasha' often functions as a legal term for someone who is guilty or in the wrong.
- The Greek 'ponēros' emphasizes the active, destructive nature of evil that intentionally spreads misery.
- Wisdom literature frequently contrasts the wicked with the righteous to illustrate their opposing paths and ultimate destinies.
