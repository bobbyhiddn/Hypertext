## Word
**WICKED** — Evil, morally wrong, unrighteous, guilty

## Old Testament
> Proverbs 28:1 — "The wicked flee when no one pursues, but the righteous are bold as a lion."

## New Testament
> Matthew 13:49 — "So it will be at the end of the age. The angels will come out and separate the evil from the righteous."

## Trivia
- The Hebrew root 'rasha' is a legal term often meaning 'guilty' or 'in the wrong.'
- In Greek, 'ponēros' describes an active, malignant form of evil that seeks to corrupt others.
- The phrase 'the wicked one' (ho ponēros) is frequently used by the Apostle John to describe Satan.
