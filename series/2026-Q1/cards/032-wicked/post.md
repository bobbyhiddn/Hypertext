## Word
**WICKED** — Morally bad, evil, guilty, or hostile to God

## Old Testament
> Psalm 1:6 — "But the way of the wicked will perish."

## New Testament
> Matthew 13:49 — "The angels will come and separate the wicked from the righteous."

## Trivia
- In Hebrew, 'rasha' implies being guilty of a crime or morally wrong, often contrasted with the righteous ('tzaddik').
- Psalm 1 compares the wicked to unstable, lifeless chaff driven away by the wind.
- The Greek 'ponēros' describes active, harmful evil, and is frequently used as a title for Satan ('the evil one').
