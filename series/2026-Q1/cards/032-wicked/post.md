## Word
**WICKED** — Morally corrupt; given to evil practices; actively harmful or contrary to God's law.

## Old Testament
> Proverbs 15:9 — "The Lord detests the way of the wicked, but he loves those who pursue righteousness."

## New Testament
> Matthew 13:49 — "...the angels will come and separate the wicked from the righteous..."

## Trivia
- The Hebrew word 'rasha' implies being guilty or in the wrong, often in a legal or covenantal sense.
- In Greek, 'ponēros' emphasizes an active, harmful, and malignant form of evil that seeks to corrupt others.
- Biblical wisdom literature frequently contrasts the destructive path of the wicked with the flourishing path of the righteous.
