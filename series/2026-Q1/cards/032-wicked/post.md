## Word
**WICKED** — Morally corrupt, evil, or guilty before God

## Old Testament
> Psalm 1:4 — "The wicked are not so, but are like chaff that the wind drives away."

## New Testament
> Matthew 13:49 — "The angels will come out and separate the wicked from the righteous."

## Trivia
- In Psalm 1, the wicked are compared to 'chaff'—the weightless, useless husk of grain blown away during winnowing.
- The Hebrew word 'rasha' often carries a legal sense of being guilty or in the wrong before a judge.
- In the New Testament, the Greek 'poneros' describes not just passive badness, but active, harmful, and corrupting evil.
