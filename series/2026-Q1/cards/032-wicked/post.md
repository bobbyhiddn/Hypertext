## Word
**WICKED** — Morally wrong, guilty, or actively harmful.

## Old Testament
> Proverbs 28:1 — "The wicked flee when no one pursues, but the righteous are bold as a lion."

## New Testament
> Matthew 18:32 — "You wicked servant! I forgave you all that debt because you pleaded with me."

## Trivia
- The Hebrew root 'rasha' conveys the idea of being disjointed, guilty, or causing trouble.
- In Biblical poetry, the 'wicked' are frequently contrasted directly with the 'righteous' (tsaddiq).
- The Greek 'ponēros' emphasizes active, harmful, and corrupting evil, rather than just abstract badness.
