## Word
**WICKED** — Morally bad, evil, guilty, acting contrary to God's law.

## Old Testament
> Psalm 37:21 — "The wicked borrows but does not pay back, but the righteous is generous and gives."

## New Testament
> Matthew 13:49 — "The angels will come out and separate the evil from the righteous..."

## Trivia
- The Hebrew 'rasha' denotes someone guilty of sin or injustice, often contrasted with the righteous.
- The Greek 'ponēros' describes active, malicious evil that seeks to corrupt and harm others.
- In the Old Testament, wickedness is frequently described as a snare or a trap that ultimately catches the wicked themselves.
