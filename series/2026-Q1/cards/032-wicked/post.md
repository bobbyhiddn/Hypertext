## Word
**WICKED** — Morally bad or guilty; acting contrary to God's law

## Old Testament
> Psalm 1:4 — "The wicked are not so, but are like chaff that the wind drives away."

## New Testament
> Matthew 13:49 — "The angels will come and separate the wicked from the righteous."

## Trivia
- In Hebrew, rasha fundamentally describes someone guilty of a crime or actively hostile to God, directly opposing the righteous (tzaddik).
- The Greek ponēros emphasizes an active, malignant form of evil that causes pain, labor, and destruction, unlike mere badness.
- In both Testaments, the wicked are frequently contrasted with the righteous using agricultural metaphors, such as chaff blown by the wind or weeds among wheat.
