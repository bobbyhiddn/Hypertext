## Word
**WICKED** — Morally bad, guilty, or actively harmful.

## Old Testament
> Psalm 1:4 — "The wicked are not so, but are like chaff that the wind drives away."

## New Testament
> Matthew 13:49 — "The angels will come out and separate the wicked from the righteous."

## Trivia
- In Hebrew, 'rasha' often carries a legal or covenantal sense of being guilty or in the wrong.
- Biblical wisdom literature frequently contrasts the destructive path of the wicked with the enduring way of the righteous.
- The Greek word 'ponēros' describes active, malignant harm rather than just passive badness or poor quality.
