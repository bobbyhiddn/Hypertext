## Word
**WICKED** — Morally bad, guilty, or actively hostile to God's ways.

## Old Testament
> Proverbs 15:9 — "The way of the wicked is an abomination to the Lord, but he loves him who pursues righteousness."

## New Testament
> Matthew 18:32 — "Then his master summoned him and said to him, 'You wicked servant! I forgave you all that debt because you pleaded with me.'"

## Trivia
- The Hebrew word 'rasha' describes someone who is guilty, morally wrong, or actively hostile to God.
- In Matthew 18, the unforgiving servant is called 'ponēros' (wicked) for refusing to show the mercy he had received.
- The Psalms frequently contrast the wicked with the righteous, depicting them as chaff driven away by the wind.
