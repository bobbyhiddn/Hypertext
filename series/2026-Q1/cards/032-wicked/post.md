## Word
**WICKED** — Morally bad in principle or practice; sinful, evil, ungodly.

## Old Testament
> Psalm 1:4 — "The wicked are not so, but are like chaff that the wind drives away."

## New Testament
> Matthew 13:49 — "This is how it will be at the end of the age. The angels will come and separate the wicked from the righteous."

## Trivia
- In Hebrew, 'rasha' describes one guilty of a crime, hostile to God, or living in defiance of His character.
- The Greek 'ponēros' emphasizes the active, destructive, and harmful nature of evil, not just its presence.
- The Psalms frequently contrast the wicked, who are like unstable chaff, with the righteous, who are like deeply rooted trees.
