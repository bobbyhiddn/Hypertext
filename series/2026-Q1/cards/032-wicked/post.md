## Word
**WICKED** — morally bad, guilty, or hostile to God

## Old Testament
> Psalm 1:4 — "The wicked are not so, but are like chaff that the wind drives away."

## New Testament
> Matthew 13:49 — "The angels will come out and separate the wicked from the righteous."

## Trivia
- In Hebrew, 'rasha' primarily denotes someone guilty of a crime or sin, often in a legal sense.
- The Greek 'ponēros' emphasizes an active, harmful evil rather than just a passive lack of goodness.
- Wisdom literature frequently contrasts the 'wicked' with the 'righteous' to highlight their opposing destinies.
