## Word
**WICKED** — Morally bad; guilty, harmful, or hostile to God

## Old Testament
> Psalm 1:4 — "The wicked are not so, but are like chaff that the wind drives away."

## New Testament
> Ephesians 6:16 — "...to extinguish all the flaming darts of the wicked one."

## Trivia
- In Hebrew, 'rasha' often carries a legal sense of being guilty or in the wrong, rather than just moral badness.
- The Greek 'poneros' describes active, harmful evil that seeks to corrupt others, not merely passive badness.
- Wisdom literature frequently contrasts the wicked with the righteous, often using agricultural metaphors like windblown chaff.
