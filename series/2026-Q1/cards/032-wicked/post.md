## Word
**WICKED** — Morally wrong, hostile to God, guilty

## Old Testament
> Psalm 1:4 — "The wicked are not so, but are like chaff that the wind drives away."

## New Testament
> 2 Thessalonians 3:2 — "...and that we may be delivered from wicked and evil men. For not all have faith."

## Trivia
- In Hebrew, 'rasha' describes someone who is morally guilty and actively hostile to God's standards.
- The Greek 'ponēros' implies a malicious, aggressive evil that actively seeks to corrupt others.
- Scripture frequently contrasts the fleeting, temporary success of the wicked with the eternal security of the righteous.
