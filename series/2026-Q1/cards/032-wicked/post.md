## Word
**WICKED** — Morally wrong, evil, guilty, or hostile to God.

## Old Testament
> Psalm 1:4 — "The wicked are not so, but are like chaff that the wind drives away."

## New Testament
> Matthew 13:49 — "The angels will come and separate the wicked from the righteous."

## Trivia
- The Hebrew word 'rasha' implies being guilty of a crime or living in defiance of God's character.
- In Psalm 1, the wicked are contrasted with the righteous and compared to chaff blown away by the wind.
- The Greek word 'poneros' emphasizes the painful, toilsome, and destructive nature of evil.
