## Word
**WICKED** — Morally corrupt; characterized by evil intent or guilty of harming others.

## Old Testament
> Psalm 1:4 — "The wicked are not so, but are like chaff that the wind drives away."

## New Testament
> Matthew 13:49 — "The angels will come out and separate the wicked from the righteous."

## Trivia
- In biblical poetry, the 'wicked' (rasha) are consistently contrasted with the 'righteous' (tsaddiq).
- The Greek word 'ponēros' emphasizes an active, malignant evil that seeks to corrupt others.
- In ancient Hebrew courts, the term carried a legal sense of being guilty or in the wrong.
