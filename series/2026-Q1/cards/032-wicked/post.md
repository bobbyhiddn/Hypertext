## Word
**WICKED** — Evil or morally wrong in principle or practice; sinful.

## Old Testament
> Psalm 1:4 — "The wicked are not so, but are like chaff that the wind drives away."

## New Testament
> Matthew 18:32 — "Then his master summoned him and said to him, 'You wicked servant! I forgave you all that debt because you pleaded with me.'"

## Trivia
- The Hebrew word 'rasha' implies being guilty of a crime or morally wrong, and is frequently contrasted with the righteous.
- In Greek, 'ponēros' denotes active, harmful evil and malice, not just passive badness or poor quality.
- The Psalms frequently use the imagery of chaff driven by the wind to describe the ultimate, fleeting fate of the wicked.
