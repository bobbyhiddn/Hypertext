## Word
**WICKED** — Morally bad or guilty; hostile to God and His righteous ways.

## Old Testament
> Psalm 1:4 — "The wicked are not so, but are like chaff that the wind drives away."

## New Testament
> Matthew 13:49 — "The angels will come out and separate the wicked from the righteous."

## Trivia
- In Hebrew, 'rasha' implies being legally guilty or in the wrong, not just morally corrupt.
- The Greek 'ponēros' emphasizes the active, harmful, and destructive nature of evil.
- Psalm 1 contrasts the wicked, who are blown away like chaff, with the righteous, who are deeply rooted.
