## Word
**WICKED** — Morally corrupt; guilty and hostile toward God.

## Old Testament
> Psalm 1:4 — "The wicked are not so, but are like chaff that the wind drives away."

## New Testament
> Matthew 13:49 — "The angels will come and separate the wicked from the righteous."

## Trivia
- In Hebrew, 'rasha' implies being guilty of a crime or departing from the right path.
- Psalm 1 contrasts the wicked, who are blown away like chaff, with the righteous, who are like deeply rooted trees.
- In Jesus' parables, angels are often depicted as separating the wicked from the righteous at the end of the age.
