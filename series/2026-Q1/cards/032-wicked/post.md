## Word
**WICKED** — Morally evil in principle or practice; guilty of sin or offense.

## Old Testament
> Psalm 1:4 — "The wicked are not so, but are like chaff that the wind drives away."

## New Testament
> Ephesians 6:16 — "...with which you can extinguish all the flaming darts of the wicked one."

## Trivia
- In Hebrew, 'rasha' implies being guilty of a crime or morally wrong, frequently contrasted with the righteous ('tzadik').
- The Greek 'ponēros' emphasizes an active, harmful, and corrupting evil, rather than just a passive lack of goodness.
- Biblical wisdom literature frequently employs the 'two ways' motif, starkly contrasting the destructive path of the wicked with the life-giving path of the just.
