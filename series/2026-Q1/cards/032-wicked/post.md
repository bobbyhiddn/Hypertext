## Word
**WICKED** — Morally corrupt, harmful, or guilty; actively hostile toward God and His covenant.

## Old Testament
> Psalm 1:4 — "The wicked are not so, but are like chaff that the wind drives away."

## New Testament
> Matthew 13:49 — "So it will be at the end of the age. The angels will come out and separate the evil from the righteous."

## Trivia
- In Hebrew, 'rasha' fundamentally implies being guilty of a crime or being actively hostile to God's order.
- The Greek 'ponēros' describes an active, harmful evil that seeks to corrupt others, rather than just passive badness.
- The Psalms frequently contrast the 'wicked' with the 'righteous' to highlight two divergent paths of life and their ultimate destinations.
