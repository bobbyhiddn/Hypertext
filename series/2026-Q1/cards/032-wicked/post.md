## Word
**WICKED** — Morally bad in principle or practice; evil; sinful

## Old Testament
> Psalm 1:4 — "The wicked are not so, but are like chaff that the wind drives away."

## New Testament
> Matthew 13:49 — "This is how it will be at the end of the age. The angels will come and separate the wicked from the righteous."

## Trivia
- The Hebrew word 'rasha' conveys the idea of being guilty or morally wrong, and is frequently contrasted with the righteous.
- In Psalm 1, the wicked are vividly compared to chaff, the useless husk of grain that is easily blown away by the wind.
- The Greek word 'poneros' describes an active, malignant form of evil that causes labor, pain, and sorrow, rather than just general badness.
