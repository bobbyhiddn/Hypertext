## Word
**WICKED** — Morally wrong, evil, or actively malicious.

## Old Testament
> Proverbs 4:19 — "The way of the wicked is like deep darkness; they do not know over what they stumble."

## New Testament
> Matthew 13:49 — "The angels will come out and separate the wicked from the righteous."

## Trivia
- The Greek word 'ponēros' originally meant 'oppressed by toils' before evolving to mean 'worthless' or 'wicked.'
- In Hebrew, 'rasha' often describes not just an evil person, but one who is actively hostile to God and His people.
- The Book of Proverbs frequently contrasts the 'wicked' (rasha) with the 'righteous' (tsaddiq) to illustrate the two paths of life.
