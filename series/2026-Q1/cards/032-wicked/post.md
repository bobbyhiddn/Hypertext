## Word
**WICKED** — morally bad in principle or practice; actively evil

## Old Testament
> Psalm 1:4 — "The wicked are not so, but are like chaff that the wind drives away."

## New Testament
> Matthew 13:49 — "The angels will come out and separate the wicked from the righteous,"

## Trivia
- In Hebrew, 'rasha' often denotes someone legally guilty of a crime or hostile to God's covenant.
- The Greek word 'ponēros' implies an active, malignant evil that intentionally seeks to corrupt others.
- Wisdom literature frequently contrasts the wicked with the righteous to illustrate two opposing paths of life.
