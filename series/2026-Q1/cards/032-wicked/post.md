## Word
**WICKED** — Morally bad in principle or practice; evil, sinful

## Old Testament
> Psalm 1:4 — "The wicked are not so, but are like chaff that the wind drives away."

## New Testament
> Matthew 13:49 — "The angels will come and separate the wicked from the righteous."

## Trivia
- In Hebrew, 'rasha' implies a guilty person who lives in active defiance of God's character.
- The Greek 'ponēros' emphasizes the active, destructive, and pain-causing nature of evil.
- Psalm 1 contrasts the wicked, who are like blown chaff, with the righteous, who are like deeply rooted trees.
