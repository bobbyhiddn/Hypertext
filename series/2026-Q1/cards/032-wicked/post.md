## Word
**WICKED** — Morally bad in principle or practice; guilty; hostile to God.

## Old Testament
> Psalm 37:21 — "The wicked borroweth, and payeth not again: but the righteous sheweth mercy, and giveth."

## New Testament
> Matthew 13:49 — "The angels shall come forth, and sever the wicked from among the just."

## Trivia
- In Hebrew, 'rasha' is often used in legal contexts to describe the guilty party in a dispute.
- The Greek 'ponēros' emphasizes active, malignant evil that causes labor, pain, and sorrow.
- Psalm 1 contrasts the wicked, who are blown away like chaff, with the righteous, who are like deeply rooted trees.
