## Word
**WISE** — possessing discernment, good judgment, and practical skill in living

## Old Testament
> Proverbs 13:20 — "Whoever walks with the wise becomes wise, but the companion of fools will suffer harm."

## New Testament
> Matthew 7:24 — "Everyone then who hears these words of mine and does them will be like a wise man who built his house on the rock."

## Trivia
- The Hebrew word 'chakam' refers not just to intellectual knowledge, but to practical skill and moral discernment [1.16].
- In Matthew 7, Jesus compares those who hear and act on His teachings to a wise builder on solid rock.
- Wisdom literature, such as Proverbs and Ecclesiastes, repeatedly contrasts the wise person with the fool.
