## Word
**WISE** — Having deep understanding, skill, and good judgment rooted in reverence for God.

## Old Testament
> Proverbs 9:9 — "Give instruction to a wise man, and he will be still wiser; teach a righteous man, and he will increase in learning."

## New Testament
> Ephesians 5:15 — "Look carefully then how you walk, not as unwise but as wise,"

## Trivia
- The Hebrew word 'chakam' encompasses not just intellectual knowledge, but practical skill, artistry, and craftsmanship [1.2].
- In biblical literature, true wisdom is consistently rooted in 'the fear of the Lord' rather than human ingenuity.
- While the Greeks highly prized 'sophos', Paul contrasted worldly wisdom with the seemingly foolish wisdom of the cross.
