## Word
**WISE** — Possessing discernment, deep understanding, and sound judgment.

## Old Testament
> Proverbs 13:20 — "Whoever walks with the wise becomes wise, but the companion of fools will suffer harm."

## New Testament
> Matthew 7:24 — "Everyone then who hears these words of mine and does them will be like a wise man who built his house on the rock."

## Trivia
- In Hebrew thought, being 'wise' (chakam) was often associated with practical skill and craftsmanship, not just intellect.
- Proverbs frequently contrasts the 'wise' person with the 'fool,' emphasizing moral choices and teachability over raw intelligence.
- Jesus advised His disciples to be 'wise as serpents and innocent as doves' when navigating a hostile world.
