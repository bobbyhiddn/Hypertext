## Word
**DOVE** — The bird that brought back the olive leaf

## Old Testament
> Genesis 8:11 — "the dove came in to him in the evening; and, lo, in her mouth was an olive leaf"

## New Testament
> Matthew 3:16 — "the Spirit of God descending like a dove"

## Trivia
- Noah sent the dove three times: once it found no rest, once it brought the olive leaf, and the third time it did not return.
- The prophet Jonah's name is the word dove; he too was sent out over the waters and came back.
- All four gospels see the Spirit descend like a dove at the Jordan - the flood's sign of peace returning over water.
