## Word
**HAGAR** — Sarai's handmaid, driven out and found by the fountain

## Old Testament
> Genesis 16:8 — "And he said, Hagar, Sarai's maid, whence camest thou? and whither wilt thou go?"

## New Testament
> Galatians 4:24 — "the one from the mount Sinai, which gendereth to bondage, which is Agar"

## Trivia
- Hagar is the first person in Scripture to give God a name - Thou God seest me.
- The angel's command is to return and submit; the promise of a multitude comes only after the going back.
- Paul makes Hagar and Sarah two covenants, which is why a handmaid of Genesis argues a point in Galatians.
