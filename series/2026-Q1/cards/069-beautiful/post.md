## Word
**BEAUTIFUL** — Fair to look upon

## Old Testament
> Genesis 12:11 — "I know that thou art a fair woman to look upon"

## New Testament
> Romans 10:15 — "How beautiful are the feet of them that preach the gospel of peace"

## Trivia
- Abram feared Sarai's beauty in Egypt; the fear, not the beauty, produced the lie that she was his sister.
- Ecclesiastes says God has made every thing beautiful in his time - yapheh joined to the idea of season, exactly as horaios is in Greek.
- The lame man of Acts 3 sat at the temple gate called Beautiful, horaia, and was healed there.
