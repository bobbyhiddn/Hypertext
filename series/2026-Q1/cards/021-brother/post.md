## Word
**BROTHER** — A male sibling, kinsman, or close associate; a fellow believer in the family of God.

## Old Testament
> Psalm 133:1 — "Behold, how good and how pleasant it is for brethren to dwell together in unity!"

## New Testament
> Hebrews 2:11 — "For both he that sanctifieth and they who are sanctified are all of one: for which cause he is not ashamed to call them brethren."

## Trivia
- Cain and Abel were the first brothers recorded in Scripture.
- Abraham referred to his nephew Lot as his brother in Genesis 14.
- Jesus nicknamed the brothers James and John 'Sons of Thunder'.
