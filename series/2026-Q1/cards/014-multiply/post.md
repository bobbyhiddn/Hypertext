## Word
**MULTIPLY** — To increase in number or quantity; to reproduce and grow abundantly.

## Old Testament
> Genesis 22:17 — "I will multiply your seed as the stars of the heaven, and as the sand which is upon the sea shore."

## New Testament
> Acts 6:7 — "And the word of God increased; and the number of the disciples multiplied in Jerusalem greatly."

## Trivia
- The first command given to humanity in Genesis 1:28.
- Used to describe the miracle of the loaves and fishes.
- Often describes the rapid growth of the early church in Acts.
