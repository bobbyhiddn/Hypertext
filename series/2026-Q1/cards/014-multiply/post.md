![MULTIPLY card](./outputs/card_1024x1536.png)

## Word
**MULTIPLY** — To become many; to increase and fill

## Old Testament
> Genesis 22:17 — "in blessing I will bless thee, and in multiplying I will multiply thy seed"

## New Testament
> Hebrews 6:14 — "Surely blessing I will bless thee, and multiplying I will multiply thee"

## Trivia
- The blessing to be fruitful and multiply is given to the fish and birds before it is ever given to man.
- After the flood the blessing is repeated to Noah twice in one chapter, as if the world were being started again.
- Hebrews 6:14 keeps the Hebrew doubling in Greek - blessing I will bless, multiplying I will multiply - rather than smoothing it into an adverb.
