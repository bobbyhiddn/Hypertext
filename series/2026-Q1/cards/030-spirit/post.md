## Word
**SPIRIT** — The animating force of God; wind, breath, or life-essence.

## Old Testament
> Genesis 1:2 — "The Spirit of God was hovering over the face of the waters."

## New Testament
> 2 Corinthians 3:6 — "For the letter kills, but the Spirit gives life."

## Trivia
- Hebrew 'ruach' (feminine) and Greek 'pneuma' (neuter) both mean 'wind', 'breath', or 'spirit'.
- The Spirit appears in the second verse of the Bible (Gen 1:2) and the final invitation (Rev 22:17).
- In the OT, the Spirit often 'rushed upon' leaders for specific tasks; in the NT, the Spirit indwells believers.
