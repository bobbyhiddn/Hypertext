## Word
**SPIRIT** — The vital principle or animating force within living beings; the third person of the Trinity; wind or breath.

## Old Testament
> Genesis 1:2 — "And the Spirit of God moved upon the face of the waters."

## New Testament
> 1 Corinthians 2:10 — "The Spirit searcheth all things, yea, the deep things of God."

## Trivia
- Both Hebrew 'ruach' and Greek 'pneuma' can mean 'wind,' 'breath,' or 'spirit' depending on context.
- In the Old Testament, the Spirit often empowered individuals for specific tasks; in the New, He indwells believers.
- The Spirit is described as a 'deposit' or 'seal' guaranteeing the believer's future inheritance.
