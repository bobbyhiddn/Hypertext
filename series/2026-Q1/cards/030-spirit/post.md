## Word
**SPIRIT** — The animating force of God; breath, wind, or the Holy Spirit.

## Old Testament
> Zechariah 4:6 — "Not by might, nor by power, but by my spirit, saith the LORD of hosts."

## New Testament
> John 3:8 — "The wind bloweth where it listeth... so is every one that is born of the Spirit."

## Trivia
- The Hebrew word 'ruach' and Greek 'pneuma' both mean 'wind,' 'breath,' or 'spirit.'
- In Genesis 1:2, the Spirit 'hovers' over the waters, a verb used for a mother bird.
- The 'fruit of the Spirit' (Galatians 5) lists nine attributes including love and peace.
