## Word
**SPIRIT** — The non-physical part of a person; the Holy Spirit of God; wind; breath; life-force.

## Old Testament
> Zechariah 4:6 — "Not by might, nor by power, but by my Spirit, says the LORD of hosts."

## New Testament
> 2 Corinthians 3:17 — "Now the Lord is the Spirit, and where the Spirit of the Lord is, there is freedom."

## Trivia
- Hebrew 'ruach' is feminine and Greek 'pneuma' is neuter; both also mean 'wind' or 'breath'.
- In the OT, the Spirit empowered leaders temporarily; in the NT, He indwells believers permanently.
- Jesus compared the Spirit to the wind, which blows where it wishes and cannot be predicted.
