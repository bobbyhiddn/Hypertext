## Word
**SPIRIT** — The non-physical part of a person; the Holy Spirit of God; breath, wind, or life force.

## Old Testament
> Zechariah 4:6 — "Not by might nor by power, but by my Spirit,' says the LORD Almighty."

## New Testament
> John 3:8 — "The wind blows where it wishes... So it is with everyone born of the Spirit."

## Trivia
- Both 'Ruach' (Hebrew) and 'Pneuma' (Greek) mean wind, breath, and spirit.
- The Spirit appears in the second verse of Genesis and the final chapter of Revelation.
- Represents the empowering presence of God, often symbolized by wind, fire, or a dove.
