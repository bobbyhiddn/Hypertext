## Word
**SPIRIT** — The animating force of life; the non-physical part of a person; the Holy Spirit of God.

## Old Testament
> Genesis 1:2 — "And the Spirit of God was hovering over the face of the waters."

## New Testament
> John 6:63 — "It is the Spirit who gives life; the flesh profits nothing."

## Trivia
- The Hebrew 'ruach' and Greek 'pneuma' both mean wind, breath, and spirit.
- The 'fruit of the Spirit' is a singular noun representing nine attributes like love and joy.
- In the Old Testament, the Spirit often 'rushed' upon leaders to empower them for specific tasks.
