## Word
**SPIRIT** — The non-physical part of a person; the Holy Spirit; breath, wind, or vital principle.

## Old Testament
> Ezekiel 37:14 — "I will put my Spirit in you and you will live."

## New Testament
> John 6:63 — "The Spirit gives life; the flesh counts for nothing."

## Trivia
- Hebrew 'ruach' and Greek 'pneuma' both mean wind, breath, and spirit.
- The Spirit is the agent of creation, hovering over the waters in Genesis 1.
- Often symbolized by elements like fire, wind, water, oil, and a dove.
