## Word
**ADAM** — The first human; mankind; earth-born.

## Old Testament
> Genesis 2:7 — "The LORD God formed the man of dust from the ground and breathed into his nostrils the breath of life."

## New Testament
> 1 Corinthians 15:45 — "The first man Adam became a living being; the last Adam became a life-giving spirit."

## Trivia
- Derived from the Hebrew 'adamah', meaning 'ground' or 'red earth'.
- Often referred to as 'ha-adam' ('the man') rather than a proper name in early Genesis.
- Paul identifies Jesus as the 'Last Adam', who brings life where the first brought death.
