## Word
**OVERTHROW** — To turn a city upside down in judgment

## Old Testament
> Genesis 19:25 — "And he overthrew those cities, and all the plain"

## New Testament
> Matthew 21:12 — "overthrew the tables of the moneychangers"

## Trivia
- Haphak means to turn over - the same verb overturns Sodom in Genesis 19 and, in Jonah, threatens Nineveh with being overturned, a city that turned instead.
- Katastrepho, to overturn, is the verb of the tables in the temple; its noun katastrophe passed into English unchanged.
- Babel was not overthrown by fire but by confusion - the tower was simply left, the first ruin abandoned rather than destroyed.
