## Word
**CLEAN** — Free from dirt, ritually pure, morally undefiled

## Old Testament
> Psalm 51:10 — "Create in me a clean heart, O God, and renew a steadfast spirit within me."

## New Testament
> John 15:3 — "You are already clean because of the word which I have spoken to you."

## Trivia
- In the Old Testament, 'tahor' referred to ritual purity, which was required for entering the presence of God.
- Jesus expanded the concept of cleanliness from outward ritual observance to inward purity of the heart.
- The Greek word 'katharos' is the root of the English word 'catharsis', meaning a cleansing or purification.
