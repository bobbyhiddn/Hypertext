## Word
**CLEAN** — Pure, unblemished, free from defilement

## Old Testament
> Psalm 51:10 — "Create in me a clean heart, O God, and renew a right spirit within me."

## New Testament
> John 15:3 — "Already you are clean because of the word that I have spoken to you."

## Trivia
- In the Old Testament, being ritually clean (tahor) was required to enter the tabernacle or temple.
- The Greek word 'katharos' is the root of the English word 'catharsis', meaning a purification or cleansing.
- Jesus redefined cleanness, teaching that true purity comes from the heart rather than external washing.
