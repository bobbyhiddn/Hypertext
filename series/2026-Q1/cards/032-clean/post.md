## Word
**CLEAN** — Pure, unblemished, free from defilement.

## Old Testament
> Psalm 51:10 — "Create in me a clean heart, O God, and renew a right spirit within me."

## New Testament
> John 15:3 — "You are already clean because of the word I have spoken to you."

## Trivia
- In the Old Testament, 'tahor' often refers to the ceremonial purity required to enter God's presence.
- Jesus radically redefined cleanness, teaching that it stems from the heart rather than external rituals.
- The Greek word 'katharos' is the root of the English word 'catharsis', meaning a purification or purging.
