## Word
**CLEAN** — Pure, unblemished, free from moral or ritual impurity

## Old Testament
> Psalm 51:10 — "Create in me a clean heart, O God..."

## New Testament
> John 15:3 — "Already you are clean because of the word that I have spoken to you."

## Trivia
- In the Levitical law, being 'clean' (tahor) was required to enter the tabernacle and participate in worship.
- The Greek word 'katharos' is the root for the English word 'catharsis', meaning a purification or cleansing.
- Jesus expanded the concept of cleanliness from external ritual washing to internal purity of the heart.
