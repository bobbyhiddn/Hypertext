## Word
**CLEAN** — Pure, unblemished, and free from defilement or sin.

## Old Testament
> Psalm 51:10 — "Create in me a clean heart, O God, and renew a right spirit within me."

## New Testament
> John 15:3 — "You are already clean because of the word I have spoken to you."

## Trivia
- In the Old Testament, 'tahor' referred to both physical cleanliness and the ritual purity required for worship.
- Jesus expanded the concept of cleanliness from external levitical rituals to internal purity of the heart.
- The Greek word 'katharos' is the origin of the English word 'catharsis', meaning a deep emotional purging or cleansing.
