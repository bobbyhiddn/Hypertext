## Word
**CLEAN** — pure, free from defilement, unblemished

## Old Testament
> Psalm 51:10 — "Create in me a clean heart, O God, and renew a right spirit within me."

## New Testament
> John 15:3 — "Already you are clean because of the word that I have spoken to you."

## Trivia
- The Hebrew word 'tahor' is used extensively in Leviticus to describe the ritual purity required to enter God's presence.
- In the New Testament, Jesus redefines 'clean' (katharos) as an inward state of the heart rather than mere external ritual.
- The biblical concept of becoming clean is closely tied to physical water rituals, prefiguring the Christian practice of baptism.
