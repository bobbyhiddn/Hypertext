## Word
**CLEAN** — Free from dirt, marks, or stains; morally uncontaminated; ritually pure.

## Old Testament
> Psalm 51:10 — "Create in me a clean heart, O God; and renew a right spirit within me."

## New Testament
> John 15:3 — "Now ye are clean through the word which I have spoken unto you."

## Trivia
- The Hebrew 'tahor' describes a state of ritual purity required for temple worship.
- Jesus declared all foods clean in Mark 7, shifting the focus to the heart.
- The phrase 'cleanliness is next to godliness' is from John Wesley, not the Bible.
