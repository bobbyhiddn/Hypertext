## Word
**CLEAN** — Ritually pure, unblemished, or morally undefiled.

## Old Testament
> Psalm 51:10 — "Create in me a clean heart, O God, and renew a right spirit within me."

## New Testament
> John 15:3 — "You are already clean because of the word which I have spoken to you."

## Trivia
- In the Old Testament, being 'clean' (tahor) was often a state of ritual readiness for worship rather than strict moral perfection.
- The Greek word 'katharos' is the root of the English word 'catharsis', which refers to an emotional cleansing or purging.
- Jesus radically redefined cleanness for His followers, teaching that true purity originates from within the heart, not from outward rituals.
