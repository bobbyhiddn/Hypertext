## Word
**CLEAN** — pure, unblemished, washed

## Old Testament
> Psalm 51:10 — "Create in me a clean heart, O God, and renew a right spirit within me."

## New Testament
> John 15:3 — "You are already clean because of the word I have spoken to you."

## Trivia
- In the Old Testament, the Hebrew word 'tahor' often referred to the ritual purity required to enter God's presence in the tabernacle.
- Jesus expanded the concept of cleanliness, telling His disciples they were 'already clean' because of His teachings.
- The Greek word 'katharos' (clean or pure) is the root of the English word 'catharsis', meaning a purging or purification.
