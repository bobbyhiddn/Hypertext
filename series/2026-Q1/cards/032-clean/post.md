## Word
**CLEAN** — free from dirt or impurity; morally pure

## Old Testament
> Psalm 51:10 — "Create in me a clean heart, O God, and renew a right spirit within me."

## New Testament
> John 15:3 — "You are already clean because of the word which I have spoken to you."

## Trivia
- In the Old Testament, the Hebrew word 'tahor' was often used for ritual purity, describing animals, vessels, and people fit for worship.
- Jesus uses the Greek 'katharos' in John 15:3, creating a wordplay with 'kathairo' (to prune or cleanse) in the previous verse.
- David's plea for a 'clean heart' in Psalm 51:10 shifts the concept of cleanliness from external ritual to internal, spiritual transformation.
