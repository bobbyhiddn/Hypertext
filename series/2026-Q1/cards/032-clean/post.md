## Word
**CLEAN** — Pure, unblemished, free from impurity

## Old Testament
> Psalm 51:10 — "Create in me a clean heart, O God, and renew a right spirit within me."

## New Testament
> John 15:3 — "You are already clean because of the word I have spoken to you."

## Trivia
- The Hebrew 'tahor' conveyed both ritual purity for Temple worship and moral purity of the heart [1.8].
- Jesus expanded the Levitical concept of cleanness, teaching that true purity comes from within rather than external rituals.
- The Greek 'katharos' (clean, pure) is the root of the English word 'catharsis', meaning a purification or purging of emotions.
