## Word
**CLEAN** — Free from dirt, impurity, or sin; ritually or morally pure.

## Old Testament
> Psalm 51:10 — "Create in me a clean heart, O God, and renew a steadfast spirit within me."

## New Testament
> John 15:3 — "You are already clean because of the word I have spoken to you."

## Trivia
- The Hebrew 'tahor' applies to both ceremonial ritual purity and internal moral innocence.
- In the ancient Near East, being ritually 'clean' was a strict prerequisite to entering the temple presence.
- Jesus expanded the concept of cleanliness from external washing rituals to internal heart attitudes.
