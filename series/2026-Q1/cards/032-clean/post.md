## Word
**CLEAN** — Ritually pure, unsoiled; free from admixture or defilement.

## Old Testament
> Psalm 51:10 — "Create in me a clean heart, O God, and renew a right spirit within me."

## New Testament
> John 15:3 — "Already you are clean because of the word that I have spoken to you."

## Trivia
- The Greek 'katharos' literally means 'without admixture' or unalloyed, often used in antiquity to describe pure metals.
- The Hebrew 'tahor' primarily described the ritual purity required for Israelites to safely approach the Tabernacle.
- Jesus frequently challenged the religious elite by prioritizing inward moral cleanness over outward ritual washing.
