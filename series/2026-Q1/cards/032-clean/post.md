## Word
**CLEAN** — Pure, undefiled, free from contamination or ritual impurity.

## Old Testament
> Psalm 51:10 — "Create in me a clean heart, O God, and renew a right spirit within me."

## New Testament
> John 15:3 — "Already you are clean because of the word that I have spoken to you."

## Trivia
- The Hebrew word 'tahor' applies to both physical cleanliness, like pure gold, and ritual purity.
- The Greek word 'katharos' is the root of the English word 'catharsis,' meaning a purging or cleansing.
- In the biblical worldview, being 'clean' was closely associated with life and holiness, while 'unclean' was tied to death and decay.
