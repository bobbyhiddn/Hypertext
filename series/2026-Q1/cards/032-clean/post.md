## Word
**CLEAN** — pure, unblemished, free from defilement

## Old Testament
> Psalm 51:10 — "Create in me a clean heart, O God, and renew a right spirit within me."

## New Testament
> John 15:3 — "Already you are clean because of the word that I have spoken to you."

## Trivia
- In the Old Testament, being 'clean' (tahor) often referred to ritual purity required to enter God's presence, not just physical hygiene.
- Jesus redefined cleanness, teaching that moral purity radiating from the heart matters more than external ritual washing.
- The Greek word 'katharos' is the root for the English word 'catharsis,' meaning a purging or cleansing of emotions.
