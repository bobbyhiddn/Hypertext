## Word
**CLEAN** — Pure, unblemished, free from dirt or moral stain

## Old Testament
> Psalm 51:10 — "Create in me a clean heart, O God, and renew a right spirit within me."

## New Testament
> John 15:3 — "You are already clean because of the word I have spoken to you."

## Trivia
- In Levitical law, being 'clean' (tahor) was required to enter the tabernacle and approach God.
- Jesus expanded the concept of cleanness from external ritual purity to internal moral purity.
- The Greek 'katharos' is the root of the English word 'catharsis', meaning a cleansing or purification.
