## Word
**CLEAN** — Free from impurity; ritually or morally pure.

## Old Testament
> Psalm 51:10 — "Create in me a clean heart, O God, and renew a right spirit within me."

## New Testament
> John 15:3 — "Already you are clean because of the word that I have spoken to you."

## Trivia
- Under the Mosaic Law, being ritually 'clean' was a strict requirement to participate in temple worship.
- Jesus elevated the concept of cleanness from outward ritual purification to an internal state of the heart.
- The Greek word 'katharos' is the linguistic root of the English word 'catharsis', meaning a purification or purging.
