## Word
**CLEAN** — Pure, free from defilement or blemish; ceremonially or morally acceptable

## Old Testament
> Psalm 51:10 — "Create in me a clean heart, O God, and renew a right spirit within me."

## New Testament
> John 15:3 — "Already you are clean because of the word that I have spoken to you."

## Trivia
- In the Levitical system, 'tahor' referred to ceremonial purity required to safely approach God's presence.
- The Greek word 'katharos' is the root of the English word 'catharsis,' meaning a purification or cleansing.
- Jesus revolutionized the concept of cleanness by declaring that true purity comes from the heart, not external rituals.
