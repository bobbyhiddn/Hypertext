## Word
**CLEAN** — Pure, free from defilement, unblemished

## Old Testament
> Psalm 51:10 — "Create in me a clean heart, O God, and renew a right spirit within me."

## New Testament
> Matthew 5:8 — "Blessed are the pure in heart, for they shall see God."

## Trivia
- In the Old Testament, 'tahor' referred to both physical cleanliness and the ceremonial purity required for worship.
- The Greek word 'katharos' is the root of the English word 'catharsis', meaning a cleansing or purging.
- Jesus expanded the concept of purity beyond external rituals, teaching that true cleanness originates in the heart.
