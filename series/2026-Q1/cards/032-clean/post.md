## Word
**CLEAN** — Free from dirt or impurity; ritually or morally pure.

## Old Testament
> Psalm 51:10 — "Create in me a clean heart, O God, and renew a right spirit within me."

## New Testament
> John 15:3 — "Already you are clean because of the word that I have spoken to you."

## Trivia
- In the Old Testament, 'clean' (tahor) often referred to the ritual purity required to safely enter the presence of God at the tabernacle.
- Jesus expanded the concept of cleanliness from external, ceremonial rituals to internal, moral purity of the heart.
- The Greek word 'katharos' is the origin of the English word 'catharsis', which refers to a profound emotional purging or cleansing.
