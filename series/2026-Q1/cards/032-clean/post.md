## Word
**CLEAN** — Free from impurity; ritually or morally pure.

## Old Testament
> Psalm 51:10 — "Create in me a clean heart, O God, and renew a right spirit within me."

## New Testament
> John 15:3 — "Already you are clean because of the word that I have spoken to you."

## Trivia
- In Levitical law, physical and ritual cleanness was a strict prerequisite for entering the tabernacle.
- The Greek word 'katharos' is the root of the English word 'catharsis', meaning an emotional purification.
- Jesus radically taught that true cleanness originates from the heart, rather than from external washing rituals.
