## Word
**CLEAN** — Free from dirt, pollution, or sin; ritually pure or morally unblemished.

## Old Testament
> Psalm 51:10 — "Create in me a clean heart, O God, and renew a right spirit within me."

## New Testament
> John 15:3 — "Already you are clean because of the word that I have spoken to you."

## Trivia
- In the Old Testament, the concept of being 'clean' (tahor) applied to animals, objects, and people, determining fitness for temple worship.
- Jesus expanded the definition of cleanliness from external ritual purity to internal moral purity of the heart.
- The Greek word 'katharos' is the root of the English word 'catharsis,' meaning a cleansing or purging of emotions.
