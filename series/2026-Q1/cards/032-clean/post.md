## Word
**CLEAN** — Pure, unblemished, free from defilement

## Old Testament
> Psalm 51:10 — "Create in me a clean heart, O God, and renew a right spirit within me."

## New Testament
> John 15:3 — "Already you are clean because of the word that I have spoken to you."

## Trivia
- In Levitical law, 'tahor' described the ritual purity required to approach God's presence.
- Jesus consistently shifted the biblical focus of cleanliness from external washings to internal moral purity.
- The Greek word 'katharos' is the origin of the English word 'catharsis', meaning a purifying release.
