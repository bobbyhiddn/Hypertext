## Word
**CLEAN** — pure, unblemished, free from defilement

## Old Testament
> Psalm 51:10 — "Create in me a clean heart, O God, and renew a right spirit within me."

## New Testament
> John 15:3 — "Already you are clean because of the word that I have spoken to you."

## Trivia
- In the Levitical system, being 'clean' (tahor) was required to enter the tabernacle and participate in worship.
- Jesus redefined cleanliness, teaching that true purity comes from the heart rather than external rituals.
- The Greek word 'katharos' is the root for the English word 'catharsis', meaning a purification or purging of emotions.
