## Word
**CLEAN** — Free from dirt, stain, or impurity; ritually or morally pure.

## Old Testament
> Psalm 51:10 — "Create in me a clean heart, O God, and renew a right spirit within me."

## New Testament
> John 15:3 — "Already you are clean because of the word that I have spoken to you."

## Trivia
- The Greek word 'katharos' is the root of the English word 'catharsis,' meaning a deep cleansing or purification.
- In the Old Testament, being ritually 'clean' (tahor) was a strict requirement for entering God's presence in the tabernacle.
- In John 15, Jesus uses a wordplay where the Greek word for 'clean' is also used for 'pruning' a vine to make it fruitful.
