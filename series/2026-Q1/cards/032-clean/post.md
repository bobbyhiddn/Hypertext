## Word
**CLEAN** — Pure, free from contamination, ritually or morally unblemished.

## Old Testament
> Psalm 51:10 — "Create in me a clean heart, O God, and renew a right spirit within me."

## New Testament
> John 15:3 — "You are already clean because of the word I have spoken to you."

## Trivia
- In Hebrew, 'tahor' refers to ritual purity, a state required to approach God's presence or the Tabernacle.
- The Greek 'katharos' was used for clean water, pure metals, and a heart free from the contamination of sin.
- Jesus challenged the Pharisees by teaching that true cleanness comes from within, not just outward ritual washing.
