## Word
**CLEAN** — Pure, unsoiled, free from impurities

## Old Testament
> Psalm 51:10 — "Create in me a clean heart, O God, and renew a right spirit within me."

## New Testament
> John 15:3 — "Already you are clean because of the word that I have spoken to you."

## Trivia
- The Greek word 'katharos' is the root of the English word 'catharsis,' meaning an emotional cleansing or purification.
- In the Old Testament, the Hebrew 'tahor' often described ritual purity, which was required to safely enter God's presence at the Tabernacle.
- Jesus expanded the concept of cleanliness from external rituals to internal purity, declaring 'Blessed are the pure in heart' (Matthew 5:8).
