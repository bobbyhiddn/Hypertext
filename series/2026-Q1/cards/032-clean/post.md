## Word
**CLEAN** — pure, free from dirt or impurity, ritually acceptable

## Old Testament
> Psalm 51:10 — "Create in me a clean heart, O God..."

## New Testament
> John 15:3 — "You are already clean because of the word I have spoken to you."

## Trivia
- In the Old Testament, 'clean' (tahor) often referred to the ritual purity required to enter God's presence.
- Jesus expanded the concept of cleanliness from external rituals to internal purity of the heart.
- The Greek word 'katharos' is the root of the English word 'catharsis', meaning a cleansing or purification.
