## Word
**CLEAN** — Free from dirt, impurity, or sin; pure and unblemished.

## Old Testament
> Psalm 51:10 — "Create in me a clean heart, O God, and renew a right spirit within me."

## New Testament
> John 15:3 — "You are already clean because of the word which I have spoken to you."

## Trivia
- In the Old Testament, 'tahor' often refers to the ritual purity required to safely enter God's presence.
- Jesus redefined cleanness, emphasizing moral purity of the heart over outward ceremonial washings.
- The Greek word 'katharos' is the root of the English word 'catharsis', meaning a purging or cleansing.
