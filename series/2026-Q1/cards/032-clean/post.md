## Word
**CLEAN** — Free from impurities; ceremonially or morally pure.

## Old Testament
> Psalm 51:10 — "Create in me a clean heart, O God, and renew a right spirit within me."

## New Testament
> John 15:3 — "Already you are clean because of the word that I have spoken to you."

## Trivia
- In Levitical law, being 'clean' was a strict prerequisite to enter the tabernacle and approach God's presence.
- The Greek word 'katharos' is the origin of the English word 'catharsis', meaning a deep emotional purging or cleansing.
- Jesus radically redefined cleanness, teaching that moral purity of the heart supersedes outward ceremonial washing.
