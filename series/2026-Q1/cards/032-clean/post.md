## Word
**CLEAN** — free from dirt, impurity, or guilt; ritually acceptable

## Old Testament
> Psalm 51:10 — "Create in me a clean heart, O God, and renew a right spirit within me."

## New Testament
> John 15:3 — "Already you are clean because of the word that I have spoken to you."

## Trivia
- In the Old Testament, being 'clean' often referred to the ritual purity required to enter the temple, rather than just physical hygiene.
- Jesus expanded the concept of cleanliness from external rituals to internal moral purity of the heart.
- The Greek word 'katharos' is the root for the English word 'catharsis', meaning a cleansing or purging of emotions.
