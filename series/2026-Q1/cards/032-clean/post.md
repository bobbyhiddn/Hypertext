## Word
**CLEAN** — Free from dirt, impurity, or sin; ritually or morally pure.

## Old Testament
> Psalm 51:10 — "Create in me a clean heart, O God; and renew a right spirit within me."

## New Testament
> John 15:3 — "Now ye are clean through the word which I have spoken unto you."

## Trivia
- In the Levitical law, being 'clean' was a prerequisite for entering the tabernacle and participating in worship.
- Jesus radically shifted the focus of cleanness from external ritual washing to internal purity of the heart.
- The Greek word 'katharos' is the root for the English word 'catharsis', meaning a purging or cleansing.
