## Word
**CLEAN** — Pure, unblemished, free from defilement

## Old Testament
> Psalm 51:10 — "Create in me a clean heart, O God, and renew a steadfast spirit within me."

## New Testament
> John 15:3 — "You are already clean because of the word I have spoken to you."

## Trivia
- In John 15, the Greek word for 'clean' (katharos) shares a root with the verb for 'prune' (kathairo).
- David's plea for a clean heart in Psalm 51 uses 'bara', the same Hebrew verb used for God's creation of the cosmos.
- Under the Mosaic law, maintaining physical and ritual cleanliness was a prerequisite for participating in tabernacle worship.
