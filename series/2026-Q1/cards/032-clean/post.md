## Word
**CLEAN** — Pure, free from defilement, purged of impurities.

## Old Testament
> Psalm 51:10 — "Create in me a clean heart, O God, and renew a right spirit within me."

## New Testament
> John 15:3 — "You are already clean because of the word I have spoken to you."

## Trivia
- The Hebrew word 'tahor' refers to ritual purity, describing both clean animals and a pure heart.
- The Greek 'katharos' means unmixed or purged, describing both pure gold and a life free from guilt.
- In John 15, Jesus uses the imagery of pruning a vine to describe how His word makes the disciples clean.
