## Word
**CLEAN** — Pure, unblemished, or ritually acceptable

## Old Testament
> Psalm 51:10 — "Create in me a clean heart, O God, and renew a right spirit within me."

## New Testament
> John 15:3 — "Already you are clean because of the word that I have spoken to you."

## Trivia
- The Hebrew word 'tahor' referred to ritual readiness to enter God's presence, not merely physical hygiene.
- Jesus used the Greek 'katharos' to emphasize internal purity, declaring His disciples clean through His spoken word.
- In the Levitical system, being 'unclean' was often a temporary ritual state resolved by washing and waiting until evening.
