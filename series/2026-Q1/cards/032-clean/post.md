## Word
**CLEAN** — Pure, free from defilement or moral stain

## Old Testament
> Psalm 51:10 — "Create in me a clean heart, O God, and renew a right spirit within me."

## New Testament
> John 15:3 — "You are already clean because of the word I have spoken to you."

## Trivia
- In the Old Testament, being 'clean' (tahor) was often a ceremonial requirement for approaching God in worship.
- The Greek word 'katharos' is the root of the English word 'catharsis,' meaning a purging or cleansing.
- Jesus redefined cleanness for His disciples, teaching that true purity comes from within the heart and through His word.
