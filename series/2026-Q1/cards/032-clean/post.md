## Word
**CLEAN** — free from dirt, unblemished, pure

## Old Testament
> Psalm 51:10 — "Create in me a clean heart, O God, and renew a right spirit within me."

## New Testament
> John 15:3 — "Already you are clean because of the word that I have spoken to you."

## Trivia
- In the Old Testament, 'tahor' referred to both physical cleanliness and ceremonial purity required for worship.
- The Greek word 'katharos' is the root of the English word 'catharsis', meaning a purging or cleansing.
- Jesus expanded the concept of cleanliness from outward ritual to inner purity of the heart.
