## Word
**CLEAN** — Free from dirt, impurities, or moral blemish; purified and acceptable.

## Old Testament
> Psalm 51:10 — "Create in me a clean heart, O God, and renew a right spirit within me."

## New Testament
> John 15:3 — "Already you are clean because of the word that I have spoken to you."

## Trivia
- In the Old Testament, 'tahor' often referred to the ritual purity required to enter the Tabernacle or consume certain foods [1.8].
- In the New Testament, Jesus emphasized that true cleanness comes from within the heart, rather than just outward ceremonial washing.
- The Greek word 'katharos' is related to the English words 'chaste' and 'chasten,' reflecting a state free from corrupt desire or guilt.
