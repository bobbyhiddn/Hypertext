## Word
**CLEAN** — Pure, unblemished, free from corruption

## Old Testament
> Psalm 51:10 — "Create in me a clean heart, O God, and renew a right spirit within me."

## New Testament
> John 15:3 — "Already you are clean because of the word that I have spoken to you."

## Trivia
- In the Old Testament, 'tahor' often refers to ceremonial purity required to enter God's presence.
- The Greek word 'katharos' is the root of the English word 'catharsis', meaning a cleansing of emotions.
- Jesus taught that true cleanness comes from within, purifying the heart rather than just washing the hands.
