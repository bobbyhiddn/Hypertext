## Word
**CLEAN** — Free from dirt, pollution, or sin; ritually pure.

## Old Testament
> Psalm 51:10 — "Create in me a clean heart, O God, and renew a right spirit within me."

## New Testament
> John 15:3 — "Already you are clean because of the word that I have spoken to you."

## Trivia
- In the Levitical system, being 'clean' (tahor) referred to ritual purity, allowing a person to approach the Tabernacle.
- Jesus redefined cleanness, stating that believers are 'already clean' through His spoken word.
- The Greek word 'katharos' is the root of the English word 'catharsis,' meaning a purging or cleansing.
