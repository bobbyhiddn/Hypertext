## Word
**CLEAN** — Pure, unblemished, free from defilement.

## Old Testament
> Psalm 51:10 — "Create in me a clean heart, O God, and renew a right spirit within me."

## New Testament
> John 15:3 — "Already you are clean because of the word that I have spoken to you."

## Trivia
- In the Old Testament, being 'clean' often referred to the ritual purity required to enter the tabernacle.
- The Greek word 'katharos' is the root of the English word 'catharsis', meaning a cleansing or purging.
- Jesus redefined cleanness, emphasizing inner purity of the heart over external ritual washing.
