## Word
**CLEAN** — Free from impurities, unblemished, ritually acceptable, or morally pure.

## Old Testament
> Psalm 51:10 — "Create in me a clean heart, O God, and renew a right spirit within me."

## New Testament
> John 15:3 — "Already you are clean because of the word that I have spoken to you."

## Trivia
- In the Levitical system, being ritually 'clean' (tahor) was a strict requirement to enter the tabernacle and participate in worship.
- Jesus redefined the concept of cleanness for His followers, teaching that moral purity of the heart supersedes external ritual washing.
- The Greek word 'katharos' is the etymological root for the English word 'catharsis', which means a purging or cleansing of emotions.
