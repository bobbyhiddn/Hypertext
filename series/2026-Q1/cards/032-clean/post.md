## Word
**CLEAN** — Pure, unblemished, free from physical or ritual defilement.

## Old Testament
> Psalm 51:10 — "Create in me a clean heart, O God, and renew a right spirit within me."

## New Testament
> John 15:3 — "Already you are clean because of the word that I have spoken to you."

## Trivia
- In the Old Testament, being 'clean' often referred to the ritual purity required to enter the tabernacle or temple.
- Jesus controversially declared all foods clean, shifting the focus from external dietary laws to internal heart purity.
- The Greek word 'katharos' is the direct root of the English word 'catharsis', which means a deep emotional cleansing or purging.
