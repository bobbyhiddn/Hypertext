## Word
**CLEAN** — Free from impurity, defilement, or moral blemish; pure.

## Old Testament
> Psalm 51:10 — "Create in me a clean heart, O God, and renew a right spirit within me."

## New Testament
> John 15:3 — "Already you are clean because of the word that I have spoken to you."

## Trivia
- In the Old Testament, ritual cleanness was required to enter the tabernacle and approach God's presence.
- The Greek word 'katharos' is the root for the English word 'catharsis', meaning a purification or cleansing.
- Jesus redefined cleanness, teaching that moral purity of the heart supersedes external dietary laws.
