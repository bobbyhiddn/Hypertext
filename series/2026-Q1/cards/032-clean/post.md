## Word
**CLEAN** — pure, unblemished, washed

## Old Testament
> Psalm 51:10 — "Create in me a clean heart, O God, and renew a right spirit within me."

## New Testament
> John 15:3 — "Already you are clean because of the word that I have spoken to you."

## Trivia
- In the Levitical system, being 'clean' (tahor) was a strict prerequisite to enter the tabernacle and participate in worship.
- Jesus famously shifted the theological focus of cleanliness from external ritual washing to internal moral purity.
- The Greek word 'katharos' is the origin of the English word 'catharsis', meaning a profound emotional or spiritual cleansing.
