## Word
**CLEAN** — Free from dirt, impurity, or sin; ritually pure.

## Old Testament
> Psalm 51:10 — "Create in me a clean heart, O God, and renew a steadfast spirit within me."

## New Testament
> John 15:3 — "You are already clean because of the word which I have spoken to you."

## Trivia
- In the Old Testament, 'clean' (tahor) often referred to the ritual purity required to approach God's presence.
- Jesus redefined cleanness, teaching that true purity comes from a transformed heart rather than external rituals.
- The Greek word 'katharos' is the root of the English word 'catharsis', meaning a profound emotional cleansing or purification.
