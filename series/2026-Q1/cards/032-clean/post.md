## Word
**CLEAN** — Pure, free from defilement, innocent

## Old Testament
> Psalm 51:10 — "Create in me a clean heart, O God; and renew a right spirit within me."

## New Testament
> John 15:3 — "You are already clean because of the word which I have spoken to you."

## Trivia
- In the Old Testament, 'tahor' often referred to the ceremonial purity required to enter God's presence.
- Jesus expanded the concept of cleanness from outward ritual washing to inward purity of the heart.
- The Greek word 'katharos' is the root of the English word 'catharsis', meaning a cleansing or purging.
