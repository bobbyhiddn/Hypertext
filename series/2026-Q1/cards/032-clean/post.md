## Word
**CLEAN** — Pure, unblemished, free from defilement

## Old Testament
> Psalm 51:10 — "Create in me a clean heart, O God, and renew a right spirit within me."

## New Testament
> John 15:3 — "Already you are clean because of the word that I have spoken to you."

## Trivia
- In the Old Testament, being 'clean' (tahor) was a strict ritual prerequisite for entering the tabernacle or temple.
- The Greek word 'katharos' is the origin of the English word 'catharsis', meaning an emotional or spiritual purification.
- Jesus frequently shifted the focus of cleanliness from external, ritual washings to internal purity of the heart.
