## Word
**CLEAN** — ritually pure, innocent, washed

## Old Testament
> Psalm 51:10 — "Create in me a clean heart, O God, and renew a right spirit within me."

## New Testament
> John 15:3 — "Already you are clean because of the word that I have spoken to you."

## Trivia
- In the Old Testament, being 'clean' (tahor) was a prerequisite for participating in temple worship and community life.
- Jesus expanded the concept of cleanliness from external, ritual purity to internal, moral purity of the heart.
- The Greek word 'katharos' is the root of the English word 'catharsis', which refers to an emotional cleansing or purification.
