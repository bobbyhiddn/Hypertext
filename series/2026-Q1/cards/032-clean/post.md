## Word
**CLEAN** — Free from dirt, pollution, or sin; ritually pure.

## Old Testament
> Psalm 51:10 — "Create in me a clean heart, O God, and renew a right spirit within me."

## New Testament
> John 15:3 — "Already you are clean because of the word that I have spoken to you."

## Trivia
- In the Old Testament, being 'clean' (tahor) primarily referred to ritual readiness to safely enter God's presence.
- Jesus expanded the concept of cleanliness from external, ritualistic washing to internal purity of the heart.
- The Greek word 'katharos' is the root for the English word 'catharsis', which means a cleansing or purging.
