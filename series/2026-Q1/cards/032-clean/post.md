## Word
**CLEAN** — Pure, unblemished, free from dirt or moral impurity.

## Old Testament
> Psalm 51:10 — "Create in me a clean heart, O God, and renew a right spirit within me."

## New Testament
> John 15:3 — "You are already clean because of the word which I have spoken to you."

## Trivia
- In the Old Testament, 'clean' (tahor) often referred to the ritual purity required to enter God's presence or participate in worship.
- Jesus radically shifted the focus of cleanliness from external, dietary rituals to internal, moral purity of the heart.
- The Greek word 'katharos' is the root of the English word 'catharsis', which means a purging or cleansing.
