## Word
**CLEAN** — Pure, unblemished, free from defilement

## Old Testament
> Psalm 51:10 — "Create in me a clean heart, O God, and renew a right spirit within me."

## New Testament
> John 15:3 — "You are already clean because of the word I have spoken to you."

## Trivia
- In Levitical law, being 'clean' (tahor) was required to enter God's presence for worship.
- Jesus expanded the concept from external ritual purity to internal purity of the heart.
- The Greek word 'katharos' is the root of the English word 'catharsis,' meaning a cleansing or purging.
