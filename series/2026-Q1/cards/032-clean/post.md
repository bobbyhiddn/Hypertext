## Word
**CLEAN** — Free from impurities; ritually or morally pure

## Old Testament
> Psalm 51:10 — "Create in me a clean heart, O God, and renew a right spirit within me."

## New Testament
> John 15:3 — "Already you are clean because of the word that I have spoken to you."

## Trivia
- In the Old Testament, being 'clean' (tahor) was often a state of ritual readiness for worship rather than a measure of moral perfection.
- Jesus redefined cleanness for His followers, teaching that moral purity flowing from the heart supersedes external ritual washing.
- The Greek word 'katharos' is the root for the English word 'catharsis,' which refers to a deep emotional cleansing or purging.
