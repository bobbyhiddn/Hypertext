## Word
**CLEAN** — Pure, free from defilement or impurity.

## Old Testament
> Psalm 51:10 — "Create in me a clean heart, O God; and renew a right spirit within me."

## New Testament
> John 15:3 — "You are already clean because of the word I have spoken to you."

## Trivia
- The Hebrew word 'tahor' describes both physical cleanliness and the ritual purity required to enter God's presence.
- The Greek word 'katharos' is the root of the English word 'catharsis', meaning a purification or cleansing.
- In John 15, Jesus tells His disciples they are 'already clean' because of the word He has spoken to them.
