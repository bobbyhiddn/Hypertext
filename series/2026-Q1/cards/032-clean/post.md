## Word
**CLEAN** — Pure, unblemished, free from dirt or guilt

## Old Testament
> Psalm 51:10 — "Create in me a clean heart, O God, and renew a steadfast spirit within me."

## New Testament
> John 15:3 — "You are already clean because of the word which I have spoken to you."

## Trivia
- The Hebrew 'tahor' was used for physical cleanliness, ritual purity, and unalloyed metals like 'pure gold'.
- In Greek, 'katharos' means unsoiled or free from admixture, giving us the English word 'catharsis'.
- Jesus expanded the concept of cleanliness from outward ritual traditions to inward moral purity of the heart.
