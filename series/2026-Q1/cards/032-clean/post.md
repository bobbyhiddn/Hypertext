## Word
**CLEAN** — pure, without blemish

## Old Testament
> Psalm 51:10 — "Create in me a clean heart, O God; and renew a right spirit within me."

## New Testament
> John 15:3 — "Already you are clean because of the word that I have spoken to you."

## Trivia
- In Hebrew, 'tahor' refers not just to physical cleanliness, but to a state of ritual and moral purity.
- The Greek 'katharos' is the root of the English word 'catharsis', meaning a purification or cleansing.
- Under the Levitical law, being 'clean' was required to approach the presence of God in the Tabernacle.
