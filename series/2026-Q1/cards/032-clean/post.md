## Word
**CLEAN** — Pure, unblemished, free from defilement

## Old Testament
> Psalm 51:10 — "Create in me a clean heart, O God, and renew a right spirit within me."

## New Testament
> John 15:3 — "Already you are clean because of the word that I have spoken to you."

## Trivia
- In the Mosaic Law, 'clean' (tahor) designated animals acceptable for food and sacrifice.
- Ritual cleansing often involved washing with water or sprinkling with a hyssop branch.
- Jesus expanded the concept of cleanness from outward ritual purity to inward moral purity of the heart.
