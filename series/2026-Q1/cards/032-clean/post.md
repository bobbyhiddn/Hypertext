## Word
**CLEAN** — Free from dirt, stain, or impurity; ceremonially acceptable or morally undefiled.

## Old Testament
> Psalm 51:10 — "Create in me a clean heart, O God, and renew a right spirit within me."

## New Testament
> John 15:3 — "Already you are clean because of the word that I have spoken to you."

## Trivia
- In Levitical law, being 'clean' was a strict requirement to enter the tabernacle and approach God.
- The Greek word 'katharos' is the root of the English word 'catharsis', referring to a purifying emotional cleanse.
- Jesus consistently expanded the concept of cleanliness from external, physical rituals to internal purity of the heart.
