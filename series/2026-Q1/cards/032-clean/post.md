## Word
**CLEAN** — Pure, unblemished, free from dirt or moral impurity

## Old Testament
> Psalm 51:10 — "Create in me a clean heart, O God, and renew a right spirit within me."

## New Testament
> John 15:3 — "You are already clean because of the word I have spoken to you."

## Trivia
- In the Old Testament, being 'clean' (tahor) was a necessary state required to enter the presence of God in the Tabernacle.
- The Greek word 'katharos' is the root of the English word 'catharsis', meaning a purification or purging of emotions.
- Jesus redefined cleanness not as an outward ritual washing of the hands, but as an inward purity of the heart and mind.
