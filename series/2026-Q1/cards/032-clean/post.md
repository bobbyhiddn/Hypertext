## Word
**CLEAN** — Ritually pure, unmixed, free from impurity

## Old Testament
> Psalm 51:10 — "Create in me a clean heart, O God, and renew a right spirit within me."

## New Testament
> John 15:3 — "Already you are clean because of the word that I have spoken to you."

## Trivia
- The Hebrew 'tahor' describes ritual purity, indicating a state of readiness to enter God's presence.
- The Greek 'katharos' means unmixed or unalloyed, like pure gold entirely free of impurities.
- In John 15, Jesus uses 'katharos' to describe branches that have been pruned by the Vinedresser to bear more fruit.
