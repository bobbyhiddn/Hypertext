## Word
**CLEAN** — Pure, unblemished, free from impurity or sin.

## Old Testament
> Psalm 51:10 — "Create in me a clean heart, O God, and renew a right spirit within me."

## New Testament
> John 15:3 — "Already you are clean because of the word that I have spoken to you."

## Trivia
- In the Old Testament, 'tahor' primarily denoted ritual purity required for worship, rather than physical hygiene.
- Jesus taught that true cleanliness comes from within, prioritizing moral purity of the heart over external washing.
- The Greek word 'katharos' is the origin of the English word 'catharsis', meaning a purging or purification.
