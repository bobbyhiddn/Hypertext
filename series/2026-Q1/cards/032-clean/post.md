## Word
**CLEAN** — pure, unblemished, washed

## Old Testament
> Psalm 51:10 — "Create in me a clean heart, O God, and renew a right spirit within me."

## New Testament
> John 15:3 — "You are already clean because of the word I have spoken to you."

## Trivia
- In the Old Testament, 'clean' (tahor) often referred to ritual purity required to enter the tabernacle or temple.
- Jesus expanded the concept of cleanliness from outward ritual to inward purity of the heart.
- The Greek word 'katharos' is the root of the English word 'catharsis', meaning a purifying or cleansing of emotions.
