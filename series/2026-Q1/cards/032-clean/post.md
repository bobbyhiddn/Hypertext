## Word
**CLEAN** — pure, innocent, free from impurities

## Old Testament
> Psalm 51:10 — "Create in me a clean heart, O God, and renew a right spirit within me."

## New Testament
> John 15:3 — "Already you are clean because of the word that I have spoken to you."

## Trivia
- The Hebrew 'tahor' primarily denoted ritual purity, a necessary state for Israelites to approach God's tabernacle.
- Jesus expanded the Jewish concept of cleanliness from external, ceremonial washings to the internal purity of the heart.
- The Greek word 'katharos' is the root of the English word 'catharsis,' which means an emotional or spiritual purification.
