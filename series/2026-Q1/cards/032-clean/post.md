## Word
**CLEAN** — Pure, unblemished, free from defilement

## Old Testament
> Psalm 51:10 — "Create in me a clean heart, O God; and renew a right spirit within me."

## New Testament
> John 15:3 — "Now ye are clean through the word which I have spoken unto you."

## Trivia
- In the Old Testament, 'tahor' describes both ritual purity (like clean animals) and moral purity (a clean heart).
- The Greek 'katharos' literally means 'without admixture' or unmixed, like pure gold or a vine pruned of dead branches.
- Jesus declared His disciples 'clean' not through ritual washing, but through the words He had spoken to them.
