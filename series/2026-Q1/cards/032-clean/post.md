## Word
**CLEAN** — Free from dirt, pollution, or ritual impurity; morally pure.

## Old Testament
> Psalm 51:10 — "Create in me a clean heart, O God, and renew a right spirit within me."

## New Testament
> John 15:3 — "Already you are clean because of the word that I have spoken to you."

## Trivia
- Under the Levitical law, being ceremonially 'clean' (tahor) was required to enter the presence of God in the tabernacle.
- Jesus radically shifted the focus of cleanliness from external rituals to internal purity of the heart.
- The Greek word 'katharos' is the origin of the English word 'catharsis', meaning a profound emotional or spiritual cleansing.
