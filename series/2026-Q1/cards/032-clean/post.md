## Word
**CLEAN** — Pure, unblemished, free from defilement

## Old Testament
> Psalm 51:10 — "Create in me a clean heart, O God, and renew a right spirit within me."

## New Testament
> John 15:3 — "You are already clean because of the word which I have spoken to you."

## Trivia
- In Levitical law, 'clean' (tahor) designated animals acceptable for food and animals without blemish acceptable for sacrifice.
- Jesus expanded the concept of cleanliness from outward ritual purity to the moral and spiritual purity of the heart.
- The Greek word 'katharos' is the root of the English word 'catharsis', meaning an emotional or spiritual purification.
