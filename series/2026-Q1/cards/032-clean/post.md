## Word
**CLEAN** — Free from dirt, pollution, or ritual impurity; pure.

## Old Testament
> Psalm 51:10 — "Create in me a clean heart, O God, and renew a right spirit within me."

## New Testament
> John 15:3 — "Already you are clean because of the word that I have spoken to you."

## Trivia
- In the Old Testament, ritual cleanliness was required for Israelites to participate in worship at the tabernacle.
- Jesus shifted the focus of cleanliness from external, ceremonial washing to internal purity of the heart.
- The Greek word 'katharos' is the root of the English word 'catharsis,' which means a cleansing or purging.
