## Word
**CLEAN** — free from impurity; ritually pure

## Old Testament
> Psalm 51:10 — "Create in me a clean heart, O God, and renew a right spirit within me."

## New Testament
> John 15:3 — "Already you are clean because of the word that I have spoken to you."

## Trivia
- In the Old Testament, 'clean' (tahor) often referred to the ritual purity required to enter God's presence.
- Jesus redefined cleanness, teaching that moral purity of the heart supersedes outward ritual washing.
- The Greek word 'katharos' is the root of the English word 'catharsis', meaning a purifying release.
