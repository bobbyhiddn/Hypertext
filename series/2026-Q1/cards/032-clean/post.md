## Word
**CLEAN** — Pure, unblemished, free from impurity

## Old Testament
> Psalm 51:10 — "Create in me a clean heart, O God, and renew a right spirit within me."

## New Testament
> John 15:3 — "You are already clean because of the word I have spoken to you."

## Trivia
- The Greek word 'katharos' is the root of the English word 'catharsis,' referring to emotional or spiritual cleansing.
- In the Old Testament, the Hebrew 'tahor' describes both physical purity, like refined gold, and the ritual cleanness required to approach God.
- Jesus emphasized inner moral purity over external ritual washing, stating that believers are made clean through His word.
