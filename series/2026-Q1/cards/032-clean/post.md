## Word
**CLEAN** — Free from impurity, defilement, or blemish; ceremonially or morally pure.

## Old Testament
> Psalm 51:10 — "Create in me a clean heart, O God, and renew a right spirit within me."

## New Testament
> John 15:3 — "Already you are clean because of the word that I have spoken to you."

## Trivia
- In the Old Testament, being ceremonially 'clean' (tahor) was a strict requirement for entering the tabernacle or temple presence.
- The Greek word 'katharos' is the root for the English word 'catharsis', meaning a deep purification or cleansing.
- Jesus radically redefined cleanness for His followers, teaching that moral purity of the heart supersedes external ceremonial washing.
