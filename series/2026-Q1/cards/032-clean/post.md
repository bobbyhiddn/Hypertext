## Word
**CLEAN** — Free from impurity; pure, innocent, or ritually acceptable

## Old Testament
> Psalm 51:10 — "Create in me a clean heart, O God, and renew a right spirit within me."

## New Testament
> John 15:3 — "Already you are clean because of the word that I have spoken to you."

## Trivia
- In Levitical law, being 'clean' (tahor) referred to the ritual purity required to safely approach the tabernacle and God's presence.
- The Greek word 'katharos' is the root of the English word 'catharsis,' which means a profound emotional purification or cleansing.
- Jesus radically redefined cleanness for His followers, teaching that true purity originates from the heart rather than external washing rituals.
