## Word
**HIGH** — Lifted far above; lofty in place, in honor, or in pride

## Old Testament
> Genesis 11:4 — "a tower, whose top may reach unto heaven"

## New Testament
> Romans 12:16 — "mind not high things"

## Trivia
- Hebrew gavoah describes lofty towers and the proud heart alike in Scripture.
- Babel's builders wanted a top in the heavens; Isaiah answers that God's ways are higher still.
- Greek hypselos shares its root with hypsos, height, the word behind English hypsometer.
