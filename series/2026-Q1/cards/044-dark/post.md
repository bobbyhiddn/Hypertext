## Word
**DARK** — Without light; the unlit deep before the first day

## Old Testament
> Genesis 1:2 — "darkness was upon the face of the deep"

## New Testament
> 1 Peter 2:9 — "out of darkness into his marvellous light"

## Trivia
- Hebrew choshek is the first condition named after the deep in Genesis 1:2 - before any light.
- The ninth plague was a darkness that could be felt, three days thick over Egypt.
- Greek skotos gives us scotoma, the physician's word for a blind spot in the eye.
