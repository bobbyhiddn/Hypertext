## Word
**METHUSELAH** — The oldest man who ever lived

## Old Testament
> Genesis 5:27 — "all the days of Methuselah were nine hundred sixty and nine years"

## New Testament
> Luke 3:37 — "which was the son of Mathusala"

## Trivia
- Nine hundred sixty and nine years is the longest life in Scripture; Jared, his grandfather, is second at nine hundred sixty and two.
- Adding the years of Genesis 5, Methuselah dies in the very year the flood comes - the oldest man outlives none of the judgment.
- Enoch, who never died, was the father of the man who lived longest.
