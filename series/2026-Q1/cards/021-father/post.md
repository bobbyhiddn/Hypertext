## Word
**FATHER** — A male parent, ancestor, or originator; the first person of the Trinity.

## Old Testament
> Psalm 68:5 — "A father to the fatherless, a defender of widows, is God in his holy dwelling."

## New Testament
> Matthew 6:9 — "Our Father in heaven, hallowed be your name."

## Trivia
- The Hebrew word 'Av' is formed by the first two letters of the alphabet (Aleph-Bet).
- God is referred to as 'Father' only about 15 times in the Old Testament, but frequently in the New.
- Jesus used the Aramaic term 'Abba' (Mark 14:36), expressing intimate respect.
