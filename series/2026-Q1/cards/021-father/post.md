## Word
**FATHER** — A male parent, ancestor, or the first person of the Trinity; a source of origin or authority.

## Old Testament
> Psalm 68:5 — "Father of the fatherless and protector of widows is God in his holy habitation."

## New Testament
> Galatians 4:6 — "God has sent the Spirit of his Son into our hearts, crying, 'Abba! Father!'"

## Trivia
- Used of God, ancestors, and biological parents.
- "Abba" is an Aramaic term of intimacy used by Jesus.
- God is addressed as Father far more frequently in the NT.
