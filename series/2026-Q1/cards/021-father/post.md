## Word
**FATHER** — A male parent, ancestor, or originator; specifically God as the first person of the Trinity.

## Old Testament
> Psalm 68:5 — "A father to the fatherless, a defender of widows, is God in his holy dwelling."

## New Testament
> Matthew 6:9 — "This, then, is how you should pray: 'Our Father in heaven, hallowed be your name...'"

## Trivia
- "Abba" is an intimate Aramaic term, akin to "Daddy," used 3 times in the NT.
- God is referred to as "Father" only ~15 times in the OT, but over 165 times in the Gospels.
- The "bet av" (father's house) was the fundamental unit of ancient Israelite society.
