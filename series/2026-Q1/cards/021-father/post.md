## Word
**FATHER** — A male parent, ancestor, or originator; a title of respect and authority; the first person of the Trinity.

## Old Testament
> Psalm 103:13 — "Like as a father pitieth his children, so the LORD pitieth them that fear him."

## New Testament
> Matthew 6:9 — "After this manner therefore pray ye: Our Father which art in heaven, Hallowed be thy name."

## Trivia
- The Aramaic word 'Abba' represents an intimate, personal father.
- In genealogies, the term can refer to a grandfather or distant ancestor.
- Used of God over 250 times in the New Testament.
