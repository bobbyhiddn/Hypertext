## Word
**FATHER** — A male parent; a title of God indicating origin, authority, and care.

## Old Testament
> Psalm 103:13 — "As a father shows compassion to his children, so the Lord shows compassion to those who fear him."

## New Testament
> Matthew 6:9 — "Pray then like this: Our Father in heaven, hallowed be your name."

## Trivia
- The Hebrew word 'Av' is formed by the first two letters of the alphabet (Aleph-Bet).
- Jesus used the Aramaic term 'Abba', an intimate word resembling 'Daddy'.
- In the Old Testament, God is called 'Father' rarely; in the New, it is His primary title.
