## Word
**FATHER** — A male parent, ancestor, or originator; a title of authority, protection, and provision.

## Old Testament
> Psalm 68:5 — "A father to the fatherless, a defender of widows, is God in his holy dwelling."

## New Testament
> Matthew 6:9 — "This, then, is how you should pray: 'Our Father in heaven, hallowed be your name...'"

## Trivia
- The Hebrew word 'Av' is built from the first two letters of the alphabet, signifying primacy.
- Jesus used the Aramaic term 'Abba' (Daddy), introducing a radical intimacy with God.
- In biblical culture, a father was the religious instructor and legal authority of the home.
