## Word
**FATHER** — A male parent; a progenitor or ancestor; the first person of the Trinity; a title of respect for an elder or spiritual leader.

## Old Testament
> Psalm 103:13 — "As a father pities his children, So the LORD pities those who fear Him."

## New Testament
> Matthew 6:9 — "In this manner, therefore, pray: Our Father in heaven, Hallowed be Your name."

## Trivia
- "Abba" is an intimate Aramaic term used by Jesus, equivalent to "Daddy."
- In ancient Israel, the father was the spiritual head and teacher of the household.
- God is addressed as "Father" over 165 times in the Gospels alone.
