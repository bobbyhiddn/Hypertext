## Word
**FATHER** — A male parent; a progenitor or ancestor; the first person of the Trinity; a title of respect and authority.

## Old Testament
> Psalm 103:13 — "As a father has compassion on his children, so the Lord has compassion on those who fear him."

## New Testament
> Matthew 7:11 — "How much more will your Father in heaven give good gifts to those who ask him!"

## Trivia
- The Aramaic term 'Abba' expresses deep intimacy, similar to 'Daddy' or 'Papa.'
- God is called 'Father' over 165 times in the Gospels, but only rarely in the Old Testament.
- In ancient Hebrew culture, the father was the head of the 'bet ab' and responsible for spiritual instruction.
