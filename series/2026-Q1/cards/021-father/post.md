## Word
**FATHER** — A male parent; a source or originator; the first person of the Trinity; a title of respect and authority within a family or spiritual community.

## Old Testament
> Psalm 103:13 — "As a father has compassion on his children, so the Lord has compassion on those who fear him."

## New Testament
> Romans 8:15 — "For you did not receive a spirit of slavery to fall back into fear, but you have received a spirit of adoption. When we cry, 'Abba! Father!'"

## Trivia
- The Hebrew 'Av' is likely mimetic of infant babbling, similar to 'Papa' in English.
- In the ancient Near East, the 'bet av' (house of the father) was the primary social and legal unit.
- Jesus used the intimate Aramaic term 'Abba' in prayer, revolutionizing the believer's access to God.
