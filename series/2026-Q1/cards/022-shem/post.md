## Word
**SHEM** — Name, reputation, or fame; the eldest son of Noah.

## Old Testament
> Genesis 9:26 — "Blessed be the LORD, the God of Shem!"

## New Testament
> Luke 3:36 — "...the son of Shem, the son of Noah..."

## Trivia
- Literally means 'name' in Hebrew; implies authority and reputation.
- Ancestor of the Semitic peoples and the covenant line of Abraham.
- Often identified in Jewish tradition as Melchizedek, King of Salem.
