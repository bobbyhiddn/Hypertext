## Word
**MULTIPLY** — To increase in number; to make many.

## Old Testament
> Genesis 22:17 — "In multiplying I will multiply thy seed as the stars of the heaven."

## New Testament
> Acts 12:24 — "But the word of God grew and multiplied."

## Trivia
- The first command given to humanity in Genesis 1:28.
- God promised Abraham his descendants would multiply like stars.
- In Acts, the 'word of God' itself is described as multiplying.
