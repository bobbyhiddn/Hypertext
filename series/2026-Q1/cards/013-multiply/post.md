## Word
**MULTIPLY** — To increase greatly in number; to reproduce or abound.

## Old Testament
> Genesis 1:22 — "Be fruitful, and multiply, and fill the waters in the seas."

## New Testament
> Acts 6:7 — "The word of God increased; and the number of the disciples multiplied."

## Trivia
- The first command God gave to living creatures.
- Used to describe the Israelites' explosive growth in Egypt.
- Jesus multiplied loaves and fish to feed thousands.
