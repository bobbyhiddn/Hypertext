## Word
**CANAAN** — The Promised Land; a region of abundance and inheritance.

## Old Testament
> Exodus 3:8 — "A good and large land, to a land flowing with milk and honey."

## New Testament
> Acts 13:19 — "He destroyed seven nations in the land of Canaan, and divided their land to them by lot."

## Trivia
- Etymology likely 'Lowland' or 'Land of Purple' (referring to dye).
- Grandson of Noah; cursed to be a 'servant of servants'.
- Symbolizes the believer's spiritual rest and inheritance.
