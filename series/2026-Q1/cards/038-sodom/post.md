## Word
**SODOM** — The city of the plain judged with fire, from which a remnant fled

## Old Testament
> Genesis 19:24 — "rained upon Sodom brimstone and fire"

## New Testament
> Luke 17:29 — "Lot went out of Sodom it rained fire"

## Trivia
- Ezekiel 16:49 names Sodom's sins as pride, fullness of bread, and neglect of the poor.
- Lot's wife looked back at the burning plain and became a pillar of salt - Luke says simply, remember her.
- The cities of the plain lay near the Dead Sea, whose salt and bitumen shape the story's landscape.
