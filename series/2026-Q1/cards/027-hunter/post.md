## Word
**HUNTER** — One who pursues game for food, sport, or capture; metaphorically, an agent of judgment or persecution.

## Old Testament
> Genesis 10:9 — "He was a mighty hunter before the LORD: wherefore it is said, Even as Nimrod the mighty hunter before the LORD."

## New Testament
> Romans 11:9 — "And David says: 'Let their table become a snare and a trap, a stumbling block and a retribution for them.'"

## Trivia
- Nimrod is the first individual described as a 'mighty hunter' in the Bible.
- Jeremiah 16:16 contrasts 'fishers' who gather with 'hunters' sent for judgment.
- Esau, a skillful hunter, famously traded his birthright for stew after a hunt.
