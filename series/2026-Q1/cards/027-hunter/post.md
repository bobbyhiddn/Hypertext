## Word
**HUNTER** — One who tracks, pursues, and captures game or enemies; a seeker of prey.

## Old Testament
> Genesis 10:9 — "He was a mighty hunter before the Lord: wherefore it is said, Even as Nimrod the mighty hunter..."

## New Testament
> 2 Timothy 2:26 — "And that they may recover themselves out of the snare of the devil, who are taken captive by him..."

## Trivia
- Nimrod was the first man described as a 'mighty hunter' in Scripture.
- Esau's skill as a hunter made him the favorite son of his father Isaac.
- Jeremiah prophesied God sending 'hunters' to find scattered exiles in judgment.
