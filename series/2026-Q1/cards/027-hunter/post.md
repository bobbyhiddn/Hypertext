## Word
**HUNTER** — One who chases game or seeks to capture; a pursuer of targets.

## Old Testament
> Jeremiah 16:16 — "I will send for many hunters, and they shall hunt them from every mountain..."

## New Testament
> Luke 11:54 — "...laying wait for him, and seeking to catch something out of his mouth..."

## Trivia
- Nimrod is the first person described as a 'mighty hunter' in the Bible.
- In Jeremiah, hunters represent agents of judgment tracking down idolaters.
- The Greek word for hunter (kynegos) literally means 'dog-leader'.
