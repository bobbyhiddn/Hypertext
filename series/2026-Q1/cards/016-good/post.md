## Word
**GOOD** — Morally excellent; virtuous; satisfactory; fulfilling a designed purpose.

## Old Testament
> Genesis 1:31 — "God saw all that he had made, and it was very good."

## New Testament
> Luke 18:19 — "No one is good—except God alone."

## Trivia
- Hebrew 'tov' implies fulfilling a designed purpose, not just moral virtue.
- God pronounces creation 'good' seven times in Genesis 1.
- Greek 'agathos' refers to inner character, while 'kalos' implies outward beauty.
